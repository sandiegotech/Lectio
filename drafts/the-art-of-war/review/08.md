# The Art of War · Chapter VIII
Grade: B
Faithful, and the damaged chapter is translated as it stands; one pronoun against the style sheet.

## Issues
- `p-007` · voice · low
  > count on your being ready for him ... count on your having made yourself impossible to attack
  恃吾有以待之 … 恃吾有所不可攻也: 吾 is our side against the enemy, which the style sheet gives as "we / our".
  Fix: count on our being ready for him. Do not count on his not attacking; count on our having made ourselves impossible to attack.

If he loves his people, he can be harried.
