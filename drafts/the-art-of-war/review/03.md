# The Art of War · Chapter III
Grade: B
Faithful and well built; three small slips where a word the chapter turns on (法, 必, 全) is dropped or a pronoun shifts.

## Issues
- `p-004` · fidelity · low
  > Attack cities only when there is no other choice.
  攻城之法，為不得已 is "the rule for attacking cities: [do it] when there is no help for it". 法 is one of the book's key terms and the chapter opens and closes its sections on it.
  Fix: The rule for attacking cities is to do it only when there is no other choice.

- `p-006` · fidelity · low
  > He contends for the world by keeping things whole. So his weapons are not blunted and his gain can be complete.
  必以全爭於天下 has 必, "always, without fail", and 利可全 repeats 全, which the style sheet gives as "whole"; "complete" breaks the chain the chapter is built on.
  Fix: He always contends for the world by keeping things whole. So his weapons are not blunted and his gain can be kept whole.

- `p-013` · voice · low
  > This is called throwing your own army into disorder and giving the victory away.
  The subject of the whole passage is the ruler ("he tells it to advance"); "your" switches person in the last sentence.
  Fix: This is called throwing his own army into disorder and giving the victory away.
