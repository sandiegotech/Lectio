## Issues
- `p-010` · fidelity · low
  > It is the nature of war that speed rules.
  故兵之情主速 opens with 故, which the style sheet keeps as "So"; it is the only 故 dropped in the chapter.
  Fix: So it is the nature of war that speed rules.

- `p-006` · voice · low
  > That is why on scattering ground you do not fight. On light ground, do not stop.
  The first of nine parallel clauses is a statement, the other eight imperatives.
  Fix: That is why on scattering ground, do not fight.

- `p-038` · fidelity · low
  > and from a thousand li away you kill his general
  千里殺將 may be "march a thousand li and kill his general"; "from ... away" could suggest killing at a distance.
  Fix: and you go a thousand li and kill his general.
