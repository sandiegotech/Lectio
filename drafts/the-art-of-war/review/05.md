# The Art of War · Chapter V
Grade: B
Momentum and timing are held throughout and the parallels kept; one sentence explains instead of translating.

## Issues
- `p-009` · fidelity · medium
  > the fighting looks disordered ... his form turns like a wheel
  鬥亂而不可亂 says the fighting *is* in disorder, not that it looks so; 形圓而不可敗 is "his form is round", round so that it has no edge to break. "Looks" and "turns like a wheel" are a commentator's reading put into the line.
  Fix: In the swirl and tangle the fighting is in disorder, yet he cannot be thrown into disorder. In the churn and blur his form is round, yet he cannot be beaten.
