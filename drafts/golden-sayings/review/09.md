# The Golden Sayings of Epictetus · Fragments
Grade: A
The twelve fragments with Greek (I–III, VII, VIII, XI, XII, XIV–XVI, XVIII, XXII) check line by line against Schenkl's Gnomologium and doubtful fragments; Crossley's closing note is kept out. The other twelve rest on Crossley and Long only (listed for the editor in APPLIED.md); as English they need nothing.
