# The Golden Sayings of Epictetus · Sayings LXXXIV–CIX
Grade: B
Very close to Schenkl, with the manuscript readings argued in the notes; LXXXIV and CVI are among the best paragraphs in the book. Two sentences mislead or run out of breath, two words are off.

## Issues
- `CIV` · fidelity · medium
  > If not, see that you are behaving like children
  Reads as an instruction to behave like children. Greek ὅρα ὅτι ὡς τὰ παιδία ἀναστραφήσῃ, "mind: you will be behaving like children".
  Fix: If not, know that you will be behaving like children
- `XCVIII` · read-aloud · medium
  > so we too should be able to talk with ourselves, … ; to turn our minds to the divine governance …; to look at how …; and if any of this needs working out, to work it out as its own reason requires.
  One sentence of some 120 words; the infinitives lose their governing "should" long before the end. The Greek has a stop after διαγωγῆς μὴ ἀπορεῖν.
  Fix: break there: "…not to be at a loss for something to do. We should turn our minds to the divine governance … everything else; look at how we used to stand … and if any of this needs working out, work it out as its own reason requires."
- `XCI` · register · low
  > the well-behaved man
  Nursery register for τὸν κόσμιον, the orderly, decent man.
  Fix: the decent man
- `XCIX` · read-aloud · low
  > A man who goes in often with certain people
  "Goes in with" is not idiomatic. Greek συγκαθιέντα τισὶν ἐπιπλέον, "who mixes often with".
  Fix: A man who mixes often with certain people

## Best lines
- LXXXIV: "Now you want me to leave the festival. I go."
- CVI: "No; but bring whatever you like and I will make it good."
