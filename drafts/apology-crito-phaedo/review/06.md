# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Phaedo · Opposites and Recollection
Grade: B
The two arguments are followed step by step and the formulas of assent hold; the key term "release" slips, and one question at 71e is construed wrongly.

Checked clause by clause against Burnet (69e–78b), not only at the cruxes; Jowett as control.

## Issues
- `p-043` · 71e · fidelity · medium
  > Shall we not supply the opposite coming to be to balance it, and will nature be lame on this side?
  οὐκ ἀνταποδώσομεν τὴν ἐναντίαν γένεσιν, ἀλλὰ ταύτῃ χωλὴ ἔσται ἡ φύσις; "Are we not to give back the opposite, but leave nature lame?": the ἀλλά sets the two against each other; "and" makes them one proposal and the reader stalls.
  Fix: Shall we not supply the opposite coming to be to balance it, but leave nature lame on this side?

- `p-001`, `p-144` · 70a, 77b · voice · medium
  > when it is released from the body … the moment it is released from the body … released from those evils … and is released from it
  ἀπαλλαγῇ, ἀπαλλαττομένη, ἀπηλλαγμένη, ἀπαλλάττηται: STYLE §11 gives ἀπαλλάττεσθαι "part from", "be rid of" and keeps "release" for λύσις, which 67d has just defined as death. Section 5 holds the line (`p-067` "parting", `p-102` "rid of"); here the terms merge.
  Fix: when it parts from the body / the moment it parts from the body / rid of those evils / and parts from it

- `p-013`, `p-041` · 71a, 71e · register · low
  > isn't it from better … isn't it?
  Contractions inside the argument (STYLE §2).
  Fix: is it not from better / is it not?

- `p-067` · 73d · fidelity · low
  > the form of the boy whose lyre it was
  εἶδος here is the boy's look, his image in the mind.
  Fix: none proposed; see second look.
