## Dropped on second look

- 01 `p-007` · Ap. 21c · "show the god" for ἀποφανῶν τῷ χρησμῷ. The "you said" that follows is addressed to the god through his oracle, so "the god" is the right addressee in English.
- 04 `p-002` · Cr. 43a · "Very early." for πάνυ μὲν οὖν. Here it answers "Isn't it still early?"; "Very much so" would read as a stock phrase, and the sense is the same.
- 05 `p-133` · Phd. 69b · "a sort of painted shadow" for σκιαγραφία. It carries the illusion, and "scene-painting" would need explaining.
- 06 `p-067` · Phd. 73d · "the form of the boy" for τὸ εἶδος τοῦ παιδός. The fixed term may be Plato's own anticipation of the forms in the next pages; left as STYLE §6 has it.
