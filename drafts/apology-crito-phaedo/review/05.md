# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Phaedo · The Philosopher's Death
Grade: B
The frame and the prison scene are vivid and exact; the defense reads clearly. One misconstrued sentence at the climax (69b–c) and small slips.

Checked clause by clause against Burnet (57a–69e), not only at the cruxes; Jowett as control.

## Issues
- `p-133` · 69b–c · fidelity · high
  > and that the truth is really a kind of purification from all such things, and that self-command and justice and courage, and wisdom itself, are a kind of purifying rite.
  τὸ δ' ἀληθὲς τῷ ὄντι ᾖ κάθαρσίς τις τῶν τοιούτων πάντων καὶ ἡ σωφροσύνη καὶ ἡ δικαιοσύνη καὶ ἀνδρεία, καὶ αὐτὴ ἡ φρόνησις μὴ καθαρμός τις ᾖ: τὸ δ' ἀληθές is "in truth" (Burnet takes it adverbially); the three virtues are the purification (κάθαρσις) from such things, and wisdom alone is the purifying rite (καθαρμός). The draft makes "the truth" the purification and lumps all four under the rite.
  Fix: and that in truth self-command and justice and courage are a kind of purification from all such things, and wisdom itself is a kind of purifying rite.

- `p-079` · 65a · voice · low
  > is straining pretty close to being dead
  ἐγγύς τι τείνειν τοῦ τεθνάναι, "comes near to being dead": "straining" pictures effort the Greek does not have and trips the ear.
  Fix: comes pretty close to being dead

- `p-085`, `p-103` · 65c, 67b · register · low
  > Then isn't it in reasoning … Or don't you think so?
  STYLE §2: every argument in the Phaedo is uncontracted; elsewhere in this section the same question is "Or do you not think so?" (`p-046`, `p-081`).
  Fix: Then is it not in reasoning / Or do you not think so?

- `p-133` · 69b · voice · low
  > a sort of painted shadow
  σκιαγραφία, scene-painting that looks solid only from afar.
  Fix: none proposed; see second look.
