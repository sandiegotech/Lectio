# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Phaedo · The True Earth and the Death (107c–118a)
Grade: B
The story is exact, and the death scene keeps Plato's order and restraint (STYLE §9). To fix: a phrase Burnet deletes at 114e–115a that steals the next line, a divergence note that contradicts the text, and some small slips in the death scene.

## Issues
- `p-026` · fidelity · medium · *Phaedo* 115a
  > so awaits the journey to Hades, ready to make it whenever fate calls.
  Burnet brackets [ὡς πορευσόμενος ὅταν ἡ εἱμαρμένη καλῇ] as an anticipation of the next sentence (the Perseus file marks it `<del>`). If it stays, Socrates' tragic "fate is calling" in p-027 arrives already spent.
  Fix: so awaits the journey to Hades.

- `p-001` · fidelity (note conflict) · medium · 107c
  > and now the danger would indeed seem terrible
  ὁ κίνδυνος. The 114d divergence note says the edition "keeps 'risk' both times", but the text says "danger" here.
  Fix: and now the risk would indeed seem terrible

- `p-001` · fidelity (fixed term) · low · 107c
  > if death were a release from everything … to be released at once from the body
  ἀπαλλαγή / ἀπηλλάχθαι. STYLE §11 keeps "release" for λύσις and gives ἀπαλλάττεσθαι as "be rid of".
  Fix: if death were a riddance of everything … to be rid at once of the body and of their own wickedness along with the soul

- `p-026` · read-aloud · low · 114e
  > as foreign to him, and judging that they do more harm than good, but has been in earnest
  The "and" ties "judging" to nothing.
  Fix: as foreign to him, judging that they do more harm than good, but has been in earnest

- `p-019` · fidelity · low · 112c
  > it flows through the earth into the streams over there
  Burnet brackets [διὰ]: τοῖς κατ' ἐκεῖνα τὰ ῥεύματα τῆς γῆς.
  Fix: it flows into the streams in that part of the earth

- `p-031` · fidelity · low · 115e
  > to speak wrongly is not only a fault in itself
  πλημμελές means "out of tune". Socrates uses the same word for the women's weeping at 117d (πλημμελοῖεν, "strike such a false note", p-043), and the echo is lost.
  Fix: is not only a false note in itself

- `p-032` · fidelity · low · 116b
  > and after that not much was said.
  οὐ πολλὰ … διελέχθη: Socrates is the subject, and it is he who says little. The death scene should keep him as the subject.
  Fix: and after that he did not say much.

- `p-035` · fidelity · low · 116e
  > very late, long after the order was given them
  The Greek says only ὀψὲ … ἐπειδὰν παραγγελθῇ. "Long after" is added.
  Fix: very late, after the order was given them

- `p-042` · fidelity (fixed term) · low · 117d
  > broke out in a roar of weeping and grief
  ἀναβρυχησάμενος κλάων καὶ ἀγανακτῶν. STYLE §11 gives ἀγανακτεῖν in the death scene as "be distressed". "A roar of weeping" turns three verbs into one picture.
  Fix: broke out roaring, weeping and distressed

- `p-047` · fidelity · low · 118a
  > To this question he gave no answer.
  οὐδὲν ἔτι ἀπεκρίνατο. Without ἔτι, the fact that this was the end of his answering is lost.
  Fix: To this question he gave no further answer.

- `p-025` · fidelity (fixed term) · low · 114b
  > who are freed and released from these regions in the earth as from prisons
  ἀπαλλαττόμενοι: by STYLE §11 this would be "rid of".

- `p-044` · fidelity · low · 117e
  > took hold of him … And he touched him again himself
  ἐφαπτόμενος (present tense: he kept feeling him) and ἥπτετο. "Again" is not in the Greek.

## Best lines
- "looking up at the man from under his brows like a bull, as he used to do" (117b).
- "That, Echecrates, was the end of our friend, a man who was, as we would say, the best of all those of his time whom we knew, and besides the wisest and the most just." (118a)

## On STYLE
- §11 (ἀπαλλάττεσθαι, "part from" or "be rid of") reads badly at 114b, where "freed and released … as from prisons" is the natural English. Allow "released" when the image is a prison.
- §1 names Burnet as the authority, but the flattened cache drops his brackets. `burnet-phaedo-grc.txt` should mark `<del>` spans so that drafters can see them.
