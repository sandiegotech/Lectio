# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Apology · The Defense
Grade: B
A faithful, steady defense in one voice; a handful of words drift from the fixed terms or add what the Greek does not say.

Checked clause by clause against Burnet (17a–35d), Jowett as control. Burnet's bracketed φωνή at 31d is correctly left out of the first clause.

## Issues
- `p-002` · 18c · fidelity · low
  > and they won their case simply by default, since no one was there to answer
  ἀτεχνῶς ἐρήμην κατηγοροῦντες ἀπολογουμένου οὐδενός: they were prosecuting an undefended case; "won" adds an outcome Socrates does not state.
  Fix: and they were accusing me simply by default, with no one there to make a defense

- `p-002` · 18c · register · low
  > when some of you were children and some were boys
  παῖδες … καὶ μειράκια: "children" and "boys" are the same thing in English, so the second stage is lost; 34d renders μειράκιον "a youth".
  Fix: when some of you were children and some were youths

- `p-005` · 20b · fidelity · medium
  > who would make them excel in the virtue proper to them
  καλώ τε κἀγαθὼ ποιήσειν τὴν προσήκουσαν ἀρετήν: the fixed term "fine and good" (STYLE §6) is dropped, and with it the irony of applying the gentleman's phrase to colts and calves.
  Fix: who would make them fine and good in the virtue proper to them

- `p-005` · 20b · voice · low
  > There certainly is, he said.
  πάνυ γε is a fixed formula, "Certainly" (STYLE §7).
  Fix: Certainly, he said.

- `p-011` · 24a · fidelity · low
  > that this very frankness makes me hated
  αὐτοῖς τούτοις ἀπεχθάνομαι, "I am hated for these very things": "frankness" names the referent for the reader, a gloss in the sentence.
  Fix: that it is for these very things that I am hated

- `p-050` · 27b · voice · low
  > and not keep raising one protest after another
  μὴ ἄλλα καὶ ἄλλα θορυβείτω: θορυβεῖν is "make an uproar" everywhere else in the speech (STYLE §6); the echo of Socrates' own plea to the court is lost.
  Fix: and not make one uproar after another

- `p-055` · 28a · register · medium
  > to show that I am not guilty under Meletus' charge
  ὡς ἐγὼ οὐκ ἀδικῶ: the charge's own verb, "do wrong" (STYLE §6); "not guilty" is the television courtroom the style sheet keeps out.
  Fix: to show that I do no wrong under Meletus' charge

- `p-059` · 30d · register · low
  > sin against the god's gift to you
  ἐξαμάρτητε, "go wrong, offend": "sin" brings Christian vocabulary in, as Jowett does.
  Fix: offend against the god's gift to you

- `p-061` · 32a · fidelity · low
  > even if by not giving way I should die on the spot
  μὴ ὑπείκων δὲ ἀλλὰ κἂν ἀπολοίμην: "on the spot" is added.
  Fix: even if I should die for not giving way

- `p-007` · 21c · fidelity · low
  > I would refute the oracle and show the god
  ἀποφανῶν τῷ χρησμῷ, "show the oracle".
  Fix: none proposed; see second look.

## Best lines
- "I must simply fight with shadows in making my defense, and cross-examine with no one to answer."
- "I will obey the god rather than you."

## Style sheet
STYLE §2 allows contractions in the Meletus exchange (24c–28a) "in the short questions and answers". The draft also contracts inside Socrates' long turns there (`p-016`, `p-032`, `p-039`, `p-040`, `p-046`, `p-050`, `p-052`), sometimes beside uncontracted forms. I think the draft is right and the sheet too narrow: the whole cross-examination is live talk. The editor should either widen the rule to "the whole exchange with Meletus" or have these turns uncontracted; no change made.
