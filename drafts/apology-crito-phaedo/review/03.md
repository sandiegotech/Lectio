# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Apology · The Last Words
Grade: B
The close is as plain as the style sheet asks, and the address "judges" is kept for the right men; one added "voice" and two small term slips.

Checked clause by clause against Burnet (38c–42a), Jowett as control.

## Issues
- `p-003` · 40a · fidelity · medium
  > The prophetic voice I am used to, the voice of the divine sign,
  ἡ εἰωθυῖά μοι μαντικὴ ἡ τοῦ δαιμονίου: μαντική is the prophetic power, not a voice; "voice" (φωνή) is reserved by STYLE §6 for 31d, and is printed twice here though the Greek has it neither time.
  Fix: The prophetic power I am used to, that of the divine sign,

- `p-003` · 40a · voice · low
  > if I was about to do something wrong
  εἴ τι μέλλοιμι μὴ ὀρθῶς πράξειν, "not act rightly": "do wrong" is the fixed rendering of ἀδικεῖν, the charge's verb, and should not be spent on a different phrase.
  Fix: if I was about to do something that was not right

- `p-005` · 41d · voice · low
  > be released from my troubles
  ἀπηλλάχθαι πραγμάτων: STYLE §11 keeps "release" for λύσις and gives ἀπαλλάττεσθαι "be rid of".
  Fix: be rid of my troubles

## Best lines
- "It is much harder to escape wickedness, for it runs faster than death."
- "But now it is time to go, I to die and you to live. Which of us goes to the better thing is clear to no one but the god."

## For the editor
At 40a some editors bracket ἡ τοῦ δαιμονίου, and I believe Burnet's OCT does; the cached Greek shows no bracket. If the printed Burnet brackets it, the fix becomes "The prophetic power I am used to was always very frequent". Not applied: I could not check the printed page.
