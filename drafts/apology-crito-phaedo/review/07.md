# The Trial and Death of Socrates · Phaedo · The Soul's Kinship (78b–84b)
Grade: B
Close to the Greek, clause by clause, and in the work's voice. What needs fixing: three phrases Burnet brackets are translated anyway, one "always" is dropped, and a few fixed terms and formulas drift.

## Issues
- `p-010` · fidelity · low · *Phaedo* 78e
  > or the many equal things or beautiful things
  Burnet brackets ἢ καλῶν (the Perseus file marks it `<del>`; the flattened cache prints it). With it, "beautiful things" comes twice in one sentence.
  Fix: or the many equal things, or all the things

- `p-008` · fidelity · low · 78d
  > Or does each of them that is, being of one form, itself by itself, stay in the same condition
  ἀεὶ αὐτῶν ἕκαστον: "always" is missing, and "always" is the word the whole argument turns on.
  Fix: itself by itself, always stay in the same condition

- `p-054` · fidelity (fixed term) · low · 81c
  > will get away itself by itself, unmixed?
  ἀπαλλάξεσθαι. STYLE §11 fixes ἀπαλλάττεσθαι as "part from". The paragraph itself and p-050 say "parts from the body".
  Fix: will part from it itself by itself, unmixed?

- `p-055` · voice (formula) · low · 81c
  > Not in the least, he said.
  οὐδ' ὁπωστιοῦν. Book 08 renders the same answer "Not in any way whatever" twice (p-071, p-095).
  Fix: Not in any way whatever, he said.

- `p-068` · fidelity (fixed term) · low · 82b
  > without philosophy and understanding
  νοῦ. STYLE §11 gives νοῦς as "intelligence" or "sense" when it is a person's.
  Fix: without philosophy and intelligence

- `p-077` · fidelity · low · 83b
  > keeps away from pleasures and desires and pains and fears … intense pleasure or fear or pain or desire
  Burnet brackets [καὶ φόβων] and [ἢ λυπηθῇ]. The draft translates both.
  Fix: keeps away from pleasures and desires and pains as far as it can … intense pleasure or fear or desire

- `p-077` · read-aloud · low · 83c
  > and he does not reckon it in.
  "Reckon it in" is not an English idiom. οὐ λογίζεται answers λογιζομένη earlier in the sentence, so keep the reckoning.
  Fix: and he does not take it into his reckoning.

- `p-079` · fidelity · low · 83c
  > the thing which most causes it to feel this
  περὶ ὃ ἂν μάλιστα τοῦτο πάσχῃ is the thing the soul feels this *about*, not its cause. Plato has no causal claim here.
  Fix: the thing it feels this about most strongly

- `p-087` · fidelity · low · 84b
  > After a nurture like that, and having pursued these things, Simmias and Cebes
  Burnet brackets [ταῦτα δ' ἐπιτηδεύσασα].
  Fix: After a nurture like that, Simmias and Cebes

- `p-087` · voice · low · 84b
  > blown away by the winds and gone flying off
  διαφυσηθεῖσα is the verb rendered "blown apart" at 80d (p-050), and the threat is the same one. "Gone flying off" is out of order and trips.
  Fix: blown apart by the winds, flying off and gone

- `p-001` · voice (formula) · low · 78b
  > Good, he said.
  καλῶς λέγεις is "Well said" in book 08 (p-005) and book 09.
  Fix: Well said, he said.

## Best lines
- "Because each pleasure and pain, as if it had a nail, nails the soul to the body and pins it there and makes it bodily" (83d).
- "like some Penelope handling her web the opposite way" (84a).

## On STYLE
- §6 fixes εἶδος as "form". At 79a–e (δύο εἴδη τῶν ὄντων, ποτέρῳ τῷ εἴδει) the word means "kind", and the drafter rightly wrote "kinds". STYLE should allow "kind" where Plato is sorting things and not naming a Form.
- §7 should add καλῶς λέγεις and οὐδ' ὁπωστιοῦν to the assent formulas, because the draft varies them.
