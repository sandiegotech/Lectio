# Genesis · Chapter 41 · Pharaoh's Dreams
Grade: B
Long and well paced; Pharaoh's retelling keeps its own small additions, and the cruxes (41:40, "Abrek!", 41:56) are left as the Hebrew has them. One word is added to the cupbearer's confession.

Coverage: 41.1-8, 41.9-13, 41.14-24, 41.25-36, 41.37-45, 41.46-52, 41.53-57 each hold their own verses; nothing missing.

## Issues
- `41.9-13` · fidelity · low
  > “Today I must bring up my offenses.
  "must" is not in the Hebrew (*et-chata'ai ani mazkir hayom*, "my offenses I am calling to mind today"); it adds a reluctance the text does not state.
  Fix: “Today I bring up my offenses.
- `41.37-45` · fidelity · medium
  > the chariot of his second-in-command
  *mirkevet hammishneh asher lo* may be "his second chariot" (KJV), the chariot belonging to Pharaoh.
  Fix: "his second chariot".

## Best lines
- `41.14-24` "Not I. God will give an answer for Pharaoh's well-being."
- `41.46-52` "because God has made me fruitful in the land of my misery."
