# Genesis · Chapter 36 · The Line of Esau
Grade: A
A clean genealogy: names in modern standard spellings, "chief" for *alluf*, the received readings kept (yemim, Dishan at 36:26, Hadar, "Anah, daughter of Zibeon").

Coverage: 36.1-8, 36.9-14, 36.15-19, 36.20-30, 36.31-39, 36.40-43 each hold their own verses; 36:16's "chief Korah" (in the Hebrew) is present.

## Issues
None. (36:26 "sons of Dishan" twice is the received text and a crux for the editor; see APPLIED.md, crux 7.)

## Best lines
- `36.40-43` "These are the chiefs of Edom, by their settlements in the land they held. This is Esau, the father of Edom."
