# Odyssey · Book X · Aeolus, the Laestrygonians, and Circe
Grade: A
Full, exact and moving; Circe, the moly and the homecoming-calves simile are rendered with great care. Two small polish items.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · low
  > own sister to Aeetes, the wizard whose mind runs to ruin.
  "Wizard" is added; the Greek gives only ὀλοόφρονος, "of deadly mind".
  Fix: own sister to Aeetes, whose mind runs to ruin.

- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > I drew the sharp sword from my thigh
  Read literally, the sword comes out of his thigh. The Greek is παρὰ μηροῦ, from beside the thigh, and seg-0032 already says "from beside my thigh". The same phrase recurs in seg-0023, seg-0025 and seg-0039.
  Fix: I drew the sharp sword from beside my thigh (and the same in seg-0023, 0025, 0039).
