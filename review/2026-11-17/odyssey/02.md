# Odyssey · Book II · Telemachus Calls the Assembly
Grade: B
Strong, faithful and well voiced throughout the assembly; a handful of small fidelity and phrasing fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · medium
  > Be ashamed before your own neighbors, then; be ashamed before the people who live around us.
  The Greek has two different appeals: be indignant yourselves (νεμεσσήθητε καὶ αὐτοί), and feel shame before the neighboring peoples. The first is dropped and the second said twice (Butler: "Have respect, therefore, to your own consciences and to public opinion").
  Fix: Be angry at this yourselves, then, and be ashamed before the people who live around us.

- `seg-0018` · fidelity · low
  > and what a man he was at finishing what he set out to do.
  The Greek is word and deed (τελέσαι ἔργον τε ἔπος τε); "word" is dropped (Butler: "never broke his word nor left his work half done").
  Fix: and what a man he was for making good his word and finishing his work.

- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > and they won't go the honest way, to her father Ikarios
  "The honest way" adds a moral the Greek does not state; the verb (ἀπερρίγασι) says they shrink from going to him.
  Fix: and they shrink from going to her father Ikarios, to let him weigh the matter,

- `seg-0020` · read-aloud · low
  > I'll loose every ruin I can on you
  "Loose ... ruin" does not land in English; the line loses its stress.
  Fix: I'll do all I can to bring ruin down on you

- `seg-0022` · read-aloud · low
  > and watch over it, night and day, was kept by the housekeeper Eurykleia, daughter of Ops, son of Peisenor — careful and wise in all things.
  Inverted passive; the subject arrives last and the sentence sags.
  Fix: and night and day the housekeeper kept watch over it: Eurykleia, daughter of Ops, son of Peisenor, careful and wise in all things.

- `seg-0027` · register · low
  > and Telemachus, in his strength, said to them
  A literal take on the formula ἱερὴ ἲς Τηλεμάχοιο; as English it suggests he is showing off his strength.
  Fix: and Telemachus, in his young strength, called to them

## Best lines
- It is a hard fight, against odds like these, to come between men and their dinner.
- the barley meal, the marrow of men's strength, in stout leather bags.
