# The Odyssey · Book XVIII · The Beggars' Fight
Grade: B
Fast, funny and complete: it keeps Echetus's full threat, which Butler cuts, and the "Un-Irused" pun. The Amphinomus speech is excellent. One gloss has been written into the sentence, and a few word choices need fixing.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · medium
  > but the young men all called him Irus, because he would run and carry a message for anyone who sent him — a man's twist on Iris, the runner of the gods.
  The clause after the dash is the translator explaining the joke. Homer gives only the reason ("because he ran messages"). "A man's twist" is also unclear.
  Fix: end the sentence at "for anyone who sent him." Put the Iris note in the enrichment sidecar if it is wanted.
- `seg-0001` · register · low
  > a public beggar who used to go cadging all over the town
  "Cadging" is British slang that many American readers will not know.
  Fix: "who used to go begging all over the town".
- `seg-0038` · register · low
  > mouthing off like that in front of everyone
  Current slang, and the word dates fast. `seg-0032` "running your mouth here" is the same register, and Melantho may carry it, but Eurymachus should not.
  Fix: "for talking like that in front of everyone, without a shred of fear".
- `seg-0025` · read-aloud · low
  > The night is near when this hateful marriage will fall to a miserable woman whose happiness Zeus has stripped away.
  The shift to third person makes the reader wonder who "a miserable woman" is. The Greek says "to me, accursed".
  Fix: "The night is near when this hateful marriage will fall on me, wretched as I am, whose happiness Zeus has stripped away."
- `seg-0023` · voice · low
  > But I do take in everything now
  This phrasing is stiff.
  Fix: "But I see and understand everything now".
- `seg-0012` · register · low
  > Then he slung his shabby, torn old wallet over his shoulder
  "Wallet" in its old sense of a bag recurs through this book (`seg-0012`) and the last. Readers picture a billfold.
  Fix: "his shabby, torn old beggar's bag".

## Best lines
- `seg-0014` "Of all things that breathe and crawl across the earth, the earth breeds nothing feebler than a man."
- `seg-0037` "those wide doors would seem narrow enough to you, for all their width, as you scrambled to get out through the porch and away."
