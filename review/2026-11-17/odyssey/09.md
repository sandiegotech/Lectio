# Odyssey · Book IX · The Cicones, the Lotus-Eaters, and the Cyclops
Grade: A
The Cyclops episode is told with full force and exact detail, and both puns (Nobody, and the play on cunning) survive. Only polish remains.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > and so too did the cunning Aeaean witch, Circe
  "Witch" is a gloss the Greek does not use here (Κίρκη Αἰαίη δολόεσσα); elsewhere the text calls her a goddess.
  Fix: and so too did Circe of Aeaea, cunning as she is

- `seg-0013` · voice · low
  > It would have been far better if we had. But I would not listen — though it would have served us well —
  Says the same regret twice in two clauses; the Greek says it once.
  Fix: But I would not listen — though it would have been far better if I had —

- `seg-0009` · voice · low
  > the daughters of Zeus who holds the storm-cloud
  The same epithet (αἰγίοχος) is "who holds/carries the storm-shield" in Books IV–VI; here and in seg-0017 it becomes "storm-cloud".
  Fix: the daughters of Zeus who holds the storm-shield (and the same in seg-0017).

## Best lines
- Nobody is my name. Nobody my mother and father call me, and all my friends.
- And now a little nothing of a man, a feeble weakling, has blinded my eye — but only after he downed me with his wine.
