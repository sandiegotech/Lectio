# Odyssey · Book VI · Nausicaa
Grade: A
Faithful, graceful and well paced; the meeting on the shore and the "one mind" blessing are as good as any English version. Two small polish items.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · low
  > Then Nausicaa took the whip and the bright reins and cracked them to start the mules.
  "Cracked them" makes her crack the reins too; the Greek lashes with the whip.
  Fix: Then Nausicaa took the whip and the bright reins, and cracked the whip to start the mules.

- `seg-0013` · read-aloud · low
  > for I don't think my troubles are over yet, but the gods have much in store for me before that.
  "Before that" has no clear antecedent.
  Fix: for I don't think my troubles are over yet; the gods have much still in store for me before they end.

## Best lines
- For there is nothing finer, nothing stronger than this — when two people keep house together, of one heart and one mind, a grief to those who hate them and a joy to those who wish them well; and they themselves know it best of all.
- A moment ago he seemed to me a wretched thing, and now he looks like the gods who hold the wide heaven.
