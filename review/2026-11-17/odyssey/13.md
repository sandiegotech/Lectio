# The Odyssey · Book XIII · The Return to Ithaca
Grade: B
Close to the Greek (closer than Butler: stern not "prow curvetted", "bright crown of Troy's towers" kept), strong voice; a handful of mistranslations and two phrases that read the wrong way.

## Issues
- `seg-0020` · fidelity · medium
  > What am I now? Whose country have I come to?
  Greek is "ah me, whose land have I come to now?" (Butler: "among what manner of people am I fallen?"); "What am I now?" reads as a crisis of identity Homer does not have.
  Fix: "Oh, no — whose country have I come to now?"
- `seg-0027` · fidelity · medium
  > endlessly clever, never full of your own scheming
  "Never full of" reads as "not full of", the opposite of the Greek "insatiable for tricks."
  Fix: "endlessly clever, never tired of scheming."
- `seg-0038` · register · medium
  > a battered wallet full of holes
  "Wallet" in its old sense of a beggar's bag; a modern reader pictures a billfold.
  Fix: "a battered beggar's bag full of holes."
- `seg-0025` · fidelity · low
  > But the wind blew them off their course, hard, however they fought it
  Drops "they had no wish to cheat me", which matters: the lie clears its fictional Phoenicians just after Odysseus has blamed the Phaeacians. The run "begged some Phoenicians aboard — gave them … — to carry me off" also trips aloud.
  Fix: "I went down to a ship and begged some Phoenicians to take me — I paid them well from my plunder — and set me down at Pylos, or in Elis where the Epeians rule. But the wind drove them off course, hard against their will; they had no wish to cheat me."
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · low
  > lord of the wide earth, shaker of the ground … the punishment is always yours to take, after, as you see fit
  The epithet is "wide in strength", not "of the wide earth"; and "to take, after," stumbles.
  Fix: "What are you saying, mighty Earth-shaker? … you are always free to punish him, now or later, as you see fit."
- `seg-0006` · voice · low
  > said to her, his words flying
  Same calque in `seg-0021`. "Winged words" is the recognizable formula; "his words flying" sounds like a mistake.
  Fix: "and spoke to her, and his words had wings:" or simply "and said to her:"
- `seg-0027` · fidelity · low
  > help you hide the treasure they sent you home with
  Drops Athena's claim that the Phaeacians gave it "by my counsel and design", which is her point.
  Fix: "help you hide the treasure the Phaeacians gave you at my prompting."

## Best lines
- `seg-0008` "a deep, sweet, unbroken sleep, the very likeness of death."
- `seg-0034` "as on the day we tore down the bright crown of Troy's towers."
