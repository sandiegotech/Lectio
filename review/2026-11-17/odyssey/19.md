# The Odyssey · Book XIX · By the Fire, and the Scar
Grade: B
This is the edition at its best: the weaving trick, the brooch, the boar hunt, the scar, the gates of horn and ivory. But the famous "lies like truth" line has been flattened, a repeated formula is rendered inconsistently, and the nurse's name changes spelling between books.

## Issues
- `seg-0016` · fidelity · medium
  > Many another likely tale he told her
  Homer's line is "he made the many lies he told seem like the truth" (*iske pseudea polla legōn etumoisin homoia*). It is one of the most quoted lines in the poem and here it is lost.
  Fix: "So he went on, telling lie after lie that sounded like the truth, and Penelope wept to hear it".
- `seg-0001` · voice · medium
  > for steel within a man's reach has a way of drawing his hand to it
  This is the same Greek line as XVI `seg-0024`, which the edition renders "for the iron itself draws a man on". A repeated line should repeat. "Steel" is also an anachronism.
  Fix: "for the iron itself draws a man on."
- `seg-0002` · form · medium
  > he called the nurse Eurycleia
  The spelling is "Eurycleia" here and in XXI–XXIII, but "Euryclea" in XVII `seg-0004` and XX. The proper noun must be one spelling across the work.
  Fix: choose one spelling (Eurycleia matches this book and three others) and apply it in XVII and XX.
- `seg-0034` · fidelity · low
  > I keep twenty geese about the house, that come up out of the water to eat the soaked grain
  The Greek is that they "eat wheat out of water", meaning soaked grain. They do not come up out of a pond.
  Fix: "I keep twenty geese about the house that eat their grain soaked in water, and I love to watch them."
- `seg-0016` · fidelity · low
  > when the West Wind has heaped it up and the East and South winds breathe upon it
  The South wind is added. In Homer the East wind melts what the West wind poured down.
  Fix: "when the West Wind has heaped it up and the East Wind thaws it".
