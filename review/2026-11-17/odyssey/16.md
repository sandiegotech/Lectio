# The Odyssey · Book XVI · Father and Son
Grade: B
The recognition scene lands, and the edition keeps "the iron itself draws a man on" and "on the knees of the gods"; there are two stumbles and one mistranslated phrase.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · medium
  > and eat and drink with his hands when the mood was on him
  The Greek is "drink and eat with the servants in the house". "With his hands" reads literally as eating with his fingers.
  Fix: "and eat and drink with the servants in the house when he had a mind to."
- `seg-0015` · read-aloud · medium
  > "Stranger," he said, "you are changed — a moment ago, and now you are not the same man.
  The syntax breaks; "a moment ago" attaches to nothing.
  Fix: "Stranger, you are not the man you were a moment ago."
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · low
  > Odysseus, son of Laertes, born to many turns
  This merges two epithets and loses "Zeus-born". Elsewhere the edition uses "born of Zeus … of the many schemes" (XIII `seg-0033`).
  Fix: "Son of Laertes, born of Zeus, Odysseus of the many schemes".
- `seg-0029` · read-aloud · low
  > she can marry the one who gives the most, the man it falls to to win her
  "To to" trips aloud.
  Fix: "and she can marry the one who gives the most, the man who is fated to win her."
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > He kissed his head and both his shining eyes
  Drops "and both his hands".
  Fix: "He kissed his head and both his shining eyes and both his hands".
- `seg-0033` · fidelity · low
  > Eurymachus, son of Polybus, answered her smoothly
  "Smoothly" gives away in advance the twist that Homer saves for the end of the speech.
  Fix: "Eurymachus, son of Polybus, answered her".

## Best lines
- `seg-0018` "No other Odysseus will ever come; I am he."
- `seg-0024` "for the iron itself draws a man on."
