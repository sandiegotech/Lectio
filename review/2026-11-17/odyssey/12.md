Grade: A
Complete and gripping, with the Sirens' song, Scylla's fishing simile and the fig tree over Charybdis all exact. One clumsy sentence remains.

## Issues
- `seg-0021` · read-aloud · low
  > where his fine broad-browed cattle grazed and his many flocks of sheep belonging to Helios who walks above us.
  "His … belonging to Helios" says the owner twice, and the same paragraph renders one epithet (τερψιμβρότου) two ways: "who gives men their joy" and then "who gives men their light".
  Fix: where the fine broad-browed cattle and the many fat flocks of Helios, who walks above us, were grazing. (And later: "the island of the Sun, who gives men joy" in both places.)
