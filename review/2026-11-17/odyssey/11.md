# Odyssey · Book XI · The Land of the Dead
Grade: B
Very fine: Anticleia, Agamemnon, Achilles and Ajax are full and true, and the famous lines hold. One phrase changes Epicaste's guilt, and two small fixes remain.

## Issues
- `seg-0023` · fidelity · medium
  > who in the blindness of her heart did a monstrous thing — she married her own son.
  "Blindness of her heart" suggests a moral fault. The Greek (ἀϊδρείῃσι νόοιο) says she acted in ignorance, not knowing who he was, and that is the point of the story.
  Fix: who did a monstrous thing without knowing it — she married her own son.

- `seg-0017` · read-aloud · low
  > and sits at the feasts a man who deals out justice is asked to share, for everyone invites him.
  The relative clause is missing its "that" and the sentence trips.
  Fix: and sits at the public feasts that a man who deals out justice should attend, for everyone invites him.

- `seg-0045` · register · low
  > for it laid in the earth that great head, Ajax
  "Head" meaning a person (κεφαλήν) is the same idiom flagged in Book I; a modern reader takes it literally.
  Fix: for it laid in the earth so great a man, Ajax,

## Best lines
- It was longing for you, my glorious Odysseus — your kindness and your counsel — that took the sweet life out of me.
- Don't gild death for me, glorious Odysseus. I would rather be a hired hand, working another man's land … and be alive, than be lord over all the wasted dead.
