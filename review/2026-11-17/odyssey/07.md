# Odyssey · Book VII · The Palace of Alcinous
Grade: B
Close and luminous; the palace and the orchard are exact and lovely. One garbled line about Arete and two small phrasing slips.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · medium
  > and the men whose wives are her friends she helps to settle even their own quarrels.
  This adopts a strained reading and inverts the syntax so the line is hard to follow. The Greek (οἷσίν τ' εὖ φρονέῃσι καὶ ἀνδράσι νείκεα λύει) says she settles quarrels for those she favors, even quarrels between men.
  Fix: and for those she favors she settles quarrels, even the quarrels of men.

- `seg-0020` · read-aloud · low
  > So she said to him, winged words:
  Garbled; a word is missing.
  Fix: So she spoke to him with winged words:

- `seg-0015` · read-aloud · low
  > who walks beside every suppliant who comes in honor.
  "Who comes in honor" is unclear; the Greek calls suppliants αἰδοίοισιν, men owed reverence. The phrase recurs in seg-0016.
  Fix: who walks beside suppliants, whom all must honor. (seg-0016: "the protector of all suppliants, whom all must honor.")
