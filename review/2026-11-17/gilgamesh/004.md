# The Epic of Gilgamesh · Tablet IV · The Road to the Cedars
Grade: B
The repeated march formula is kept with real discipline and the third dream is superb; Shamash's warning is moved out of place and two of the five dreams are summarized away.

No comparison edition is bundled. Fidelity points below are checked from knowledge of the Standard Babylonian text and are unverified against a bundled source.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · high
  > And as Gilgamesh prayed once more to the sun-god, Shamash heard him out of heaven
  In the SB, Shamash's warning (hurry, Humbaba wears one of his seven cloaks, six laid aside) comes at the forest's edge, after all the dreams, when Gilgamesh weeps before him. Here it sits between the third dream and the summary of "more dreams along the road", so the order of events is wrong. The last sentence ("the warm wind of Shamash blew at Humbaba's face, so that he could not move") belongs to the fight in Tablet V and spends that climax early. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: move seg-0010 after seg-0011 (so it immediately precedes arrival at the forest), opening "Gilgamesh wept before Shamash, and Shamash heard him out of heaven..." and cut its final sentence about the wind.
- `seg-0011` · fidelity · medium
  > There were more dreams along the road, and not all of them can be read now, for the clay is broken.
  The SB has five dreams. The fourth (a monstrous Anzu-like creature breathing fire, and a man who binds Gilgamesh's arms and frees him) is broken but readable in large part; the fifth (a wild bull) is fragmentary. Summarizing both, in the narrator's voice, drops a dream that survives. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: render the fourth dream in the same pattern as the first three (formula, dream, Enkidu's reading), then mark the rest as an editorial note: "[A fifth dream follows, but the clay is too broken to read it whole.]"
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > Beside the two of us we were no bigger than reeds in a marsh.
  The image in the source is flies of the reed-beds: the mountain falls and the two men are like tiny flies. The sentence is also garbled ("Beside the two of us we were"). Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: "Beside the mountain, the two of us were like flies in the reeds."
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > who was born in the wild and knew the meaning of such things
  An explanation added to the narration; the source just has Enkidu answer.
  Fix: "And Enkidu said to him:"
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > For a long time Gilgamesh could not speak; the dream had frightened him to the bone.
  Not in the source; added emotion.
  Fix: cut the sentence.
- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > the path that ran up into the wood was thick with brush, and it was good to walk.
  Thick brush that is good to walk on contradicts itself. The source sets sweet shade against a thicket of thorns.
  Fix: "the shade was sweet and full of rest, and the undergrowth beneath it was a tangle of thorns."

## Best lines

- "Gilgamesh lay down with his chin on his knees, and sleep came over him, the way sleep falls on all people."
