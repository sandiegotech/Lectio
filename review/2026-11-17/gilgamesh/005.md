# The Epic of Gilgamesh · Tablet V · Humbaba and the Cedars
Grade: B
A powerful tablet with the forest's music and all thirteen winds intact; the opening repeats the end of Tablet IV, and several lines explain what the text leaves to the reader.

No comparison edition is bundled. Fidelity points below are checked from knowledge of the Standard Babylonian text (including the forest-noise lines from the Suleimaniyah tablet) and are unverified against a bundled source.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · form · medium
  > And there before them was the cedar mountain, the home of the gods, the throne of Irnini.
  Tablet IV seg-0012 already has the arrival, the cedar mountain "home of the gods, the throne of Irnini" and the restful shade. A reader going through in order meets the same arrival twice. The lines belong to the opening of Tablet V.
  Fix: cut the duplicated arrival from Tablet IV (end IV with the gate and Enkidu's numb hand, or with "and they went in" without the description) and keep this fuller version here.
- `seg-0011` · fidelity · medium
  > For the door was to be a gift for Enlil's own house in Nippur — a thing made from the forest, given back to the god who had set Humbaba to guard the forest.
  An editor's comment on the irony, written into the story. Enkidu's speech already says where the door goes.
  Fix: cut the sentence.
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > and he listened to it the way a king listens to musicians at his table.
  The source compares the noise to musicians and drummers playing daily before Humbaba. The king at his table is added. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: end at "...before the face of Humbaba." and cut "For this was the garden... at his table."
- `seg-0003` · form · low
  > “A slippery path holds no fear for two who hold each other up,” one said.
  "One said" leaves the speech without a speaker. Name the speaker the source gives, or make it shared.
  Fix: "...they said to each other."
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · low
  > Hermon split, and Lebanon split.
  The source names Sirion (the old name for Hermon). Swapping in the modern identification is a gloss; also "Then Shamash... did the thing Ninsun had asked of him on the temple roof" adds a cross-reference the text does not make.
  Fix: "Sirion and Lebanon split apart." and "Then Shamash loosed the great winds against Humbaba..."
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · low
  > since it was Enkidu who held the king's ear, and his pleading turned to something sharper.
  Added motive and stage direction.
  Fix: "And Humbaba turned to Enkidu."
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · low
  > May Enkidu have no portion of life beyond his friend Gilgamesh — let him not outlive this day's work.
  The curse in the source is that the pair should not grow old, and that Enkidu should have no one to bury him but Gilgamesh. "Let him not outlive this day's work" is not in it and blurs a curse the epic later fulfils exactly. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: "May the two of you never grow old. And may Enkidu find no one to bury him but his friend Gilgamesh."
- `seg-0010` · read-aloud · low
  > And a great fullness came down over the mountain, twice over — abundance poured down over the mountain.
  The sentence doubles back on itself and loses its stress.
  Fix: "And rain came down over the mountain in abundance."

## Best lines
- "High in the branches the mother monkeys called and the young monkeys squealed, and the whole green mountain was a kind of music"
- "And Gilgamesh stood on the raft and held in his hands the head of Humbaba."
