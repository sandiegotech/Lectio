# The Epic of Gilgamesh · Tablet VI · Ishtar and the Bull of Heaven
Grade: B
The best tablet so far: Gilgamesh's catalogue of Ishtar's lovers has real bite and the Bull fight moves fast. It needs small fixes, mostly where the text has been made more polite and in the closing apparatus.

No comparison edition is bundled. Fidelity points below are checked from knowledge of the Standard Babylonian text and are unverified against a bundled source.

## Issues
- `seg-0013` · form · medium
  > Here Tablet VI breaks off, and the dream he carries to Gilgamesh — the great gods sitting in judgment — is taken up at the head of Tablet VII.
  Tablet VI does not break off. It ends whole, with Enkidu rising to tell his dream, and the dream opens Tablet VII. The sentence is apparatus in the narrator's voice, it is inaccurate, and it gives away the next tablet. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: "And he rose from his bed, and said to his friend:" (end the tablet on the open speech, as the clay does).
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > Put out your hand; touch me here.
  The source is explicit: Ishtar tells Ishullanu to touch her vulva. "Here" makes it coy, which changes the tone of the propositioning that Gilgamesh is condemning. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: "Put out your hand and touch my sex."
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > The Bull lashed its tail and threw its slaver in Enkidu's face.
  The source has the Bull spit in his face and fling its dung at him with its tail. The dung, the insult of it, is dropped. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: "The Bull spat in Enkidu's face, and with its thick tail it flung its dung at him."
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > A palace that caves in on the soldiers inside it. Pitch that blackens the hands that carry it.
  The catalogue in the SB also has an elephant that shakes off its harness. One item of a famous list is missing. Unverified against a bundled source.
  Fix: after "A palace that caves in on the soldiers inside it." add "An elephant that throws off its harness."
