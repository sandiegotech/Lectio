# The Iliad · Book III · The Duel for Helen
Grade: B
A beautiful book. The Teichoscopia and the bedroom scene are first-rate. A wrong epithet, a muddled introduction for Laodice, and a simile bent out of shape need fixing.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > the Greeks sail home to Argos and the green country of Achaea
  The Greek formula is "horse-pasturing Argos and Achaea of the lovely women". "Green country" is invented, and the women, which matter in a duel over Helen, are lost. The same line comes back in seg-0023.
  Fix (both places): "the Greeks sail home to Argos, where the horses graze, and to Achaea of the lovely women"

- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · medium
  > taking the shape of her sister-in-law — the wife of Antenor's son, for Helicaon had married Laodice, the loveliest of Priam's daughters.
  The reader can't tell whose shape Iris takes until the end of the sentence, and even then it is unclear.
  Fix: "taking the shape of her sister-in-law Laodice, the loveliest of Priam's daughters, wife of Antenor's son Helicaon."

- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > that's how hard the edge of your scorn cuts.
  Paris compares Hector's *heart*, unyielding and fearless, to the axe. Turning it into "scorn" makes the simile about Hector's insult and loses Paris's backhanded praise.
  Fix: "Your heart is always unbending, like an axe a shipwright drives through a beam, the blade adding its force to the man's stroke — that is how fearless the heart in your chest is."

- `seg-0031` · read-aloud · low
  > and the sword shattered against it, three and four pieces, and fell from his hand.
  The missing preposition makes it trip.
  Fix: "and the sword shattered against it into three, four pieces, and fell from his hand."

- `seg-0013` · voice · low
  > they spoke low to one another, with wings in their words
  An over-literal formula that reads as a mannerism here. Elsewhere the edition uses "winged words" cleanly.
  Fix: "they spoke low to one another, winged words"

- `seg-0027` · register · low
  > climbed up beside him in the splendid car
  "Car" for chariot is a leftover archaism, and the segment says "chariot" two lines earlier.
  Fix: "in the splendid chariot"

## Best lines
- "So she spoke. But the life-giving earth already held them fast, away in Lacedaemon, in their own dear country."
- "you cursed, pretty Paris, you woman-crazed seducer, all looks and lies"
