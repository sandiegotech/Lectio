# The Iliad · Book XIV · The Deception of Zeus
Grade: B
A fine book: the deception scene is graceful and exact. A dropped name and a few slips of wording.

## Issues
- `seg-0015` · fidelity · medium
  > they took me in and raised me in their own house, when Zeus thrust Cronus down
  This drops "taking me from Rhea", so the reader loses who gave Hera into their care (Butler: "having taken me over from Rhaea").
  Fix: they took me from Rhea and raised me in their own house, when Zeus thrust Cronus down.
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > the Greeks in headlong rout, their wall thrown down, the Trojans driving them before it
  "Before it" has no right antecedent and reads as if the wall were driving them.
  Fix: the Trojans driving them headlong.
- `seg-0031` · register · low
  > Any tough soldier holding a small shield
  "Tough soldier" is a touch colloquial for the "staunch fighter" of the source.
  Fix: Any staunch fighter holding a small shield.
- `seg-0035` · register · low
  > the painted car
  "Car" for chariot is leftover archaism, and today it means an automobile.
  Fix: the painted chariot.

## Best lines
- seg-0017: "in it was love, in it was longing, in it was the soft, coaxing talk of lovers — the whispered word that steals the wits even of the wise."
- seg-0037: "so that his head and mouth and nostrils hit the ground before his shins and his knees as he fell."
