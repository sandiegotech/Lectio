# The Iliad · Book X · The Night Raid
Grade: B
The Doloneia is crisp, close and grimly funny in the right places. The one real fault is an invented last line that editorializes.

## Issues
- `seg-0050` · fidelity · high
  > And so the night, which had begun in sleepless dread, ended in the salt water and the wine — the only Greek victory in many days, and it had cost them nothing.
  No source has this. Book X ends with the libation to Athena (seg-0049). The added sentence is commentary dressed as narrative, and its claims are the editor's ("the only Greek victory in many days"). It is the same kind of invented closing beat as Book V seg-0058.
  Fix: delete the segment (or empty it and flag it for the human gate if the id must stand).

## Best lines
- "the two went forward like a pair of lions through the black night, through the slaughter and the corpses and the scattered arms and the dark blood."
- "For an evil dream had stood that night above his head: the grandson of Oeneus himself, by the design of Athena."
