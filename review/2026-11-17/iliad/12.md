# The Iliad · Book XII · The Storming of the Wall
Grade: A
Excellent. The wall's future ruin, Polydamas and the omen, Sarpedon's speech to Glaucus and Hector at the gate are all faithful and strong aloud. Ship it.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · register · low
  > the Greeks and Trojans fought on without let
  "Without let" (without hindrance) is an archaism almost no reader now carries.
  Fix: "the Greeks and Trojans fought on without pause"

- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > whom the great tawny horses of the Selleis breed had carried from Arisbe
  The Greek says his great sorrel horses carried him from Arisbe, from the river Selleis. There is no "Selleis breed". Book II (seg-0069) has the line right.
  Fix: "whom his great sorrel horses had carried from Arisbe, from the river Selleis"
