# The Iliad · Book XVIII · The Shield of Achilles
Grade: B
Achilles' grief and the Shield are rendered beautifully. One real error: Hephaestus's welcome to Thetis is given to his golden handmaids.

## Issues
- `seg-0029` · fidelity · high
  > They moved to hold him up as he came near Thetis, sat her on a fine chair, took her hand, and said: "Thetis, honored and always welcome …"
  The speaker is wrong. In the source the handmaids help their master, and then Hephaestus himself comes to Thetis, takes her hand and speaks (Butler: "while he drew near to Thetis, seated her upon a goodly seat, and took her hand in his own, saying"). As written, the serving-women make his promise, and "Hephaestus answered" in seg-0031 has nothing to answer.
  Fix: They moved busily to hold their master up, and he came near to Thetis, sat down on a fine chair beside her, took her hand, and said: "Thetis, honored and always welcome …"
- `seg-0018` · fidelity · medium
  > so sharp and brazen was the voice of the son of Aeacus
  Achilles is Aeacus's grandson. The source has *Aiakides* (see XVI and XVII).
  Fix: the voice of the grandson of Aeacus.
- `seg-0029` · fidelity · low
  > the huge, sooty creature heaved himself up from the anvil
  "Sooty" is added. The source calls him a huge, monstrous figure (Butler: "the mighty monster").
  Fix: the huge, misshapen god heaved himself up from the anvil.
- `seg-0023` · register · low
  > now is no time to peddle that kind of talk to the people
  "Peddle" is a little cheap for Hector's rebuke.
  Fix: now is no time to spread that kind of talk among the people.
