# The Iliad · Book XVI · The Death of Patroclus
Grade: B
A strong book, and the death of Patroclus lands with full weight. A handful of fidelity slips around titles and the dying speech, and one ungrammatical line.

## Issues
- `seg-0046` · fidelity · high
  > it was deadly fate and the son of Leto who killed me, and of men, Euphorbus; you are only the third to take my armor
  The famous taunt is that Hector is only the third in the killing, not the third to take the armor (Butler: "third only in the killing of me"). Apollo stripped the armor, so the line as written undercuts itself.
  Fix: and of men, Euphorbus; you come only third in the killing of me.
- `seg-0046` · fidelity · medium
  > you will go down under the hands of blameless Achilles, the son of Aeacus
  Achilles is Peleus's son. The source has *Aiakides*, a descendant of Aeacus. The same slip is in seg-0007, seg-0008 and seg-0047 ("the swift-footed son of Aeacus"), and it recurs across the work, which elsewhere says "grandson of Aeacus".
  Fix: blameless Achilles, grandson of Aeacus (and "the swift-footed grandson of Aeacus" throughout).
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > God keep me from a rage like the one you nurse
  "God keep me" brings in a monotheist idiom. The source says "may such anger never take hold of me".
  Fix: May no such rage as yours ever take hold of me.
- `seg-0035` · read-aloud · medium
  > for a man was killed who was no worst among the Myrmidons
  This is ungrammatical, and the reader stumbles.
  Fix: for a man was killed who was far from the worst of the Myrmidons.
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > as if I were some nobody with no honor at all
  The source image is a dishonored drifter, an outsider with no standing (Butler: "a vagrant"). "Some nobody" also appears in XV.
  Fix: as if I were some homeless drifter with no honor at all.
- `seg-0019` · register · low
  > he tumbled from the car
  "Car" for chariot recurs here and in seg-0022, seg-0023, seg-0024 and seg-0042.
  Fix: he tumbled from the chariot (and likewise in the others).
- `seg-0040` · form · low
  > no, nor Achilles's
  The possessive is inconsistent: seg-0009 has "Achilles' own companion".
  Fix: no, nor Achilles'.

## Best lines
- seg-0043: "and there in the whirl of dust he lay, mighty in his might, his horsemanship forgotten."
- seg-0047: "his spirit flew from his limbs and went down to the house of Hades, mourning its fate, leaving manhood and youth behind."
