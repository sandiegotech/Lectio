# The Iliad · Book XVII · The Fight over Patroclus
Grade: B
Among the best books of the edition: the weeping horses, Ajax's prayer for light, and the tug-of-war over the body are all superb. Only small fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > the horses of the proud son of Aeacus
  Achilles is Aeacus's grandson. The source has *Aiakides*, and this same book has "the grandson of Aeacus" in seg-0019 and seg-0029.
  Fix: the horses of the proud grandson of Aeacus.
- `seg-0010` · register · low
  > That great name of yours is wasted; you're a runner.
  "Runner" reads as an athlete. The source means a man who runs away, a coward.
  Fix: That great name of yours is wasted; you're a man who runs.
- `seg-0011` · register · low
  > Zeus, who makes even a brave man run and strips the win from him
  "The win" is sports-page usage.
  Fix: and snatches the victory from him.
- `seg-0031` · register · low
  > alone in the sacred car
  "Car" for chariot recurs here and in seg-0032 ("get down from the car").
  Fix: alone in the sacred chariot / I'll get down from the chariot.

## Best lines
- seg-0030: "For of all things that breathe and move upon the earth, there is nothing more wretched than a man."
- seg-0039: "and if it's your will to destroy us, then destroy us — but in the light."
