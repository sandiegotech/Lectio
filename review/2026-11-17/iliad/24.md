# The Iliad · Book XXIV · The Ransom of Hector
Grade: B
A worthy close. Priam's plea, the two jars, Niobe and the three laments are exact and moving, and the last line is perfect. The fixes are a repeated patronymic and a few small word choices.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > "Take heart, Priam, son of Dardanus," she said.
  Priam is Laomedon's son (XX). *Dardanides* means "of Dardanus's line". The same rendering is in seg-0024 and twice in seg-0042 ("Priam, son of Dardanus, gazed at Achilles … wondered at Priam, son of Dardanus").
  Fix: "Take heart, Priam, of the line of Dardanus," / in seg-0042, drop the patronymic the second time: "And Achilles in turn wondered at Priam".
- `seg-0039` · register · low
  > and I would sin against the command of Zeus
  "Sin" carries a Christian sense. The source means "transgress". It recurs in seg-0040 ("and sin against the command of Zeus").
  Fix: and I would break the command of Zeus.
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > that sense of shame that ruins a man, or saves him, depending on how he heeds it
  "Depending on how he heeds it" explains the line inside the text. The source says only that shame both harms and helps men greatly.
  Fix: that sense of shame that does men great harm, and great good.
- `seg-0037` · fidelity · low
  > In all the land bounded by Lesbos out to sea
  This drops "Lesbos, the seat of Macar", the one proper noun in the line.
  Fix: In all the land bounded by Lesbos, Macar's seat, out to sea
- `seg-0049` · register · low
  > for many a Greek bit the dust under Hector's hands
  The cowboy cliché again (see XIX, XXII). It sits badly inside Andromache's lament.
  Fix: for many a Greek bit the earth under Hector's hands

## Best lines
- seg-0035: "I have brought myself to do what no other man on earth has done — to lift to my lips the hand of the man who killed my son."
- seg-0054: "Such was the funeral of Hector, breaker of horses."
