# The Iliad · Book XX · The Gods Go to War
Grade: B
Faithful and energetic. The theomachy staging, Aeneas's genealogy and Achilles' rampage all read well. Two clauses blur their sense, and there are small form fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0034` · read-aloud · medium
  > This too has been found sharp before now.
  "This" has no clear antecedent. Hector means his own spear (Butler: "my spear has a point as well as yours").
  Fix: My spear too has been found sharp before now.
- `seg-0021` · read-aloud · medium
  > fool that he was, his mind missing it: that the glorious gifts of the gods do not break easily
  The aside is tangled, and the colon splice loses the listener.
  Fix: fool that he was, not to see in his heart that the glorious gifts of the gods do not break easily
- `seg-0023` · form · low
  > so the son of Cronos doesn't rage
  The spelling is inconsistent: the rest of the edition has "Cronus".
  Fix: so the son of Cronus doesn't rage
- `seg-0037` · register · low
  > he crashed headlong from his car
  "Car" for chariot, again in seg-0038 ("the rails around the car").
  Fix: from his chariot / the rails around the chariot.
