# The Iliad · Book VIII · The Tide of Battle Turns
Grade: A
Faithful and strong, from the golden chain to the watchfires. Only polish needed.

## Issues
- `seg-0034` · fidelity · low
  > But you — you reckless, brazen thing — will you really dare lift that great spear against Zeus?
  Softened. Zeus's message calls Athena a "fearless bitch" (*kuon adees*). The edition keeps Helen's "bitch" in Book VI, so it should keep the insult here.
  Fix: "But you — you shameless bitch, if you really dare lift that great spear against Zeus —"

- `seg-0031` · register · low
  > to fetch the hound of hell out of Erebus
  "Hell" brings in a Christian frame. The Greek is "the hound of hateful Hades".
  Fix: "to fetch the hound of hateful Hades out of Erebus"

- `seg-0039` · register · low
  > I won’t care a whit how angry you are
  "A whit" is a leftover archaism.
  Fix: "I won’t care in the least how angry you are"
