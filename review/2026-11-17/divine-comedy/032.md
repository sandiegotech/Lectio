# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXXII
Grade: A
Cocytus is cold and hard as it should be; the Bocca episode keeps its savagery. Two small slips.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > better for you if here you'd been sheep or goats.
  Dante's "qui" means here in the world, where the poet writes ("mei foste state qui pecore o zebe"); "here" in this tercet reads as here in Hell.
  Fix: better for you if you had been born sheep or goats.

- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > with its muzzle out of the water — back when / the peasant girl is dreaming of her gleaning —
  "Back when" points to a past moment; Dante means the season, early summer, when she often dreams of gleaning.
  Fix: with its muzzle out of the water, in the season / when the peasant girl dreams of her gleaning —
