# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto VI
Grade: A
Cerberus is vivid and Ciacco's prophecy is clear without being explained; two small word choices.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · register · low
  > it had shut down before the pity of those two
  "Shut down" now reads as machinery; Dante's mind simply closed ("che si chiuse").
  Fix: it had closed itself before the pity of those two

- `seg-0022` · fidelity · low
  > and the party from the woods
  "La parte selvaggia" is the rustic party, the country-bred Whites (Longfellow: "the rustic party"); "from the woods" literalizes it into a place.
  Fix: and the rustic party

## Best lines
- "You were made before I was unmade."
- "The just are two, and no one there will hear them."
