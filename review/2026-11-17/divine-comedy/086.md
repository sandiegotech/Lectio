# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XIX
Grade: A
The Eagle's argument on the virtuous pagan is lucid and severe, and the catalogue of bad kings keeps its bite; only polish.

## Issues
- `seg-0049` · form · low
  > as first installment of it, Nicosia / and Famagosta wail and rage
  The last tercet spills into the closing line, so seg-0050 has two lines where the source has one.
  Fix: seg-0049 "…as first installment, Nicosia / and Famagosta wail and rage because of their own beast," and seg-0050 "who never strays from the others' flank."
- `seg-0006` · read-aloud · low
  > though they do not follow the story.
  Ambiguous; source: they praise the memory but do not carry on its example.
  Fix: "praise it — though they do not follow its example."
- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > to those who joy up there.
  "joy" as a verb is archaic.
  Fix: "with songs known only to those who rejoice up there."
