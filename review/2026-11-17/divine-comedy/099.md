# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXXII
Grade: B
Bernard's tour of the Rose is faithful and has fine moments, but the verse here leans on filler and padded pentameter more than the cantos around it, and one line has a stray pronoun.

## Issues
- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · medium
  > so opposite she sits the seat of John —
  "she" has no place in the sentence (Mary is on the other side); source: so, opposite, does the seat of the great John.
  Fix: "so, opposite, the seat of the great John — / holy always, who endured / the desert, martyrdom, then two years in Hell —"
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > beneath her, Rachel takes her seat, you see, / with Beatrice ranged beside her there.
  "you see" and "ranged … there" are line-filling; the canto has several such fillers (seg-0005 "as I myself", seg-0030 "the heights of that great altitude").
  Fix: "beneath her Rachel sits, as you can see, / and Beatrice beside her."
- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > and under him to share the dividing fell / to Francis, Benedict, and Augustine,
  Inverted and hard to follow aloud.
  Fix: "and under him, chosen to keep that line, / sit Francis, Benedict, and Augustine,"
- `seg-0026` · read-aloud · low
  > enough, with innocence, to win salvation / that the parents' faith alone should answer for them;
  The subject arrives two lines late.
  Fix: "True, in the early centuries, the parents' faith / was enough, with innocence, / to win salvation for their children;"
- `seg-0022` · form · low
  > with grace in varying measure — here, let the result
  The sentence runs across the tercet break ("let the result / suffice"), so the segment does not end where the source stanza does.
  Fix: end seg-0022 at "…let the result suffice." and begin seg-0023 "And this is set down plain and clear".

## Best lines
- "is the father by whose reckless taste / the human race tastes bitterness so deep;"
- "here we will make full stop, like a good tailor / who cuts the gown to fit the cloth he has,"
