# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXXIII
Grade: B
Bernard's prayer and the final vision are worthy of the poem, and the last line survives intact; but that line's lead-in strands a "by" at the end of a line, and two clauses lose their sense.

## Issues
- `seg-0048` · read-aloud · medium
  > like a wheel that is moved evenly and whole, by
  The most famous line in the poem is set up by a dangling "by" at the end of the line; "and whole" is also padding.
  Fix: seg-0048 "Here power failed the high imagining; / but already my desire and will were turning / like a wheel that is moved evenly," and seg-0049 "by the Love that moves the sun and the other stars."
- `seg-0035` · fidelity · medium
  > and anything / perfect outside of it is flawed within.
  Source: what is perfect there is defective outside it. The Lectio talks about things "perfect outside of it," which is the opposite case.
  Fix: "is gathered whole in this, and outside it / whatever is perfect there falls short."
- `seg-0015` · read-aloud · medium
  > no creature's eye, you cannot believe, / ever looked with sight so clear.
  "you cannot believe" sits in the sentence like an aside to the reader; source: it is not to be believed that any creature's eye could enter it so clearly.
  Fix: "then they turned to the Eternal Light, into which / no creature's eye, we must believe, / could ever look with sight so clear."
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > toward the last salvation, the end of all.
  "the end of all" is added.
  Fix: "higher still, toward the final salvation."
- `seg-0029` · read-aloud · low
  > Deep in its depths I saw, gathered and
  The line ends on "and," which weakens one of the canto's great images.
  Fix: "Deep in its depths I saw, bound up by love / into a single book, the leaves / scattered through all the universe:"

## Best lines
- "so the Highest Joy may open to his eyes."
- "So the snow comes unsealed under the sun; / so, on the wind, in the weightless leaves, / the Sibyl's oracles were scattered and lost."
