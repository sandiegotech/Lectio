# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXX
Grade: B
The verse is some of the finest in the edition (the river of light, the Rose, the poet's surrender), but from the middle on the stanzas have slid one line out of step with the source.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · form · medium
  > the hard last matter of its labor, ending,
  From here to the end each segment carries the first line of the next source tercet: seg-0015 holds the end of Beatrice's speech, seg-0016 opens with her closing quotation mark and then starts the lightning simile mid-sentence, seg-0024 begins with the last line of the sparks passage, and so on to seg-0050. Segments no longer match the source stanzas, so Compare and the spine pair the wrong lines, and every segment ends mid-sentence.
  Fix: re-cut seg-0012–0050 to the source tercets as Longfellow divides them (seg-0012 ends "…to a final close,"; seg-0013 "when with the bearing … the largest of the spheres / into the heaven that is pure light:"; and so on). The wording itself can stay.
- `seg-0048` · fidelity · low
  > by then, a man so false that openly
  "so false" is added; source says only "such a one" that he will not walk one road with Henry.
  Fix: "by then, such a man that openly / or in secret he will not walk one road with Henry."

## Best lines
- "light / of the intellect, brimming with love — love / of the true good, brimming with joy — a joy / that goes past every sweetness there can be."
- "has made you like the little child who dies / of hunger and yet drives away the nurse."
