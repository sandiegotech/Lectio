# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XI
Grade: B
The map of Hell is laid out lucidly and without glosses; one clause loses its point.

## Issues
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > there is no help for it — he must repent,
  "Sanza pro si penta" is repents in vain, to no profit (Longfellow: "Must needs without avail repent"); "no help for it" reads as "unavoidably" and the futility is lost.
  Fix: and on his own goods; so in the second round / he must repent, and all to no avail,

## Best lines
- "so that your art is, you might say, God's grandchild."
- "that doubting pleases me as much as knowing."
