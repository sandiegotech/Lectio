# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XIV
Grade: A
Guido del Duca's descent of the Arno (hogs, curs, wolves, foxes) and his lament for Romagna are sharp and well voiced; small polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0048` · read-aloud · low
  > "That was the hard bit
  On the ear "bit" reads as "portion" before the bridle sense arrives.
  Fix: "That was the hard bit in the mouth

- `seg-0038` · register · low
  > now that your family has cleared out
  "Cleared out" is casual, and slightly comic for an extinct noble house.
  Fix: now that your family is gone

- `seg-0051` · fidelity · low
  > and so He who discerns it all strikes you down."
  The source is "beats" or chastises you (vi batte); "strikes you down" is harsher and suggests death.
  Fix: and so He who discerns it all chastises you."
