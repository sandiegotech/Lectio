# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XVI
Grade: B
The roll call of old houses keeps its proper names and its bite; three lines garble their sense.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · medium
  > at the first slip Guinevere set down in writing.
  Source: at Guinevere's first fault written in the romance (the lady of Malehaut coughs); the Lectio makes Guinevere the writer.
  Fix: "at Guinevere's first slip, the one the old book tells."
- `seg-0032` · fidelity · medium
  > a load so heavy / it will soon throw the ship overboard,
  Source: a new treachery so heavy it will soon be jettisoned from the ship; the ship is not thrown overboard.
  Fix: "with a fresh treachery, a load so heavy / it will soon be thrown from the ship,"
- `seg-0039` · read-aloud · medium
  > to the man who shows / its teeth or his purse
  "its teeth" attaches the teeth to the breed; it is the man who bares his teeth.
  Fix: "to whoever runs, but to the man who shows / his teeth or his purse turns lamb-gentle,"
- `seg-0044` · fidelity · low
  > rings that shield with a gold border
  "gold" is a gloss; source says only "binds it with a border."
  Fix: "though the man who rings that shield with a border"

## Best lines
- "Truly you are a cloak that quickly shortens, / so that, unless we add to you day by day, / Time keeps circling with his shears."
- "Everything of yours has its own death, / just as you do; but in some it stays hidden, / because they last long and your lives are short."
