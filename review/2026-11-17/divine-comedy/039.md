# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto V
Grade: B
Buonconte's death and La Pia are superb; one misread line (Giovanna) and one knotted line need fixing.

## Issues
- `seg-0030` · fidelity · high
  > Giovanna, no one — no one cares for me;
  Reads as Buonconte addressing Giovanna; the source says neither Giovanna (his widow) nor anyone else cares for him (Longfellow: "Giovanna, nor none other cares for me").
  Fix: Neither Giovanna nor anyone else cares for me;

- `seg-0022` · read-aloud · medium
  > as long as can't doesn't cut off will.
  The bare "can't" and "will" read as verbs, so the line trips; they are quoted words.
  Fix: so long as "I cannot" does not cut off "I will."

## Best lines
> Siena made me, Maremma unmade me —
> and there watched / a lake spread on the ground out of my veins.
