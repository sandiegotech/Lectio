# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto X
Grade: A
Farinata and Cavalcante are sharply drawn; "You said held — held?" carries Dante's "ebbe" exactly.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · low
  > through the city of fire, speaking so plainly,
  "Parlando così onesto" is speaking so courteously or decorously (Longfellow: "speaking thus modestly"), not plainly; Farinata is praising Dante's manners.
  Fix: through the city of fire, speaking so courteously,

- `seg-0007` · fidelity · low
  > and you yourself have inclined me to that."
  Drops "non pur mo", not only now: Virgil has trained him to brevity before (Inf. III).
  Fix: and you have taught me that, and not just now."

## Best lines
- "as if he held all Hell in great contempt."
- "What? You said held — held? Is he not still alive?"
