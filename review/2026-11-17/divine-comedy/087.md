# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XX
Grade: A
The river-murmur rising through the Eagle's neck and the "Now he knows" litany are beautifully done; polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > "now you must hold your gaze on, hard,
  The stranded preposition trips the line.
  Fix: "is what you must now look at steadily,"
- `seg-0050` · form · low
  > moving their small flames / to the words.
  The canto's single closing line has been broken into two short lines.
  Fix: "moving their small flames in time to the words."
- `seg-0048` · read-aloud · low
  > so the song takes on more delight —
  "so" collides with the "so" that opens the next tercet's main clause; source: whereby the song gains pleasure.
  Fix: "and the song takes on more delight from it —"

## Best lines
- "songs that slip and fall from my memory."
- "but it conquers because it wills to be conquered, / and, conquered, conquers by its own kindness."
