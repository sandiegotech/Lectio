# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XX
Grade: B
Hugh Capet's indictment of his line is fierce and the triple "amends" lands; one clause misreads what spurs the souls.

## Issues
- `seg-0040` · fidelity · medium
  > as the feeling that spurs our going moves us / now to a quicker, now to a slower pace.
  The feeling spurs them to speak (ch'a dir ci sprona), and the pace is the pace of speaking; "our going" makes it about walking, though these souls lie prone.
  Fix: as the feeling that spurs us to speak / drives us now to a quicker, now a slower pace.

## Best lines
> Last of all the cry goes up: "Crassus, / tell us — you know — what does gold taste like?"
> and I see him killed between living thieves.
