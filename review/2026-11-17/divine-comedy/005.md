# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto V
Grade: A
Francesca's speech is clean and moving; the anaphora on Love and the famous lines all survive.

## Issues
- `seg-0020` · fidelity · low
  > that she followed Ninus and was his wife;
  "Succedette a Nino" is succeeded him on the throne (Longfellow: "succeeded Ninus"); "followed" can be read as trailing after him.
  Fix: that she succeeded Ninus and was his wife;

- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > drives the spirits on before its force, / wheeling and battering, it gives them no peace.
  Comma splice; the participles float between two main clauses.
  Fix: drives the spirits on before its force, / and wheeling them and battering, gives them no peace.

## Best lines
- "Love, which absolves no loved one from loving,"
- "That day we read in it no further."
