# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto IV
Grade: B
Limbo reads with real dignity and the roll call stays clean; one tangled tercet and one dangling appositive.

## Issues
- `seg-0026` · read-aloud · medium
  > "The honored name of them / that still rings of them up in your life
  "Of them … of them" tangles the line and loses the stress; Dante: "L'onrata nominanza / che di lor suona sù ne la tua vita" (Longfellow: "The honourable name, / That sounds of them above there in thy life").
  Fix: And he to me: "The honor of their name, / which still resounds up there in your life, / wins grace in Heaven, and Heaven raises them like this."

- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > it isn't enough, because they were not baptized, / the gateway into the faith you hold.
  "The gateway" hangs off "baptized" with nothing to attach to; Dante names baptism as the gate.
  Fix: it isn't enough, because they had no baptism, / which is the gateway to the faith you hold.

## Best lines
- "so that I was the sixth among such minds."
- "and I come to a place where nothing shines."
