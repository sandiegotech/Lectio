# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXIV
Grade: A
Peter's examination on Faith reads like a real viva, crisp and good-humored, and the creed keeps its weight; three clauses want smoothing.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > to whom our Lord gave the keys, / which he carried down, of this miraculous joy,
  The interrupting clause splits "keys … of this joy"; "he" is also ambiguous between Christ and Peter (source: Christ carried them down).
  Fix: "to whom our Lord left the keys / of this miraculous joy, which He brought down to earth,"
- `seg-0030` · read-aloud · low
  > Then from the depth of light that there / shone forth came:
  "shone forth came" stacks two verbs.
  Fix: "Then out of the deep light shining there / came the words: 'This precious jewel,"
- `seg-0041` · register · low
  > and whence your belief was offered to you.
  "whence" is leftover archaism.
  Fix: "and where your belief came to you from."

## Best lines
- "but tell me — do you have it in your purse?" / And I: "Yes — so bright and so round / that in its minting I have no doubt at all.""
- "that they bear both 'are' and 'is' together."
