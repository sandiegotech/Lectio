# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto VI
Grade: B
The invective against Italy has real heat and the irony toward Florence lands; one knotted tercet.

## Issues
- `seg-0044` · read-aloud · medium
  > Many carry justice in their hearts, but the shot / comes slow, so it won't leave the bow unconsidered;
  The archery image is tangled ("the shot comes slow ... it won't leave the bow"); the reader has to reparse it.
  Fix: Many hold justice in their hearts, but loose it slowly, / so it won't leave the bow without thought; / your people keep it always on their lips.

## Best lines
> Slave Italy, hostel of grief, / ship without a pilot in a great storm, / no lady of provinces — a brothel!
> rulings so fine that what you spin in October / doesn't last to the middle of November.
