# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto II
Grade: A
Close, clear and sometimes lovely; the flowers simile and the closing line land.

## Issues
- `seg-0033` · form · low
  > She called Lucia and made her plea: / 'Your faithful one is in need of you now,
  Mary's words sit inside Beatrice's speech, which sits inside Virgil's; they reuse single quotes, so the reader cannot tell where Beatrice's quotation resumes.
  Fix: use double quotes for the third level: She called Lucia and made her plea: / "Your faithful one is in need of you now, / and I commend him to your care."
