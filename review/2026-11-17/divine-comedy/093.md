# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXVI
Grade: B
The examination on Love and Adam's account of language are clear and moving; the mirror image in Adam's speech is turned around.

## Issues
- `seg-0036` · fidelity · medium
  > that makes all things its own reflection, / while nothing makes that mirror reflect itself.
  Source: the true mirror makes itself the reflection of all things, and nothing can make Him its reflection (God contains every thing; no thing can contain God). The Lectio inverts both halves.
  Fix: "for I see it in the truthful mirror / that makes itself the image of all things, / while nothing can make itself its image."
