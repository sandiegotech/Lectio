# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXXIII
Grade: A
Beatrice's prophecy and the close at Eunoë are faithful and the last line rings true; minor polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0023` · register · low
  > the stone-stilling water of Elsa around your mind,
  "Stone-stilling" is an opaque coinage; the Elsa's waters are known for coating things in stone.
  Fix: the petrifying water of Elsa around your mind,

- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > "Modicum, et non videbitis me;
  Christ's words are left in Latin with no help; the point (a little while and you will not see me, and again a little while and you will see me) is lost on most readers. Dante does keep the Latin, so a note may serve better than rewriting.
  Fix: Keep the Latin and add the English in the enrichment notes, or render: "A little while, and you will not see me; / and again, my beloved sisters, / a little while, and you will see me."

- `seg-0048` · form · low
  > remade — the way new plants are renewed / with new leaves — / pure and ready to rise to the stars.
  The last tercet and the closing line are merged into one four-line segment, with a two-word half-line in the middle.
  Fix: From those most holy waters I came back / remade, the way young plants are made new / with new leaves, (new segment:) pure and ready to rise to the stars.

## Best lines
> pure and ready to rise to the stars.
> the life that is a running toward death.
