# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXIII
Grade: B
The mother-bird opening, the leaping poem, and the crowning of Mary are all beautifully carried; one line turns Mary's relation to her Son around.

## Issues
- `seg-0040` · fidelity · medium
  > close behind the One it sprang from.
  Source "sua semenza": her offspring, Christ, who rose ahead of her; the Lectio makes Mary spring from him.
  Fix: "as it climbed upward after her own Son."
- `seg-0030` · fidelity · low
  > fixed it utterly on the greatest fire.
  Source: "the greater fire," i.e. the greatest of the lights left in view (Mary), Christ having risen out of sight; "greatest" suggests Christ.
  Fix: "and fixed it utterly on the greater fire."

## Best lines
- "the sacred poem has no choice but to leap, / like a man who finds the road has given out."
- "This is no crossing for a little boat, / the water my bold prow goes cutting through, / nor for a pilot who would spare himself."
