# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XX
Grade: A
The diviners' twisted bodies and Virgil's founding of Mantua read clearly; a few small word choices.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > of the first song, the one about the drowned.
  "D'i sommersi" is those sunk below, the damned in general (Longfellow: "the submerged"); "the drowned" suggests people who died in water.
  Fix: of the first song, the song of those sunk below.

- `seg-0044` · register · low
  > He said this to me, and we walked on the while.
  "The while" is leftover archaism.
  Fix: He said this to me, and all the while we walked on.

- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > the face was twisted back toward the kidneys,
  "Le reni" is the loins or small of the back; "kidneys" is anatomical and odd here.
  Fix: the face was twisted round toward the back,

## Best lines
- "Here pity lives when it is wholly dead."
- "that anything else would be burnt-out coals."
