# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXXIV
Grade: B
Lucifer, the climb through the center and the last line are strong; the opening tercet carries a translation of the Latin into the verse, and the ending tercets lose their shape.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · medium
  > "Vexilla regis prodeunt inferni — / the banners of the king of Hell advance / toward us.
  The English line is a translation of the Latin added into the poem, a gloss that turns the tercet into four lines; Dante has only the Latin, then "verso di noi" (Longfellow: "Vexilla Regis prodeunt Inferni / Towards us").
  Fix: "Vexilla regis prodeunt inferni / toward us. So look ahead," my Master said, / "and see if you can make him out."

- `seg-0043` · form · low
  > and rushed back, leaving the hollow behind it." / There is a place down there, as far from Beelzebub
  From `seg-0041` on, the English runs a line behind Dante's tercets, so Virgil's speech ends in the middle of a segment and the narration starts in the same one.
  Fix: re-break seg-0041 to seg-0044 on Dante's tercets (Inf. XXXIV 121–123, 124–126, 127–129, 130–132), closing Virgil's speech at the end of seg-0042.

- `seg-0044` · read-aloud · low
  > through the rock it has eaten away with its winding, gently-falling course.
  At nineteen syllables the line is twice the length of its neighbors and loses its stress.
  Fix: through the rock it has worn away / in its winding course, and it falls gently.
