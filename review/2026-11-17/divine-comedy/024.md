# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXIV
Grade: B
The frost simile, the climb and Vanni Fucci's prophecy are all strong; one phrase misparses aloud.

## Issues
- `seg-0016` · read-aloud · medium
  > No one wins fame / lying on down, or tucked under a quilt —
  Heard aloud, "lying on down" becomes "lying down"; Dante: "seggendo in piuma," sitting on feathers (Longfellow: "sitting upon down").
  Fix: No one wins fame / sitting on feather cushions, or tucked under a quilt —

## Best lines
- "as smoke leaves in the air, or foam on water."
- "And I've told you this to make you grieve."
