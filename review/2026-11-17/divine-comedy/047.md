# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XIII
Grade: A
The envious with sewn eyes and Sapìa's confession are rendered with real pity and bite; minor polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0034` · voice · low
  > a shade that waited, / looking the part
  "Looking the part" is an idiom for seeming suitable; Dante means the shade's look was one of waiting.
  Fix: a shade whose face / seemed to be waiting

- `seg-0051` · form · low
  > but it's the admirals who'll lose the most."
  The canto's closing single line is folded into the last tercet's segment, unlike the other cantos (and Longfellow, which keeps it separate), so segment counts diverge at the end.
  Fix: Split the last line into its own segment.
