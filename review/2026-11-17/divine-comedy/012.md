# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XII
Grade: B
The Minotaur and the Centaurs are lively and exact; one garbled line in Virgil's answer and one small added gloss.

## Issues
- `seg-0029` · read-aloud · medium
  > answered: "Yes, he's alive, and alive alone / it's my task to show him the dark valley.
  "Alive alone" makes no sense; "sì soletto" means Dante is alone and so Virgil must guide him (Longfellow: "and thus alone / Me it behoves to show him the dark valley").
  Fix: answered: "Yes, he's alive; and since he is so alone, / it falls to me to show him the dark valley.

- `seg-0007` · fidelity · low
  > schooled by your sister and her thread.
  "And her thread" is added; Dante says only "ammaestrato da la tua sorella." A gloss slipped into the line.
  Fix: schooled by your sister.
