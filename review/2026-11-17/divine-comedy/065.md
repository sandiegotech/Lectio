# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXXI
Grade: A
Dante's confession, the immersion in Lethe and the unveiling are faithful, sharp and moving ("lift up your beard" keeps its venom); polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · read-aloud · low
  > that the voice moved, and yet went out / before my mouth could set it free.
  "Went out" reads first as "came out", the opposite of the sense (it died before it was spoken).
  Fix: that the voice moved, and yet died / before my mouth could set it free.

- `seg-0028` · fidelity · low
  > farther than she had outshone all others here.
  The source says "than she outshone the others when she was here", meaning on earth; "here" now points to Eden.
  Fix: farther than she outshone all others when she lived on earth.

## Best lines
> here in our court the wheel turns back against the blade.
> standing above me, saying, "Hold me, hold me."
