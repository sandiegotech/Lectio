# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XIX
Grade: B
Nicholas III and the tirade against the simonist popes have full force; one misleading literalism and two awkward lines.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · medium
  > We had already climbed onto the next tomb,
  Dante's "tomba" here means the next ditch, the third bolgia; in English "tomb" sends the reader back to the heretics' sepulchres of Canto IX and X.
  Fix: We had already climbed, above the next ditch, / out to the part of the ridge that hangs

- `seg-0025` · read-aloud · low
  > Beneath my head are dragged the others down,
  Inverted, half-archaic order that trips aloud.
  Fix: Beneath my head the others are dragged down,

- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > And I: "Whatever you want is good with me.
  "Is good with me" is current small talk; "Tanto m'è bel, quanto a te piace" (Longfellow: "What pleases thee, to me is pleasing").
  Fix: And I: "Whatever pleases you pleases me.

## Best lines
- "I pocketed wealth, and down here, myself."
- "How are you any different from the idol-worshipper, / except he prays to one, and you to a hundred?"
