# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XVI
Grade: A
The three Florentines wheel and plead convincingly; the waterfall and the rising of Geryon are well built. Two casual phrases.

## Issues
- `seg-0027` · register · low
  > they all replied, "lucky you, free to speak your mind.
  "Lucky you" is chatty and a shade sarcastic; "felice te" is a blessing (Longfellow: "Happy art thou").
  Fix: they all replied, "happy are you, who can speak your mind like that.

- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > God, the wounds I saw on their bodies —
  "God," as an interjection is modern and casual for "Ahimè"; the same tic returns at `seg-0040` ("God, how careful a man should be") for "Ahi quanto."
  Fix: Ah, the wounds I saw on their bodies — / and at seg-0040: Ah, how careful a man should be

## Best lines
- "To a truth that wears the face of a lie / a man should keep his lips shut as long as he can,"
- "You could not have said one Amen / as fast as they had vanished;"
