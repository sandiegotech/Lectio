# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXIII
Grade: B
Forese's meeting is tender and his tirade against the women of Florence keeps its bite; one line changes the sense.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > and the smell of water — with no way to reach either?
  The source ends "non sappiendo como": who would believe the scent could waste them so, if he did not know the cause. The edition adds the idea that they cannot reach the food, which the source does not say here.
  Fix: and the smell of water — not knowing how?

> climbing and circling round and round the mountain / that straightens you, whom the world bent crooked.
