# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XVII
Grade: B
Geryon and the flight down are vivid and precise; one clause gives Virgil a threat he never makes, and two words are off register.

## Issues
- `seg-0030` · fidelity · medium
  > But his threat shamed me into courage,
  In Dante it is shame that threatens ("ma vergogna mi fé le sue minacce, / che innanzi a buon segnor fa servo forte"); Virgil has made no threat, only an encouragement.
  Fix: But shame made its threats to me, / the shame that makes a servant brave before a good master.

- `seg-0043` · register · low
  > makes the falconer groan, "Damn it, you're dropping" —
  "Damn it" is a modern oath for "Omè, tu cali!" (Longfellow: "Ah me, thou stoopest").
  Fix: makes the falconer groan, "Oh no, you're coming down!" —

- `seg-0001` · register · low
  > "Behold the beast with the pointed tail,
  "Behold," twice in the tercet, is leftover archaism for "Ecco."
  Fix: "Look — the beast with the pointed tail, … / look at the one whose stench fills the whole world!"

## Best lines
- "Its face was the face of an honest man,"
- "is the wind in my face and the wind from below."
