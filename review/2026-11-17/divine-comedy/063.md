# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXIX
Grade: B
The pageant unfolds with clarity and wonder; one tangled tercet in the chariot comparison, and the opening psalm is left in bare Latin.

## Issues
- `seg-0039` · read-aloud · medium
  > No splendid car like this ever gladdened / Africanus, or Augustus — not even Rome's;
  "Not even Rome's" dangles: it reads as "not even Rome's car". The source says Rome never gladdened Africanus or Augustus with so fine a car.
  Fix: Rome never gladdened Africanus, / or even Augustus, with a car so splendid; / beside it, the Sun's own chariot would be poor,

- `seg-0001` · register · low
  > "Beati quorum tecta sunt peccata."
  Bare Latin; the reader loses the psalm's point (blessed are those whose sins are covered), which prepares the confession to come. The edition renders most such lines in English.
  Fix: "Blessed are they whose sins are covered."
