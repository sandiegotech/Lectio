# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto V
Grade: B
Faithful and well-paced through the vow doctrine and the entry into Mercury; a few word choices need tightening.

## Issues
- `seg-0016` · read-aloud · medium
  > and on this point / I have already spoken with that precision.
  "with that precision" points at nothing; source: on this point it was stated precisely above.
  Fix: "unless it is carried through; and on this point / I spoke precisely a moment ago."
- `seg-0025` · register · low
  > be graver than this in your moves.
  "moves" reads as game or slang; source is "movements," i.e. your impulses and undertakings.
  Fix: "Christians, be slower and graver in what you set out to do."
- `seg-0039` · voice · low
  > before your campaign of war is laid down
  "campaign of war" is redundant; source "before the warfare is abandoned."
  Fix: "before your warfare here is done —"
- `seg-0001` · form · low
  > “If I blaze upon you
  Curly quotation marks here, straight quotes in Cantos I–IV; the edition should use one style throughout.
  Fix: normalize quotation marks across the work.

## Best lines
- "so, in a greater rapture, the holy figure / hid itself from me within its own radiance; / and so, wrapped close, close in its light, it answered me"
- "and like an arrow that strikes the mark / before the bowstring has gone quiet,"
