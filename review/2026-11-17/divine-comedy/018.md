# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XVIII
Grade: A
Malebolge is mapped clearly and the panders and flatterers keep Dante's coarseness without overplaying it.

## Issues
- `seg-0044` · register · low
  > of that filthy, disheveled slut down there,
  "Fante" is a serving-wench, a low but neutral word; Dante saves "puttana" for the next tercet, and "slut" spends it early.
  Fix: of that filthy, disheveled woman down there,

- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > God, how they made them lift their heels
  The interjection "God," again for "Ahi" (see Canto XVI); it is becoming a tic.
  Fix: Ah, how they made them lift their heels
