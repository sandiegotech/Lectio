# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XVII
Grade: B
The exile prophecy keeps its famous lines intact and hits hard; a reversed clause, a broken relative, and a merged final line need fixing.

## Issues
- `seg-0018` · fidelity · medium
  > but the truth that deals out vengeance will bear witness.
  Source: the vengeance will bear witness to the truth that dispenses it; the Lectio swaps subject and object.
  Fix: "but the vengeance will bear witness / to the truth that deals it out."
- `seg-0021` · read-aloud · medium
  > the vile and witless company / you are thrown among into this valley;
  "among into" is ungrammatical.
  Fix: "will be the vile and witless company / you will fall with into this valley;"
- `seg-0047` · form · medium
  > nor on any other proof that does not show."
  The canto's single closing line has been folded into the last tercet, making a four-line stanza and 47 segments against the source's 48; every other canto keeps the closing line on its own.
  Fix: split "nor on any other proof that does not show." into its own segment.
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > no triangle can hold two right angles wide,
  Source says two obtuse angles.
  Fix: "no triangle can hold two obtuse angles,"
- `seg-0042` · voice · low
  > will, yes, find your words harsh.
  "yes" is filler.
  Fix: "will surely find your words harsh."

## Best lines
- "You shall learn how salt is the taste / of another man's bread, and how hard the path / going down and climbing up another man's stairs."
- "and let them scratch wherever it may itch."
