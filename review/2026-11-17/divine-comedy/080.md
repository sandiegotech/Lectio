# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XIII
Grade: A
Hard scholastic matter made speakable, and the closing warnings against rash judgment land with proverb force; only polish needed.

## Issues
- `seg-0019` · fidelity · low
  > nor from the Love bound into them,
  Source: the Love "that makes a third with them" (in-threes itself); "bound into them" loses the Trinity the line names.
  Fix: "from Him, nor from the Love that makes the Three,"
- `seg-0020` · fidelity · low
  > draws its own rays back through its goodness
  Source "aduna": gathers or focuses its rays into the nine orders; "draws back" suggests withdrawal.
  Fix: "of its own goodness gathers its rays / into nine subsistences, as in a mirror,"
- `seg-0042` · register · low
  > and all the rest who set out and knew not where;
  "knew not" is leftover archaism.
  Fix: "and all the rest who set out not knowing where;"

## Best lines
- "whose hand trembles though his craft is sure."
- "for I have seen the thornbush all winter / show itself stiff and savage to the touch, / and then carry the rose on its crown,"
