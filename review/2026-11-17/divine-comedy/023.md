# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXIII
Grade: B
The escape down the bank and the hypocrites under their gilded lead are strong; one image is lost and one tercet repeats itself.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · medium
  > And he: "If I were a pane of leaded glass
  "Piombato vetro" is glass backed with lead, a mirror; a modern reader pictures a stained-glass window, and the image of reflection is lost.
  Fix: And he: "If I were a mirror

- `seg-0005` · read-aloud · low
  > because of us — fooled, hurt, made fools of —
  "Fooled … made fools of" says the same thing twice; Dante: "con danno e con beffa," with injury and mockery.
  Fix: because of us, with injury and with scorn so great

- `seg-0014` · register · low
  > not even pausing to throw on more than a shift;
  "Shift" for an undergarment is no longer common usage.
  Fix: not even stopping to throw on more than a nightgown;

## Best lines
- "like his own son, not a companion."
- "and one was that he lies, and fathers all lies."
