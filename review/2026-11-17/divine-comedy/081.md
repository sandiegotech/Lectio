# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XIV
Grade: B
Solomon's answer on the resurrected body is luminous and the cross of Mars arrives well, but two lines invert their sense and one simile produces an unfortunate misreading aloud.

## Issues
- `seg-0027` · fidelity · medium
  > and she must be left out / from among the sights my memory could not keep.
  "left out from among" excludes her from those sights; source: she was so beautiful he must leave her among the sights his memory could not follow.
  Fix: "appeared to me that I must leave her / among the sights my memory could not keep."
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > is as much grace as it has earned beyond its worth.
  "earned" contradicts "beyond its worth"; source: the vision is as great as the grace it has beyond its merit.
  Fix: "the ardor the measure of our seeing, and the seeing / as great as the grace it is given beyond its merit."
- `seg-0039` · read-aloud · medium
  > drift across the sunbeam that streaks the dark / people rig for their own shade
  Across the line break "the dark / people" reads aloud as "the dark people"; the clause is also hard to follow.
  Fix: "drift across a sunbeam slanting through the shade / that people rig against the glare / with all their craft and art."
- `seg-0028` · fidelity · low
  > borne up to higher rescue
  "salute" here is blessedness, not rescue.
  Fix: "borne up to a higher blessedness, my Lady alone with me."
- `seg-0029` · register · low
  > more red than it was wont.
  Leftover archaism.
  Fix: "which seemed to me redder than before."
- `seg-0035` · read-aloud · low
  > for on that cross flashed Christ, like lightning gone,
  "like lightning gone" is unclear; source: Christ flashed out on the cross like lightning.
  Fix: "for on that cross Christ flashed out like lightning,"

## Best lines
- "That One and Two and Three who lives forever / and reigns forever in Three and Two and One,"
- "how much they longed for their own dead bodies — / and not for themselves alone, perhaps, but for / the mothers, for the fathers,"
