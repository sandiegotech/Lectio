# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto II
Grade: A
The long lecture on the moon spots stays lucid and spoken; the opening address to the little boats is superb.

## Issues
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · low
  > on this body, the Moon, that make people back on earth
  "the Moon" is a gloss slipped into the sentence; Dante says only "this body."
  Fix: "on this body, that make people back on earth"
- `seg-0007` · register · low
  > for that God-shaped kingdom
  "God-shaped" carries a modern devotional-catchphrase echo; source is "the deiform realm," the realm formed like God.
  Fix: "for the kingdom made in the likeness of God"

## Best lines
- "Don't push out onto the open sea: lose me, / and you may find yourselves lost too."
- "how reason, / when it trails the senses, flies on short wings."
