# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXVI
Grade: A
Ulysses' speech and last voyage are the high point of the edition so far: plain, grave, and exact.

## Issues
- `seg-0040` · fidelity · low
  > to live like animals, dumb and grazing,
  "Dumb and grazing" is added to one of the most quoted lines in the poem ("fatti non foste a viver come bruti"); the padding blunts it.
  Fix: Consider where you came from. You were not made / to live like beasts, / but to follow after virtue and knowledge.'

## Best lines
- "to this last little watch our senses keep, / to the small waking time we have left,"
- "until the sea had closed over us again."
