# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXXII
Grade: A
The tree's renewal and the allegory of the chariot (eagle, fox, dragon, harlot and giant) are clear and vivid; small fixes only.

## Issues
- `seg-0050` · fidelity · low
  > Sure of itself as a fortress on a high peak,
  In the source it is the harlot who sits secure ("sicura", feminine); "itself" pins the simile on the chariot.
  Fix: Secure as a fortress on a high peak,

- `seg-0002` · voice · low
  > a wall of not-caring
  The hyphenated coinage is awkward aloud; plain English has the word.
  Fix: a wall of indifference

- `seg-0054` · form · low
  > made a shield, hiding from me the harlot / and the strange new beast.
  The canto's single closing line is broken across two lines.
  Fix: made a shield from me for the harlot and the strange new beast. (one line)

## Best lines
