# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto I
Grade: B
Faithful, plain and well paced; the opening lines survive intact. A few small slips of syntax and word choice.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · medium
  > that wood, so wild and rough and unrelenting / the fear comes back the moment I think of it.
  The relative "that" is missing, so the line reads as a run-on ("unrelenting the fear"); Dante: "che nel pensier rinova la paura" (Longfellow: "Which in the very thought renews the fear").
  Fix: that wood, so wild and rough and unrelenting / that the fear comes back the moment I think of it.

- `seg-0036` · fidelity · low
  > He'll be the salvation of that low Italy
  "Umile Italia" (Virgil's humilemque Italiam) is humble or low-lying Italy; "low" alone reads as a value judgment.
  Fix: He'll be the salvation of that humble Italy

- `seg-0021` · fidelity · low
  > who seemed faint, hoarse from long silence.
  "Fioco" is one word; giving both "faint" and "hoarse" is a gloss doubled into the line.
  Fix: who seemed faint from long silence.

- `seg-0029` · fidelity · low
  > the noble style that has earned me my name.
  "Lo bello stilo" is the beautiful style (Longfellow: "the beautiful style").
  Fix: the beautiful style that has earned me my name.
