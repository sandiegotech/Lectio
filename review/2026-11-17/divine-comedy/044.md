# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto X
Grade: B
The carved examples are vivid and the address to proud Christians is strong; a softened "vengeance", a muted famous phrase, and two register slips.

## Issues
- `seg-0028` · fidelity · medium
  > "Lord, give me justice / for my dead son.
  The widow asks for vengeance (fammi vendetta); "justice" softens her cry and blurs Trajan's reply about justice in seg-0031.
  Fix: "Lord, avenge me / for my dead son. My heart is breaking."

- `seg-0032` · fidelity · medium
  > made this speech you could see —
  Dante's "visibile parlare" is a phrase readers know; the paraphrase loses it.
  Fix: made this visible speech —

- `seg-0012` · register · low
  > the peace that broke Heaven's long embargo,
  "Embargo" is a trade-policy word; the source is a long ban or interdict.
  Fix: the peace that opened Heaven after its long ban,

- `seg-0031` · register · low
  > if you let your own slide?"
  "Let slide" is too casual for the widow's rebuke.
  Fix: if you neglect your own?"

- `seg-0019` · read-aloud · low
  > why men dread an office not given them to hold.
  Reads as a stray fragment after the ark; the link (Uzzah's death) needs a "for which".
  Fix: for which men fear an office not given to them.

## Best lines
> don't you see that we are worms, / born to become the angelic butterfly / that flies toward justice with nothing to shield it?
> seemed, through his weeping, to be saying: "I can't go on."
