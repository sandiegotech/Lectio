# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XII
Grade: B
Bonaventure's life of Dominic has energy and the double-rainbow opening is fine; a few phrases misfire on sense or on a word's modern meaning.

## Issues
- `seg-0038` · fidelity · medium
  > But the track laid down by the rim of that wheel, / at its very best, is abandoned now:
  "at its very best" has no source; Dante means the track cut by the topmost part of the rim (the founder's path) is now deserted.
  Fix: "But the rut the top of that wheel's rim cut / is abandoned now:"
- `seg-0043` · register · medium
  > always set the sinister concerns aside.
  "sinister" here is the old sense of left-hand, worldly; a reader today hears "evil," which makes the boast meaningless.
  Fix: "always put worldly concerns last."
- `seg-0027` · fidelity · low
  > O his father, Felix — "happy" indeed! / O his mother, Joanna — "grace of God" —
  The meanings of the names are glosses inserted into the line, and the nested double quotes clash with the speech's own quotation marks.
  Fix: "O his father, truly Felix! / O his mother, truly Joanna, / if the names mean what they're said to mean!"
- `seg-0028` · fidelity · low
  > following Ostian law and Taddeo's medicine,
  "Ostiense" is a man (the canonist of Ostia), parallel to Taddeo, not a kind of law.
  Fix: "following the Ostian's law books and Taddeo's,"
- `seg-0045` · fidelity · low
  > and Peter the Eater,
  The proper name is Peter Comestor (Longfellow: Mangiador); translating the epithet makes him sound comic.
  Fix: "and Peter Comestor, and Peter of Spain,"

## Best lines
- "where the wine-crust was, there is mold."
- "a page / that reads, 'I am what I always was.'"
