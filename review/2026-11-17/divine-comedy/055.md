# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXI
Grade: A
Statius's arrival and the comedy of the suppressed smile are handled with real tact; two small fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0033` · register · low
  > my work would not have weighed a single gram.
  "Gram" is metric and modern-technical; Dante's unit is the dram (dramma).
  Fix: my work would not have weighed a single dram.

- `seg-0024` · read-aloud · low
  > praised the Lord — may he speed them up soon."
  "Speed them up" reads as "make them go faster"; the sense is send them upward.
  Fix: praised the Lord — may he soon send them upward."
