# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XII
Grade: A
The carved pavement keeps Dante's anaphora ("I saw" / "O" / "It showed", gathered in the Troy tercet) and the canto moves well; two small polish points.

## Issues
- `seg-0017` · read-aloud · low
  > made the fatal necklace cost his mother / so dearly for what she wore.
  "For what she wore" repeats the necklace and makes the end of the tercet limp.
  Fix: made his mother pay so dearly / for the ill-starred necklace she wore.

- `seg-0024` · voice · low
  > long enough to see how lost your road has gone!
  "How lost your road has gone" is vague; the source is "your evil road".
  Fix: long enough to see the evil road you're on!

## Best lines
> The dead looked dead, the living looked alive;
> O human creatures, born to fly upward, / why do you fall at the slightest breath of wind?
