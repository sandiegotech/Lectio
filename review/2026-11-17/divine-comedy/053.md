# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XIX
Grade: A
The Siren dream is unsettling as it should be and Pope Adrian's confession is plain and grave; polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0018` · register · low
  > "What is it with you, that you keep staring at the ground?"
  "What is it with you" is a casual modern idiom that jars in Virgil's mouth.
  Fix: "What troubles you, that you keep staring at the ground?"

- `seg-0020` · fidelity · low
  > the one for whom, above us, they still weep?
  The source says she alone is wept for on the terraces above from here on; "still weep" loses both points.
  Fix: the one for whom alone, above us, they now weep?
