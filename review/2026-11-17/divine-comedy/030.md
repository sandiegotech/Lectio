# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XXX
Grade: A
The falsifiers' slanging match between Master Adam and Sinon is quick and funny, and Virgil's rebuke lands; two small slips.

## Issues
- `seg-0014` · register · low
  > just as that one going yonder dared
  "Yonder" is leftover archaism.
  Fix: just as that one going off over there dared

- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > "Oh," I said to him, "so the other one / doesn't set its teeth in you — be so kind,
  Dante's "se l'altro non ti ficchi / li denti a dosso" is a wish ("so may the other not…"); "so the other one doesn't" reads first as a realization or a purpose clause.
  Fix: "Oh," I said to him, "as you hope the other / never sets its teeth in you, be so kind,
