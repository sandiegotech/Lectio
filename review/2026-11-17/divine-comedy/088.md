# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXI
Grade: B
Peter Damian's speech is spare and scalding, and the golden stair is well seen; one pronoun misleads and the closing line is broken badly.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · medium
  > how glad I was to obey / my guide from heaven — once he weighed
  "he" means the reader from seg-0007, but sitting after "my guide" it reads as the guide, and makes Beatrice male.
  Fix: "would know, weighing the one side against the other, / how glad I was to obey / my guide from heaven."
- `seg-0048` · form · low
  > could make nothing of it, the thunder so / overwhelmed me.
  The closing line is split mid-phrase ("so / overwhelmed").
  Fix: seg-0047 ends "that nothing here could match it; nor could I" and seg-0048 reads "make it out, the thunder so overwhelmed me."

## Best lines
- "The mind that shines clear here is smoke on earth."
- "so that two beasts go forward under one hide. / O Patience, that you bear so much!"
