# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXVII
Grade: A
Peter's invective blazes ("my place, my place, my place") and Beatrice's lecture on the Primum Mobile stays clear; two small fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0013` · read-aloud · low
  > that his face had not been changed by more.
  Inverted and hard to parse; source: his voice was so altered that his face itself had not changed more.
  Fix: "in a voice so altered from the one before / that his face itself had not changed more."
- `seg-0021` · register · low
  > will bring its rescue soon, the way I see it.
  "the way I see it" reads as casual opinion; source: as I foresee (Peter sees it in God).
  Fix: "will bring its rescue soon, as I foresee."

## Best lines
- "He who usurps my place on earth — my place, / my place, my place, which now stands empty / in the sight of the Son of God —"
- "One who still lisps will keep the holy fasts, / then, once his tongue is loosed, will gorge himself / on any food at all, under any moon."
