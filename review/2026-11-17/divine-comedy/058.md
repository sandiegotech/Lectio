# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XXIV
Grade: A
Bonagiunta and the "sweet new style" exchange are rendered exactly; the second tree and the angel of temperance are lovely. Polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0036` · voice · low
  > crying up toward the leaves I-don't-know-what,
  The hyphenated compound (again in seg-0040, "a voice I-don't-know-whose") is a mannerism that reads oddly aloud.
  Fix: crying I don't know what up toward the leaves, (and in seg-0040: So said a voice among the boughs, I don't know whose;)

- `seg-0051` · form · low
  > hungering always only as much as is just."
  The canto's closing line is folded into the last tercet's segment (four lines), unlike Longfellow, which keeps it separate; segment counts diverge at the end.
  Fix: Split the last line into its own segment.
