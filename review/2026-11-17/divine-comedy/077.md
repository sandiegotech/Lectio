# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto X
Grade: B
One of the strongest cantos so far: the address to the Reader and the clock simile are lovely. One line about the stair inverts its sense.

## Issues
- `seg-0029` · fidelity · medium
  > where no one comes back down / without first climbing it again
  Source: the stair "where none descends without reascending," i.e. whoever comes down will climb it again; "first" reverses the order.
  Fix: "leads you up the stair where no one comes down / who will not climb it again,"
- `seg-0036` · fidelity · low
  > was Peter — Peter the Lombard,
  "the Lombard" is a gloss; Dante gives only "that Peter," and the widow's-mite clue identifies him.
  Fix: "was that Peter who, like the poor widow, / gave his whole treasure to the Church."
- `seg-0015` · fidelity · low
  > I cannot tell, though I call on every gift
  Drops "so that it could be imagined": the point is that telling cannot make the reader picture it.
  Fix: "I could not tell so it could be imagined, / though I call on genius, art, and practice — but believe it, / and ache to see it for yourself."

## Best lines
- "and the brightness of her laughing eyes / broke my one mind across a scatter of things."
- "Whoever does not grow wings to fly up there / can wait for word of it from a man struck dumb."
