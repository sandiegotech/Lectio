# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto VII
Grade: B
Sordello's recognition and the valley of princes read cleanly; one misattached clause in the roll of kings.

## Issues
- `seg-0042` · fidelity · medium
  > Pedro, who sings with him — / for whom Provence and Apulia already grieve.
  "For whom" makes Peter the cause of grief; in the source Provence and Apulia grieve because of the large-nosed Charles's heirs ("onde ... già si dole").
  Fix: Pedro, who sings with him; / and so Provence and Apulia grieve already.

- `seg-0029` · fidelity · low
  > Before the little sun goes down to its nest
  "Il poco sole" is what little sun is left, not a small sun.
  Fix: Before what little sun is left goes to its nest

## Best lines
> Not by doing, but by not doing, I lost / the sight of that high Sun you long for, / the Sun I knew too late.
> And see the other, who has made a bed / of his own palm, sighing into his cheek.
