# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto II
Grade: B
Strong, vivid canto (the angel boatman, Casella) but a smuggled gloss throws the tercets off by a line for six segments and the last tercet is collapsed.

## Issues
- `seg-0016` · fidelity · medium
  > "In exitu Israel de Aegypto" — / When Israel went out of Egypt — all of them
  The English translation of the Latin is glossed into the verse; Dante leaves the Latin bare. The extra line pushes every tercet from seg-0016 to seg-0021 one line late, so segments split mid-sentence and no longer match Longfellow's seg-0016..0021.
  Fix: "In exitu Israel de Aegypto," / they all sang together in one voice, / with everything that psalm sets down after it. (then re-break 0017–0021 back on the tercets: cross and casting down / the crowd left strange to the place / day breaking, Capricorn chased / new people lift their faces / Virgil answers)

- `seg-0044` · form · medium
  > like people going who don't know where; nor was our own leaving any less in haste.
  Two verse lines run together; the canto's closing single line is lost (Longfellow has it as its own line/segment).
  Fix: like people going who don't know where; / (new line) nor was our own leaving any less in haste.

- `seg-0031` · fidelity · low
  > how were so many years taken from you?
  Source is "so much time" (tanta ora), asking about the delay, not "years".
  Fix: But you — how has so much time been taken from you?

- `seg-0012` · voice · low
  > fanning the air with feathers that never fall, / that do not molt away like mortal hair.
  "that never fall" and "do not molt" say the same thing twice; source is "eternal feathers".
  Fix: fanning the air with his eternal feathers / that do not molt like mortal hair.

- `seg-0025` · register · low
  > so all those lucky spirits
  "Lucky" is too casual for anime fortunate.
  Fix: so all those fortunate spirits
