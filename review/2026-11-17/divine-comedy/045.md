# The Divine Comedy · Purgatorio, Canto XI
Grade: B
The Paternoster and Oderisi's speech on fame are among the best passages so far; one line of the fame speech is garbled.

## Issues
- `seg-0031` · fidelity · medium
  > unless a coarse age follows and dims the rest.
  The source says the green lasts at the top only if a cruder age follows; "and dims the rest" is added and makes the line say something else.
  Fix: unless a cruder age comes after it.

- `seg-0046` · fidelity · low
  > he brought himself / to beg — to tremble in every vein.
  "To beg" is a gloss inserted into the line; the source has only the trembling.
  Fix: he brought himself / to tremble in every vein.

## Best lines
> Your fame is the color of grass: it comes, / it goes, and the same sun that draws it green / out of the earth is the one that burns it pale.
> Florence, as haughty / then as she is for sale today.
