# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXV
Grade: A
The opening hope of return to Florence is moving and the canto ends on a true shock; two lines are loose.

## Issues
- `seg-0030` · read-aloud · low
  > set the mark, and that shows it to me, / for all the souls God has made his friends.
  "that shows it to me" leaves both pronouns floating; source: the Scriptures set the sign, and the sign points to what is promised the souls God has made his friends.
  Fix: "set out the sign, and the sign points me / to what is promised all the souls God has made his friends."
- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > you stood for it.
  Unclear; source: you embody Hope as often as Jesus favored the three.
  Fix: "you were its very figure."

## Best lines
- "so that for years it has worn me thin —"
- "Earth in the earth my body is, and shall be / with all the others there, until our number / matches the eternal reckoning."
