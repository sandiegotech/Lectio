# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XV
Grade: B
Cacciaguida's old Florence is the best set piece in this stretch, plain and tender; one line pins the prophecy on the wrong hill.

## Issues
- `seg-0037` · fidelity · medium
  > Your Uccellatoio had not yet outdone / the hill of Montemalo — which, outdone / in its rising, will be outdone in its fall.
  The relative "which" now points to Montemalo (Rome); in the source it is Florence's Uccellatoio that, having outdone Rome in its rise, will outdo it in its fall.
  Fix: "Montemalo had not yet been outdone / by your Uccellatoio — which, as it outdid it / rising, will outdo it in its fall."
