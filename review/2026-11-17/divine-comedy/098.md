# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XXXI
Grade: A
The white Rose, the turn to find Bernard instead of Beatrice, and the farewell prayer are among the edition's best pages; small polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0047` · fidelity · low
  > fixed and intent upon his burning warmth,
  Read literally this is Bernard's own warmth; the sense is the one he burns for, Mary, and "turned his own there" then lacks its object.
  Fix: "fixed and intent on her he burned for, / turned his own eyes to her with such great love"
- `seg-0041` · voice · low
  > outblaze in splendor all the rest of the front.
  "front" is a stiff carry-over of Longfellow's "front"; the word reads oddly in plain English.
  Fix: "outblaze in splendor all the rest of the rim."

## Best lines
- "I meant to ask one thing; another answered."
- "So I prayed; and she, so far away, / looked down at me once more, and seemed to smile — / then turned again to the eternal fountain."
