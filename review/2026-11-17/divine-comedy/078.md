# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto XI
Grade: B
The life of Francis is told with warmth and pace; the main fix is that Thomas's quotation of his own earlier line no longer matches Canto X.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · medium
  > where I said just now, 'where the sheep grow fat,'
  Thomas is quoting himself from Canto X, where the Lectio reads "where a man / grows fat and sound, if only he does not stray." The callback (here and in seg-0047) no longer matches, so the reader cannot hear the echo the whole canto builds to.
  Fix: here, "'where a man grows fat'"; in seg-0047, "'where a man grows fat, if he does not stray.'" (or change Canto X to the sheep wording; the two must agree).
- `seg-0027` · register · low
  > first kicked his shoes off
  "Kicked off his shoes" suggests lounging; source: he bared his feet, as a friar does.
  Fix: "first went barefoot, and ran toward such peace,"
- `seg-0008` · register · low
  > you want me to unpack
  "Unpack" in the sense of "explain" is current talk-show idiom.
  Fix: "and you want me to sift again, / in words plain and wide enough"
- `seg-0018` · register · low
  > but Orient, if he means to name it right.
  "Orient" now reads as dated or loaded; the pun is on sunrise.
  Fix: "but Sunrise, if he means to name it right."

## Best lines
- "for the sake of a certain Lady to whom, / as to death itself, no one unbars the door of joy."
- "and running, thought himself too slow."
