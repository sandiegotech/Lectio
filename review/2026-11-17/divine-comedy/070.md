# The Divine Comedy · Paradiso: Canto III
Grade: B
Piccarda's speech is beautifully kept and the great line lands; one argument clause is garbled and the mirror simile tangles.

## Issues
- `seg-0026` · fidelity · medium
  > if it is the nature of love / to live here
  Source: "if to be in charity is necessary here"; the Lectio turns a condition of the place into a claim about love's nature.
  Fix: "within these circles, if to live in love / is necessary here — and if you look well into what love is."
- `seg-0005` · read-aloud · medium
  > comes to the eye / no harder to make out than that
  The comparison runs backward and has to be decoded; source: outlines so faint that a pearl on a white brow reaches the eye no less dimly.
  Fix: "the outlines coming back so faint / that a pearl on a white forehead / reaches the eye no less dimly —"
- `seg-0028` · form · low
  > who draws our will into his own.
  The tercet has been stretched to four lines; source is a three-line stanza.
  Fix: "it pleases the whole realm, as it pleases the King / who draws our will into his own." (fold the first two lines: "so that, however we are ranked from sphere to sphere / across this realm, it pleases all the realm, / as it pleases the King who draws our will to his.")

## Best lines
- "And in his will is our peace. It is the sea / toward which all things are moving"
- "to sing "Ave Maria" — and singing, faded, / like something heavy sinking through deep water."
