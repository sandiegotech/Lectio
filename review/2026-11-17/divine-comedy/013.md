# The Divine Comedy · Inferno: Canto XIII
Grade: B
Pier della Vigna's speech is superb; a misleading adjective, one doubled clause and one charged word to fix.

## Issues
- `seg-0039` · fidelity · medium
  > naked and clawed, fleeing so wildly
  "Graffiati" means scratched, torn by the thorns (Longfellow: "naked and scratched"); "clawed" reads as having claws, like the Harpies just described.
  Fix: naked and scratched, fleeing so wildly

- `seg-0032` · read-aloud · low
  > When the savage soul tears itself loose / from the body it ripped itself away from,
  The same act is said twice; Dante: "si parte … dal corpo ond' ella stessa s'è disvelta," the soul departs the body it uprooted itself from.
  Fix: When the savage soul departs / from the body it has torn itself away from,

- `seg-0022` · register · low
  > The whore who never takes her sluttish eyes
  "Sluttish" now reads as a modern insult rather than Dante's "occhi putti," whorish eyes.
  Fix: The whore who never takes her whorish eyes

## Best lines
- "I think he thought that I was thinking"
- "made me unjust against my own just self."
