## Chapter 74 · The Master Carpenter
Grade: A
Faithful and grim; one sentence switches person.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · read-aloud · low
  > But suppose you could make them always fear death, so that whenever someone did wrong I could seize him
  "You could make them ... I could seize" changes speaker mid-sentence; the text (若使民常畏死，而為奇者，吾得執而殺之) keeps "I."
  Fix: "But suppose the people always feared death, and whenever someone did wrong I could seize him and put him to death — who then would dare?"

### Best lines
> whoever hews in the master carpenter's place will hardly fail to cut his own hand.

## Chapter 75 · Why the People Go Hungry
Grade: B
The three-part indictment is strong; the closing maxim says nearly the opposite of the text.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · high
  > It is only the one who does not strain after living who truly knows how to value life.
  夫唯無以生為者，是賢於貴生: the one who does not strive for life is *wiser than* the one who prizes life. Prizing life is the fault being criticized; the edition makes it the reward. Legge: "to leave the subject of living altogether out of view is better than to set a high value on it."
  Fix: "Only the one who does not strain after living is wiser than the one who prizes life."

### Best lines
> The people make light of death because those above them grasp so hard at life.

## Chapter 79 · Holding the Left of the Tally
Grade: B
Faithful in sense, but an explanation is set into the sentence.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > keeps the left half of the tally, the creditor's record,
  "The creditor's record" is a gloss on 左契, correct but not in the text or in Legge; it belongs in a note.
  Fix: "And so the sage keeps the left half of the tally, and yet never presses his claim against anyone."

### Best lines
> The Way of heaven plays no favorites; it is always on the side of the good.

## Chapter 80 · A Small Country
Grade: A
Faithful and tender; one tense wobbles.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · read-aloud · low
  > Though they had tools that could do the work of ten or a hundred hands
  Past "had" here, present "have" in seg-0002 for the same construction (雖有).
  Fix: "Though they have tools that could do the work of ten or a hundred hands, let no one use them."

### Best lines
> The neighboring country is so close you can see it, and the crowing of its cocks and the barking of its dogs carry across to you — yet the people grow old and die without ever going back and forth.
