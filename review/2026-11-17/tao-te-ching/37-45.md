## Chapter 38 · Virtue and Its Decline
Grade: B
Faithful through the whole descent from Way to ritual, but the key term changes name here.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · voice · medium
  > The highest virtue is not virtuous, and that is why it has virtue.
  德 is "power" in chs. 10, 21, 23, 28, 41, 54, 59, 65 and "virtue" here (and in 51, 55, 63, 68); a reader cannot tell that 38's "virtue" is 21's "great power" or 28's "constant power." 玄德 is "mysterious power" in 10 and 65 but "mysterious virtue" in 51. Choose one word for 德 across the work. If "power" stays, this line needs it most.
  Fix: "The highest power does not cling to its power, and that is why it has power. The lowest power never lets go of it, and that is why it has none." (and "power" for "virtue" through seg-0004).
- `seg-0006` · read-aloud · low
  > He lets the one go and takes hold of the other.
  After "substance ... husk ... fruit ... flower," the ear cannot tell which is kept (去彼取此). Same phrase as ch. 12.
  Fix: "He lets that go and takes hold of this."

### Best lines
> Ritual is the thin husk of loyalty and good faith, and the beginning of disorder.

## Chapter 39 · Those That Attained the One
Grade: B
Faithful and well built, but the carriage line is given twice under two different readings.

### Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · medium
  > Take a carriage apart piece by piece, and there is no carriage left to find. The highest renown is to have no renown.
  致數輿無輿 is rendered once as "carriage" (Legge's reading) and again as 譽 "renown" (the emended reading); the text has one line, and the doubled version adds a sentence.
  Fix: keep one: "Take a carriage apart piece by piece, and there is no carriage left to find." (cut the renown sentence).

### Best lines
> So the noble takes the humble as its root, and the high takes the low as its foundation.

## Chapter 42 · The Way Bore the One
Grade: A
Faithful, including the yin/yang line Legge obscures; one term drifts from ch. 39.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · voice · low
  > orphaned, lonely, or unworthy
  孤寡不穀 is "orphaned, widowed, unworthy" in ch. 39; the same royal titles should read the same.
  Fix: "to be called orphaned, widowed, or unworthy"

### Best lines
> The Way bore the One; the One bore the Two; the Two bore the Three; and the Three bore the ten thousand things.

## Chapter 45 · Great Perfection Seems Flawed
Grade: A
Faithful; one clause chimes awkwardly.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · read-aloud · low
  > yet its use is never used up
  "Use ... used up" echoes clumsily (其用不窮).
  Fix: "Great fullness seems empty, yet it is never used up."

### Best lines
> Great straightness seems bent. Great skill seems clumsy. Great eloquence seems to stammer.
