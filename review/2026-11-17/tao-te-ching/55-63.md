# Tao Te Ching · Chapters 55–63
Edition: lectio-modern · compared against legge-1891 and the received Chinese text (no original bundled).

## Chapter 55 · Full of Virtue, Like a Newborn Child
Grade: A
Faithful and frank about the infant; much clearer than Legge's verse in seg-0003.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · voice · low
  > One full of virtue is like a newborn child.
  德 again as "virtue," where most chapters say "power" (see ch. 38); title too.
  Fix: follow whatever single word the edition settles on, e.g. "One full of power is like a newborn child."

### Best lines
> It can cry all day without growing hoarse — such is the perfection of its harmony.

## Chapter 56 · Those Who Know Do Not Speak
Grade: A
The famous first line is intact; one pronoun slips.

### Issues
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > Such a one cannot be drawn close ... And so they are honored by all the world.
  "Such a one" turns into "they" in the next sentence; the subject should hold.
  Fix: "And so such a one is honored by all the world."

### Best lines
> Those who know do not speak; those who speak do not know.

## Chapter 57 · Win the World by Not Meddling
Grade: A
Faithful, and the sage's four sayings keep their rhythm.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · voice · low
  > the more strange things start to happen
  "Start to" is filler (奇物滋起, "strange things multiply").
  Fix: "the more strange things happen."

### Best lines
> The more laws and decrees are posted up, the more thieves and robbers there are.

## Chapter 58 · Fortune and Misfortune
Grade: A
Faithful and better than Legge's verse; two word choices drift.

### Issues
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > the people grow crafty and broken
  缺缺 is "deficient, lacking"; "crafty" is added.
  Fix: "the people grow sly and wanting." or simply "the people grow broken."
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > straight but never severe
  直而不肆: "straight but not reckless" (Legge: "allows himself no license"); "severe" is a different fault.
  Fix: "straight but never overbearing,"

### Best lines
> Misfortune is what fortune leans on; fortune is where misfortune hides.

## Chapter 59 · Sparing
Grade: A
Faithful; one phrase doubles on itself.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > storing up an ever deeper store of power
  "Storing up ... store" repeats the word.
  Fix: "and yielding early means building up an ever deeper store of power."

### Best lines
> This is what it means to have deep roots and a firm base — the way of long life and lasting vision.

## Chapter 60 · Like Cooking a Small Fish
Grade: A
The famous opening is exact; the close adds a location.

### Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > the power of each flows back and gathers in the people
  故德交歸焉 says the powers converge and return; "in the people" is an added interpretation (Legge: "converge in the virtue (of the Tao)").
  Fix: "Since neither does harm, the power of each flows back and meets."

### Best lines
> Governing a great state is like cooking a small fish.
