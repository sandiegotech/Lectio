## Chapter 28 · Return to the Uncarved Block
Grade: A
Faithful to the received order of the three stanzas and far clearer than Legge's verse; only the title collides.

### Issues

## Chapter 30 · Against Force of Arms
Grade: A
Faithful and grave; one tense slips.

### Issues

## Chapter 32 · The Uncarved Block
Grade: B
Faithful until the closing simile, which is put the wrong way round.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > The Way is to the world what the rivers and streams are to the sea — everything flows down to it and home.
  As written the Way is the streams, yet "everything flows down to it"; 猶川谷之於江海 makes the Way the great river and sea the streams run into (Legge: "like that of the great rivers and seas to the streams from the valleys").
  Fix: "The Way is to the world what the sea is to the rivers and streams: everything flows down to it and home."

### Best lines
> and the people, with no one to command them, would fall into order by themselves.
