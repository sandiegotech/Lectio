# Tao Te Ching · Chapters 46–54
Edition: lectio-modern · compared against legge-1891 and the received Chinese text (no original bundled).

## Chapter 46 · Knowing You Have Enough
Grade: A
Faithful to the Wang Bi text; the horses image is vivid.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > There is no calamity greater than not knowing when you have enough
  The base has a third clause before this one, "no guilt greater than to sanction ambition" (罪莫大於可欲), which the edition omits. Wang Bi lacks it, so the omission is defensible, but it departs from the base without a note.
  Fix: if the base is followed, open with "There is no crime greater than having things to desire;"

### Best lines
> When the Way prevails in the world, the swift horses are sent back to haul dung for the fields.

## Chapter 50 · No Room for Death
Grade: A
Faithful and strong; one phrase is stiff.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > yet move the whole way toward the ground of death
  "The whole way toward the ground of" is padded and stiff; 動之死地 is simply "move into the place of death."
  Fix: "And three in ten are those who live, yet move straight into the place of death."

### Best lines
> Because in him there is no room for death.

## Chapter 51 · The Way Gives Life, Virtue Nurtures
Grade: B
Faithful and well paced, but its closing term contradicts ch. 10.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · voice · medium
  > This is what is called the mysterious virtue.
  The same sentence (是謂玄德) closes ch. 10 as "the mysterious power," and ch. 65 also has "mysterious power"; the reader is given two names for one thing. See the 德 note in ch. 38: choose one word through the work (title and seg-0001–0003 too).
  Fix: "This is what is called the mysterious power." (and "its power nurtures them" in seg-0001 and seg-0003).

### Best lines
> it rears them and raises them, it shelters them and ripens them, it feeds them and shields them.

## Chapter 52 · The Mother and Her Children
Grade: B
Faithful through the first half; the closing lines add glosses and misread one clause.

### Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · medium
  > and you will leave no harm behind you
  無遺身殃 is "bring no harm upon yourself" (遺 = bring on, 身 = your own person); "leave behind you" points outward and loses 身. Legge: "from his body ward all blight."
  Fix: "and you will bring no harm upon yourself."
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > Use the outer light, but turn back to the inner light
  "Outer" and "inner" are glosses; 用其光，復歸其明 contrasts light (光) with brightness/clarity (明), the word used in seg-0004.
  Fix: "Use the light, but return to clarity,"

### Best lines
> To see the small is clarity. To keep to the soft is strength.
