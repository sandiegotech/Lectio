# Tao Te Ching · Chapters 64–72
Edition: lectio-modern · compared against legge-1891 and the received Chinese text (no original bundled).

## Chapter 64 · A Journey of a Thousand Miles
Grade: A
Faithful and concrete; "learns to unlearn" is closer to the Chinese than Legge.

### Issues
(none)

### Best lines
> A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet.

## Chapter 65 · Ruling Without Cleverness
Grade: A
Faithful and plain; the last phrase is slightly free.

### Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > until they come round to the Great Harmony
  大順 is "great accord" or "great conformity" (Legge: "a great conformity"); "Harmony" brings in 和, a different word the edition uses elsewhere (ch. 42, 55).
  Fix: "until they come round to the great accord."

### Best lines
> So to rule a state by cleverness is to rob the state; to rule a state without cleverness is its blessing.

## Chapter 67 · The Three Treasures
Grade: A
Faithful, and the three treasures are clearly named.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > Compassion wins when it attacks
  以戰則勝 is "in battle it wins"; "attacks" sharpens it, against the defensive tenor of chs. 68–69.
  Fix: "Compassion wins when it fights and holds firm when it defends."

### Best lines
> Because I am compassionate, I can be brave; because I am restrained, I can be generous

## Chapter 68 · The Virtue of Not Contending
Grade: B
The four portraits are excellent; the closing pair swaps the edition's own terms.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · voice · medium
  > This is the virtue of not contending. This is the power that comes of using the strength of others.
  不爭之德 gets "virtue," while "power" (the edition's usual word for 德) is spent on 用人之力, which is only "using the strength of others." The reader of ch. 41 or 54 will misread which line names 德.
  Fix: "This is the power of not contending. This is called using the strength of others." (and retitle "The Power of Not Contending").

### Best lines
> The best warrior is not warlike. The best fighter shows no anger.

## Chapter 69 · The Side That Grieves Wins
Grade: A
Faithful, and the soldier's saying reads naturally; one pronoun jars.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > to underestimate your enemy is to come close to losing my treasures
  "Your enemy ... my treasures" switches person mid-sentence.
  Fix: "There is no disaster greater than underestimating the enemy; to underestimate the enemy is to come close to losing my treasures."

### Best lines
> it is the side that grieves that wins.

## Chapter 72 · When the People Stop Fearing
Grade: A
Faithful (and closer to the Chinese than Legge); two phrases are unclear.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > do not bear down on the life that keeps them alive
  "The life that keeps them alive" circles on itself; 無厭其所生 is "do not crush what they live by."
  Fix: "do not bear down on what they live by."
- `seg-0004` · read-aloud · low
  > He lets the one go and takes hold of the other.
  Third use of this phrase (chs. 12, 38); after two "but" clauses the ear cannot tell which is kept.
  Fix: "He lets that go and takes hold of this."

### Best lines
> When the people no longer fear what they ought to fear, then a far greater dread comes down upon them.
