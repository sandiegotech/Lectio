# Tao Te Ching · Chapters 10–18
Edition: lectio-modern · compared against legge-1891 and the received Chinese text (no original bundled).

## Chapter 10 · Mysterious Power
Grade: B
Faithful questions and a strong close; one gloss is written into the line.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > can you keep to the female, the yielding, receptive part?
  "The yielding, receptive part" explains 為雌 inside the sentence; the text says only "play the female" (Legge: "as a female bird").
  Fix: "When the gate of heaven opens and shuts, can you play the part of the female?"

### Best lines
> Can you cleanse the dark mirror of your mind until it is without a flaw?

## Chapter 12 · The Belly, Not the Eye
Grade: A
Vivid and faithful; one pronoun is unclear.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > He lets the one go and takes hold of the other.
  After "the belly, not the eye," "the one ... the other" leaves the ear unsure which is kept (去彼取此, "rejects that, takes this").
  Fix: "He lets that go and takes hold of this."

### Best lines
> The five colors blind the eye. The five notes deafen the ear. The five flavors numb the palate.

## Chapter 13 · Favor and Disgrace
Grade: A
Clear argument; minor variant and pronoun points only.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > Favor is a thing from above
  Follows the 寵為上 variant and drops the disgrace half; Legge (received text) has "Disgrace is being in a low position."
  Fix: "Favor is a thing from above, disgrace from below;" or follow the base.
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > the one who honors the world as their own body
  Singular "their" here, while the rest of the edition uses "he" for the ideal person; the shift is audible.
  Fix: "So the one who honors the world as his own body ... who loves the world as his own body"

### Best lines
> The reason I can suffer great trouble is that I have a body; if I had no body, what trouble could reach me?

## Chapter 15 · The Ancient Masters
Grade: B
The portrait of the masters is excellent; the second question reverses its sense.

### Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · high
  > Who can stay still until the moment to stir?
  孰能安以久動之徐生 asks who can stir what is at rest so it slowly comes to life (Legge: "Let movement go on, and the condition of rest will gradually arise"); the edition turns it into waiting.
  Fix: "Who can stir what is at rest and slowly bring it to life?"
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > like someone alert to enemies on every side
  四鄰 is "neighbors on all four sides"; "enemies" sharpens it.
  Fix: "Watchful, like someone wary of the neighbors on every side."

### Best lines
> Who can let the muddy water settle and slowly grow clear?

## Chapter 16 · Returning to the Root
Grade: A
Strong chain at the close; one term reads oddly.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · register · low
  > this is called returning to what was fated
  "What was fated" suggests predestined events; 復命 is returning to one's destiny or nature (Legge: "fulfilled their appointed end").
  Fix: "this is called returning to one's destiny. Returning to one's destiny is the constant;"

### Best lines
> For all their teeming growth, each one comes back to its root.

## Chapter 17 · The Best Ruler Is Barely Known
Grade: A
Plain and memorable; the last line leans a little toward agency.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > we did this ourselves
  我自然 is closer to "it happened of itself" / "we are this way by nature" (Legge: "We are as we are, of ourselves!"); "did this" adds doing. Defensible and widely used; keep only if deliberate.
  Fix: "the people all say: it happened of itself."

### Best lines
> The best ruler is one the people barely know is there.
