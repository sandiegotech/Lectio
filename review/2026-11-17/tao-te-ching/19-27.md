## Chapter 20 · Alone and Adrift
Grade: B
The finest lament in the book, kept intact, but the register slips twice and the last line explains itself.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > for I am nourished by the Mother, the Way.
  "The Way" is a gloss written into the line (it is Legge's parenthesis), and 貴 ("I prize") is dropped: 而貴食母, "I value being fed by the mother."
  Fix: "I alone am different from all the rest — I prize being fed by the Mother."
- `seg-0001` · register · medium
  > a respectful "yes" and a careless "yeah,"
  "Yeah" is slang and dates the line; the contrast (唯 / 阿) is between a deferential and an offhand reply.
  Fix: "Between a respectful 'yes' and a curt one, how much difference is there really?"
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > I drift like the restless sea
  澹 is calm, vast; "restless" reverses the image (Legge: "carried about as on the sea").
  Fix: "I drift like the wide sea;"
- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > like a country bumpkin
  "Bumpkin" is comic; 鄙 is plain "rustic" (Legge: "a rude borderer").
  Fix: "awkward and useless, like a rustic."

### Best lines
> I am weary and forlorn, like one with no home to return to.

## Chapter 22 · Yield and Remain Whole
Grade: A
Faithful paradoxes, cleanly cut; small polish only.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > He does not justify himself, and so he is known.
  不自是故彰: "he does not think himself right, and so he stands out." "Is known" is weaker than 彰.
  Fix: "He does not insist he is right, and so he stands out."
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > Truly it keeps a person whole, and brings them home.
  Singular "them" for "a person," against the edition's usual "he."
  Fix: "Truly it keeps you whole, and brings you home."

### Best lines
> Yield, and remain whole. Bend, and stay straight. Empty yourself, and be filled. Wear out, and be made new.

## Chapter 24 · Standing on Tiptoe
Grade: A
Faithful; one line is vague.

### Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > Insist you are right, and you are not seen for what you are.
  自是者不彰: "you do not stand out" (Legge: "is not distinguished"); "seen for what you are" says something else. Should echo the fix in 22.
  Fix: "Insist you are right, and you do not stand out."

### Best lines
> Stand on tiptoe, and you cannot keep your balance.

## Chapter 25 · Something Formed Before Heaven and Earth
Grade: B
Fine and faithful through the four greats; the famous last line is explained into the text.

### Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > the Way follows what is natural — what is so of itself.
  Two renderings of 自然 are set side by side, the second a gloss on the first; the line loses its stress.
  Fix: "and the Way follows what is so of itself."
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > and the human being is great too
  The base and received text have 王, "the king" (Legge: "the (sage) king"); "human being" follows a variant. Defensible, given the next line, but unmarked.
  Fix: keep only if the edition's note records the variant; otherwise "and the king is great too ... and the king is one of them."

### Best lines
> I do not know its name. Pressed to name it, I call it the Way; and straining further, I call it Great.
