# Nicomachean Ethics · Book I · The Good for Man
Grade: B
Faithful, complete and well paced; one key term (teleios) is split across two English words and breaks the argument's thread, and the "political animal" line has gone missing.

## Issues
- `seg-0015`, `seg-0016`, `seg-0017` · fidelity · medium
  > not all ends are final ... the most final of them ... final without qualification
  The function argument depends on *teleios* carrying through to its conclusion, but here it is "final", then "most complete" in seg-0019 ("in accordance with the best and most complete") and "complete virtue" in seg-0026/0034, so the reader cannot see that the definition answers the question. "Final" in this sense is also not one a modern reader carries.
  Fix: use "complete" throughout: "so plainly not all ends are complete ... the most complete of them"; "complete without qualification"; "happiness is plainly something complete and self-sufficient"; and seg-0010 "not complete enough".
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · medium
  > since a human being is by nature made for a community
  This is the famous *politikon ho anthrōpos* (Chase: "adapted to a social existence"), and the paraphrase makes it hard to recognize. The edition also uses "statecraft" for *politikē*, so the link to the political science is lost.
  Fix: "since a human being is by nature a political animal, made for life in a community."
- `seg-0010`, `seg-0016`, `seg-0019`, `seg-0022`, `seg-0025`, `seg-0034` · voice · medium
  > virtue turns out to be not final enough ... every excellence ... in accordance with excellence ... activity in accordance with virtue
  *Aretē* switches between "virtue" and "excellence" without signal, sometimes inside one argument (seg-0019 "excellence" against seg-0022 "virtue" for the same definition). A reader will think these are two things. Seg-0037 then needs "excellences" of character, which is fine, but the switching has to follow a rule.
  Fix: pick one rule and keep to it. Use "virtue" for the moral sense throughout; use "excellence" only where the craft analogy is live (seg-0018 to 0019 harpist), with "excellence, or virtue" at the first crossover in seg-0019.
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · low
  > in the category of substance (God, or intelligence)
  *Ho theos kai ho nous*: the capital "God" brings in a monotheist reading. Chase has "a god" for the parallel at seg-0033.
  Fix: "(a god, or mind)".
- `seg-0012` · register · low
  > in place (a habitation)
  "Habitation" is leftover archaism (*diaita*).
  Fix: "in place (a good place to live)".
- `seg-0018` · fidelity · low
  > by grasping the work, the characteristic function, of a human being
  This is a doubled gloss inside the sentence. "Function" is used alone everywhere after it.
  Fix: "by grasping the function of a human being."
- `seg-0004` · voice · low
  > To secure it for one person is something;
  *Agapēton*, "worth being content with", becomes "is something", which is flat and reads like a shrug.
  Fix: "To secure it for one person is worth having; to secure it for a whole people and for cities is finer and more godlike."
- `seg-0036` · read-aloud · low
  > when a person means to move them right, swerve off to the left
  Spoken aloud, "move them right" sounds like "move them correctly".
  Fix: "when a person means to move them to the right, swerve off to the left".

## Best lines
- "presenting our happy person as a kind of chameleon, built on no firm ground." (seg-0028)
- "blessed, but as human beings are blessed." (seg-0030)
