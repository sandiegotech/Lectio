# Nicomachean Ethics · Book IX · Self-Love and the Need for Friends
Grade: B
A warm, persuasive Book IX. The self-love chapters and the argument from shared awareness come across with real force. What needs fixing is one quotation imported from the base translator, the famous "political animal" line, and a few slips of register.

## Issues
- `seg-0046` · fidelity · medium
  > because love "is kindled by the eyes and fed by gazing,"
  This is not a quotation in Aristotle, who says plainly that love exists and grows chiefly through sight. Chase dropped in a line of Shakespeare ("engendered in the eyes, / With gazing fed"), and the edition has kept it as if Aristotle were quoting someone.
  Fix: "choosing that sense above all the others because it is chiefly through sight that love comes to be and lives".
- `seg-0035` · fidelity · medium
  > Man is a social animal, made by nature to live with others
  The Greek is *politikon*, the famous "political animal". Book I (seg-0017) renders the same phrase "made for a community", and Book VIII (seg-0041) says "political life", so the line appears in three forms and is recognizable in none.
  Fix: "Man is a political animal, made by nature to live with others" (and match Book I).
- `seg-0023` · fidelity · low
  > that comes only when he aches for the beloved when she is gone and longs for her presence.
  The Greek beloved has no gender, and in its setting is as often male. "She" is an addition.
  Fix: "that comes only when he aches for the beloved in absence and longs for the beloved's presence."
- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > since no one would pay a cent for what they know
  "Cent" brings in American currency.
  Fix: "since no one would pay anything for what they know".
- `seg-0002` · register · low
  > If that had been the deal both intended, fine.
  This reads as a spoken shrug. The Greek is "it would have been well".
  Fix: "If both had meant that to be the bargain, all would have been well."
- `seg-0037` · read-aloud · low
  > Life alone is a heavy thing
  It can be heard as "life by itself". The point is the solitary man's life.
  Fix: "Life is hard for the man who lives alone".
- `seg-0031` · fidelity · low
  > to do what is just, or self-controlled, or in any way virtuous
  This is *sōphrona*, which should be "temperate" (the work-wide term issue).
  Fix: "to do what is just, or temperate, or in any way virtuous".

## Best lines
- "He would rather have one year of intense joy than many tame ones, one great and noble life than a long ordinary one, one great and noble act than many small ones." (seg-0034)
- "for this is what 'living together' means for human beings, not merely feeding in the same place, as it does for cattle." (seg-0040)
