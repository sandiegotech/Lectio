# Nicomachean Ethics · Book V · Justice
Grade: B
A careful, lucid Book V that keeps hard technical stretches (proportion, exchange, equity) clear. The worked example of exchange has a wrong number, and the Euripides quotation is garbled.

## Issues
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · high
  > Let a house be worth five minae and a bed worth a tenth of that; then it is clear how many beds equal a house — five.
  The arithmetic is wrong. In Aristotle the bed is a tenth of B, which is ten minae, so it costs one mina and five beds equal the house (Chase: "C a bed worth 1/10th of B"). As written, a bed is half a mina and the house would equal ten beds.
  Fix: "Let a house be worth five minae and a bed one mina; then it is clear how many beds equal a house — five."
- `seg-0025` · fidelity · medium
  > "My mother he has slain; the tale is short — either he killed her willing and himself willing, or with her will but against his own"?
  "Himself willing" reads as if he killed himself, which wrecks the puzzle. The point is that both he and she were willing, or she was willing and he was not. The lines are verse in the source (Chase sets them as three lines) and have been run into prose.
  Fix: set it as verse —
  "My mother he has slain; the tale is short:
  he willing, and she willing; or she willing,
  and he against his will."
- `seg-0032` · read-aloud · medium
  > the one is having less than the mean, the other having more, like health in medicine and good condition in training.
  The comparison hangs on the wrong word. It is the mean that is like health and fitness, not the two evils.
  Fix: "the one is having less than the mean, the other more — the mean being here what health is in medicine and fitness is in training."
- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · low
  > most lawful acts are just the acts that naturally flow from virtue in its full sense
  In a book about justice, "just" as an adverb reads at first as the adjective.
  Fix: "most lawful acts are simply the acts that flow from virtue in its full sense".
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · low
  > the judge is meant to be justice come alive — justice given a face.
  *Dikaion empsychon* is one image. The second phrase is an added flourish.
  Fix: "the judge is meant to be justice come alive."

## Best lines

- "There is wisdom in the saying of Bias, that office will show the man" (seg-0006)
