# The Prince · Chapter XIX · Avoiding Contempt and Hatred
Grade: A
The book's longest chapter holds its line from conspiracy theory through the ten emperors without flagging; it is more accurate than the comparison text in several places (e.g. "when things outside are quiet"). Small fixes only.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > The first of these is the Parlement — the high court — and its authority.
  "The high court" is an editor's gloss inside the sentence; the source just says "el parlamento."
  Fix: "The first of these is the Parlement and its authority." (gloss in the margin)
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · low
  > He then went to find him in France, and stripped him of his state and his life.
  As in IV, "Francia" here is Roman Gaul; "France" is an anachronism for Albinus in AD 197.
  Fix: "He then went to find him in Gaul"
- `seg-0017` · read-aloud · low
  > Julianus the emperor being known for his slackness, Severus persuaded the army
  The opening absolute construction is stiff and Latinate.
  Fix: "Knowing the emperor Julianus for a sluggard, Severus persuaded the army"

## Best lines
"and the moment you open your mind to a discontented man, you give him the means to content himself"
"And here it must be noted that hatred is earned by good deeds as much as by bad ones"
