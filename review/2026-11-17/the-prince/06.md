# The Prince · Chapter VI · By Your Own Arms and Virtù
Grade: A
One of the edition's strongest chapters: the archer image, the innovator passage, and the armed prophets are all exact and memorable. Two small polish points.

## Issues
- `seg-0007` · read-aloud · low
  > the moment the crowd began not to believe him
  Literal from "cominciò a non crederli," but "began not to believe" trips in English.
  Fix: "the moment the crowd stopped believing him"
- `seg-0008` · register · low
  > from there he earned his promotion to prince
  "Promotion" has an office ring; the source says he deserved to be made their prince.
  Fix: "and from there he earned the right to be made their prince"

## Best lines
"The innovator has enemies in everyone who did well under the old order, and lukewarm defenders in everyone who might do well under the new."
"This is why all armed prophets have conquered, and the unarmed have been destroyed."
