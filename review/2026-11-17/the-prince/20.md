# The Prince · Chapter XX · Fortresses and Other Expedients
Grade: A
Faithful and well argued; the ladder image and "the best fortress there is" come through cleanly. A handful of polish points, including spelling drift in place names.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > you are forced to turn to mercenary soldiery, which is what was said of it above
  The relative clause does not attach to anything and stalls the sentence.
  Fix: "you are forced to turn to mercenary soldiery, whose nature I have described above"
- `seg-0003` · register · low
  > Such methods, besides, argue weakness in the prince
  "Argue" in the sense "indicate" is a sense few readers still carry.
  Fix: "Such methods, besides, betray weakness in the prince"
- `seg-0003` · form · low
  > when they were routed at Vailà
  Spelled "Vaila" in XII; similarly "Forlì" here (`seg-0006`) but "Forli" in III and XIII. Pick one spelling for each and use it throughout.
  Fix: standardize on "Vailà" and "Forlì" (or drop accents everywhere).
- `seg-0005` · read-aloud · low
  > a prince who has newly taken a state by means of inside support from within it
  "Inside ... from within it" says the same thing twice.
  Fix: "a prince who has newly taken a state with help from inside it"

## Best lines
"so that he may have cause to overcome them and to climb higher up the very ladder his enemies have carried to him."
"So the best fortress there is, is not to be hated by the people"
