# The Prince · Chapter VII · By Others' Arms and Fortune
Grade: B
A gripping, accurate Borgia chapter (the Ramiro scene and the deathbed confession are superb), held back by one tense slip that turns Borgia's plan into a fact.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · medium
  > he pounced on Pisa. After that, Lucca and Siena would fold at once
  Machiavelli's imperfects ("saltava in Pisa ... Lucca e Siena cedeva") are conditional, part of the plan cut short by Alexander's death; "pounced" reports it as done, then the next sentence switches to "would," and the reader cannot tell what happened.
  Fix: "he would have pounced on Pisa. After that, Lucca and Siena would have folded at once"
- `seg-0008` · register · low
  > and partly insured against present dangers
  "Insured" reads as the policy word; the source is "assicurato," made secure.
  Fix: "and partly secured against present dangers"
