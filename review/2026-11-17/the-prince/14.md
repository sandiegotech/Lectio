# The Prince · Chapter XIV · The Prince and the Art of War
Grade: A
Faithful and well shaped; Philopoemen's questions read as live speech, and the closing image of fortune finding the prince ready is kept. One awkward phrase.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > he should live in the hunt, hardening his body to exertion

  Fix: "he should be always out hunting, hardening his body to exertion"
