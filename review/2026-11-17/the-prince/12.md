# The Prince · Chapter XII · The Kinds of Troops, and Mercenaries
Grade: A
A long chapter carried with real energy: the indictment of mercenaries is sharp, every captain and battle is in place, and the closing catalogue of soft war lands with contempt. One small explanatory addition.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > he ruins you in the ordinary way — by losing.
  "By losing" is the editor's explanation of Machiavelli's dry "per l'ordinario"; the joke works better unexplained, and a reader gets it from the context.
  Fix: "And if the captain is no good, he ruins you in the ordinary way."

## Best lines
"swaggering among friends, cowardly before the enemy; no fear of God, no faith with men. Your ruin is put off only as long as the assault is put off: in peace you are stripped by them, in war by the enemy."
"Italy has been overrun by Charles, plundered by Louis, violated by Ferdinand, and insulted by the Swiss."
