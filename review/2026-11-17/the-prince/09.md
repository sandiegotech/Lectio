# The Prince · Chapter IX · The Civil Principality
Grade: A
Clear, exact, and quotable; the argument for the people over the nobles reads as one continuous voice. One idiom slips toward slang.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > If they hold back out of faint-heartedness — a natural shortage of spine — then make use of them
  The dash makes the second phrase read as the editor's gloss, and "shortage of spine" is colloquial; the source is a plain pair, "pusillanimità e defetto naturale d'animo."
  Fix: "If they hold back out of timidity and a natural want of courage, then make use of them"

"then everyone runs to him, everyone promises, and with death nowhere in sight, everyone is ready to die for him. But in hard times, when the state needs its citizens, few are to be found."
