# The Prince · Chapter I · The Kinds of Principalities
Grade: B
Clean and exact, but the chapter's last clause smuggles a definition of virtù into the sentence.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > either by fortune or by virtù — a man's own skill and nerve.
  The gloss is the editor's, not Machiavelli's; the source ends on the bare pair "o per fortuna o per virtù" (Marriott: "by fortune or by ability"). Keeping the Italian word is a defensible choice, but its definition belongs in the margin, not the sentence.
  Fix: "either by fortune or by virtù." (with virtù glossed in enrichment)
