# The Prince · Chapter XXV · Fortune, and How to Resist Her
Grade: B
The river image, the Julius episode, and the notorious last paragraph are all faithful and vivid; but at the one point where the book sets virtù directly against fortune, the edition drops its own key word.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > She shows her power where no ordered strength stands ready to resist her
  The source is "dove non è ordinata virtù a resisterle," and in `seg-0003` "conveniente virtù" becomes "proper strength." The edition keeps "virtù" untranslated for twenty-four chapters; switching to "strength" here, in the chapter that pits virtù against fortune, breaks the thread a reader has been following.
  Fix: "She shows her power where no virtù has been set in order to resist her" and, in `seg-0003`, "Had she been protected by proper virtù, as Germany, Spain, and France are"
