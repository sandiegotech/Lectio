# Confessions · Book II · The Stolen Pears
Grade: B
Strong and faithful; the pear-tree passage lands with full force. A few word choices and one form inconsistency need fixing.

## Issues
- `seg-0003`, `seg-0011`, `seg-0017` · form · low
  > 'Still, those who marry will have trouble in the flesh, and I would spare you.'
  This book quotes with single quotation marks; Books I, III and VII use double. One style across the work.
  Fix: convert to double quotes ("Still, those who marry ...", "so that idleness ...", "Come on — let's do it,").

- `seg-0014` · register · medium
  > to play at a cripple's freedom
  "Cripple" reads as a slur to many current readers and is not in the Latin, which says *mancam libertatem*, "a maimed freedom" (Pusey: "a maimed liberty").
  Fix: "to play at a maimed kind of freedom, prisoner that I was".

- `seg-0005` · register · low
  > the one true and good landlord of that field of yours, my heart
  "Landlord" now means someone who collects rent; *dominus agri* is the owner and master of the field.
  Fix: "the one true and good Lord of that field of yours, my heart."

- `seg-0004` · read-aloud · low
  > and I handed it both my hands
  The surrender idiom (*totas manus dedi*) does not come through; it reads like a slip.
  Fix: "and I surrendered to it completely."

- `seg-0007` · voice · low
  > Misery! Do I dare to say ...
  "Misery!" is not an exclamation anyone uses; the Latin is *Ei mihi!* The same problem recurs in seg-0017, "misery on me for having it".
  Fix: "Oh, my God! Do I dare to say ..." / "and woe to me for having it."

- `seg-0011` · fidelity · low
  > For they are beautiful; they do become us
  "Become us" adds "us"; the Latin (*speciosa sunt atque decora*) only says they are beautiful and fitting.
  Fix: "For they are beautiful, and they have their grace."

- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > Envy litigates over excellence
  Legal jargon in a run of otherwise plain personifications.
  Fix: "Envy fights over who excels".

## Best lines
- "I loved my own falling — not the thing I fell toward, the falling itself." (seg-0009)
- "Just someone saying, 'Come on — let's do it,' and the shame of not being shameless." (seg-0017)
