# Confessions · Book XII · The Heaven of Heavens
Grade: B
Close and lucid. The heaven of heavens, the formless matter and the five readings of Genesis 1:1 all keep their exact distinctions. One sentence copies Pusey's knotted syntax, and one term could mislead a reader.

## Issues
- `seg-0021` · read-aloud · medium
  > For that to which it is always good to hold fast to God outstrips every stretching-out and every rolling span of time.
  This carries over Pusey's construction ("For that, to which it is ever good to cleave fast to God, surpasses …") and cannot be parsed aloud. The sense is: because it is always good for it to cling to God, it outstrips all time.
  Fix: For it holds fast to God, as it is always good for it to do, and so it outstrips every stretching-out and every rolling span of time.

- `seg-0039` · fidelity · low
  > by choice, as the fruit is before the flower
  Here *electione* means priority in value, and "choice" reads as a decision someone makes. The same word recurs later: "Not prior in choice: sound is not better than song."
  Fix: by value, as the fruit is before the flower … Not prior in value: sound is not better than song.

- `seg-0005`, `seg-0017`, `seg-0018`, `seg-0023`, `seg-0028`–`seg-0033` · form · low
  > 'This is no intelligible form, like life or like justice …'
  This book uses single quotation marks throughout, while Books VIII–XI use double. This is the same work-wide issue filed for Books II, IV and VI.
  Fix: change to double quotation marks, keeping single quotes for quotations nested inside them.
