# Confessions · Book I · Infancy and Boyhood
Grade: B
Close to the Latin throughout, often closer than Pusey, and it reads well aloud; a handful of small fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0037` · fidelity · medium
  > For in your sight, what was fouler by then than I was?
  The Latin (*nam in illis iam quid me foedius fuit*) says "among them" / "in their eyes" (Pusey: "Before them"), not God's sight; the point is that he disgusted even people like himself.
  Fix: "For in their eyes, what was fouler by then than I was? — I even disgusted people like myself, ..."

- `seg-0026` / `seg-0027` · register · medium
  > "golden shower"
  Literal and also Pusey's word, but the phrase now carries a sexual slang sense that will make many readers laugh at exactly the wrong moment.
  Fix: "shower of gold" in both segments ("Jove, as they tell it, once sent a shower of gold into Danae's lap").

- `seg-0010` · fidelity · low
  > let him love to find you by not finding the answer, rather than find the answer and not find you.
  "the answer" is a gloss added twice; the Latin paradox is bare (*non inveniendo invenire potius quam inveniendo non invenire te*).
  Fix: "let him love to find you without finding it out, rather than find it out and not find you."

- `seg-0011` · register · low
  > I would be laughed at and told off ... deserved a telling-off ... forbade my being told off
  "Told off" is British and casual, used three times in four lines.
  Fix: "scolded" / "deserved scolding" / "forbade my being scolded".

- `seg-0011` · fidelity · low
  > they charm it away with remedies of their own
  Latin is *nescio quibus remediis*, "with some remedies or other"; "charm" adds magic, and the shrug of "I don't know what" is lost.
  Fix: "Mothers and nurses say they cure it with remedies of some kind or other."

- `seg-0023` · fidelity · low
  > with no drum-beat of punishment driving me on
  Added image; the Latin is "no burden of punishment pressing me".
  Fix: "with no weight of punishment driving me on".

- `seg-0025` · read-aloud · low
  > even those who have climbed aboard the wood of the cross barely manage to cross
  "cross ... cross" in one breath trips the ear.
  Fix: "which even those who have climbed aboard the wood of the cross can barely get across".

## Best lines
- "you made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you." (seg-0001)
- "Except that adult play is called 'business.'" (seg-0015)
