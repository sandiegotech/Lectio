# Confessions · Book VI · More Wretched, and You Nearer
Grade: B
Excellent narrative prose: Ambrose reading silently, Alypius in the amphitheater and the hatchet in the forum are all superb. It needs one factual fix, one tangled sentence fixed, and some register polish.

## Issues
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > a young man — one of the students, and the real thief
  The Latin says *adulescens quidam ex advocatis*, a young lawyer (Pusey: "a young man, a lawyer, the real thief"). The irony of a lawyer stealing outside the court is lost.
  Fix: "a young man — a lawyer, and the real thief — carrying a hidden hatchet, ..."

- `seg-0004` · read-aloud · medium
  > And when I discovered that man made by you in your own image was not understood by your spiritual children — ... — as though they believed you penned inside the shape of a human body (even though ...), I blushed, and gladly
  The quoted phrase is not marked, a dash and a parenthesis come before the verb, and "was not understood ... as though" garbles the sense.
  Fix: "And I discovered that your spiritual children, whom you have brought to second birth by grace out of our Catholic mother, do not take the words 'man made in your image' to mean that you are penned inside the shape of a human body. What a spiritual substance might be I still could not conceive, not even faintly, not even in shadow; but I blushed, and gladly: ..."

- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > a speech in which I would tell plenty of lies, and be applauded for the lying by men who knew
  "Who knew" what? The Latin (*ab scientibus*) means those who knew the lies were lies.
  Fix: "and be applauded for the lying by men who knew they were lies".

- `seg-0009` · register · low
  > whether I would rather be glad or scared
  "Scared" is too casual next to "glad", and it loses the point that he was afraid.
  Fix: "whether I would rather be glad or afraid".

- `seg-0002` / `seg-0003` · register · low
  > so that drunkards would get no license to soak / on furlough from the din of other men's business
  "Soak" is slang, and "furlough" is a military word; both stand out in a plain register.
  Fix: "so that drunkards would get no excuse to gorge themselves" / "on holiday from the din of other men's business".

- whole section · form · low
  > 'How do you know those books were given ...'
  This book uses single quotation marks; Books I, III and VII use double. (The same issue is filed for Books II and IV.)
  Fix: normalize to double quotes.

## Best lines
- "I was holding my heart back from assenting to anything, afraid of a headlong fall — and the hanging was killing me instead." (seg-0006)
- "From day to day I put off living in you; I did not put off dying, daily, in myself." (seg-0020)
