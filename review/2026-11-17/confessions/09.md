# Confessions · Book IX · Baptism, and the Death of Monica
Grade: B
Ostia and Monica's death are rendered with real power. One pronoun error turns a line against God, and Ambrose's hymn has lost its verse form.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · high
  > and that consumes you out of love the way it consumes a meal
  The flattering tongue consumes *us*, not God: "would out of love devour us, as it doth its meat."
  Fix: and that loves us the way it loves a meal, and so consumes us.

- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > comforting Verecundus in his sadness, as far as friendship allowed, since our conversion had not broken it
  This reverses the logic. Verecundus was sad *about* their conversion, and they comforted him with the friendship still intact ("who sorrowed ... that our conversion was of such sort").
  Fix: comforting Verecundus, who was saddened by our conversion, though our friendship stood unbroken

- `seg-0033` · form · medium
  > "God, creator of all things, ruler of the sky: you clothe the day in beautiful light, the night in the grace of sleep, so that rest may hand our slackened limbs back to the use of work …"
  Ambrose's hymn *Deus creator omnium* is quoted as verse (Pusey sets it in rhymed lines). Here it runs on as one prose sentence.
  Fix: set it as eight short lines: "God, creator of all things, / ruler of the sky, / you clothe the day in beautiful light, / the night in the grace of sleep, / so rest may give our slackened limbs / back to the use of work, / lift up our tired minds, / and loosen the knots of anxious grief."

- `seg-0003` · register · low
  > we carried your words fixed deep in our gut
  "Gut" is coarse for *visceribus* in a sentence about God's words.
  Fix: we carried your words fixed deep inside us

- `seg-0014` · register · low
  > You had made him well.
  This reads as "you had cured him." The source means "you had made him excellently."
  Fix: You had made him a fine boy.

- `seg-0021` · register · low
  > the kind of stuff that swollen, undigested discord belches up
  "Stuff" drops the register in the middle of a sustained period.
  Fix: the kind of thing that swollen, undigested discord belches up
