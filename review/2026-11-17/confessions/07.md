# Confessions · Book VII · The unchangeable light
Grade: B
The finest book so far: the sponge in the sea, the Platonist books, and the ascent to "that which is" are exact and luminous. Two word choices mislead, and the title's capitalization is out of line with the other books.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · register · medium
  > So gross was my heart — I was not clear even to my own sight
  Today "gross" means disgusting. The Latin (*incrassatus corde*) means thick or dull-hearted, and a reader will take the wrong sense.
  Fix: "So thick and dull was my heart — I was not clear even to my own sight — ..."

- `seg-0008` · register · low
  > ran up and down the whitewashed roads of the world
  *Dealbatiores vias* means the brighter, more splendid roads. "Whitewashed" now suggests a cover-up, which is the opposite emphasis.
  Fix: "ran up and down the brighter roads of the world".

- heading · form · low
  > Book VII · The unchangeable light
  Every other book title uses title case ("The Death of a Friend", "Take It and Read"); this one and Book X ("The palaces of memory") do not.
  Fix: "The Unchangeable Light".

## Best lines

- "O eternal Truth, and true Love, and beloved Eternity!" (seg-0016)
