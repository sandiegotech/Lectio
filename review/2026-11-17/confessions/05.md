# Confessions · Book V · Faustus and Ambrose
Grade: A
Faithful, lucid, and well paced; Faustus, the flight from Monica and the arrival at Milan all read beautifully. Only polish remains.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · register · low
  > But what good is the most elegant of waiters to a man thirsty for a costlier drink?
  *Pocillator* is a cup-bearer (Book III already uses "cup-bearer"). "Waiter" puts a modern restaurant into the image.
  Fix: "But what good is the most elegant of cup-bearers to a man thirsty for a costlier drink?"

- `seg-0011` · voice · low
  > was fed, all right, by the energy and feeling of his performance
  "All right" is a conversational tic that is not in the voice elsewhere.
  Fix: "was indeed fed by the energy and feeling of his performance".

- `seg-0023` · form · low
  > straggling through Manichean tricks / drunk on Manichean vanities
  Book III spells it "Manichaean". One spelling for the work.
  Fix: "Manichaean" in both places.
