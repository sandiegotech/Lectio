# Confessions · Book X · The palaces of memory
Grade: B
The strongest stretch of the edition: the memory argument holds together, and "Late have I loved you" lands. One clause loses its point, one key antithesis goes soft, and a few words slip in register.

## Issues
- `seg-0042` · fidelity · medium
  > and to do it not just someday, but now, at my present age
  The source contrast is between the next life and this one ("not only during life, but even at my present age"; *non solum in hac vita sed etiam in hac aetate*): God can do this even in this life. "Someday" erases that.
  Fix: and to do it not only within this life, but now, at my present age

- `seg-0034` · fidelity · medium
  > They love truth when it gives light; they hate it when it gives evidence.
  The antithesis is light and accusation ("they hate her when she reproves"; *redarguentem*). "Gives evidence" is vague and spoils the line.
  Fix: They love truth when it shines on them; they hate it when it convicts them.

- `seg-0039` · fidelity · low
  > What misery! Lord, have mercy on me. Look — I am not hiding my wounds.
  The source repeats the cry three times ("Woe is me!" before "lo! I hide not my wounds"). This keeps two. Also, "Grief upon grief for the world's good fortunes" turns a pronounced woe (*vae*) into a feeling.
  Fix: What misery! Lord, have mercy on me. What misery! Look — I am not hiding my wounds. … Woe to the world's good fortunes, and woe again … And woe, and woe, and woe again to the world's adversities …

- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · low
  > where a closeness holds that no having-enough can pull apart
  "Having-enough" is a coined compound that stops the ear at the climax of a five-part cadence.
  Fix: where an embrace holds that no fullness can pull apart

- `seg-0026` · register · low
  > a deep, borderless manyness
  "Manyness" sounds coined. The plain word for *multiplicitas* will do.
  Fix: a deep and boundless multiplicity

- `seg-0055` · register · low
  > From this disease of curiosity come the freak shows exhibited in the theater.
  "Freak shows" brings in a carnival-sideshow image the source does not carry (*miracula*, "strange sights").
  Fix: From this disease of curiosity come the marvels put on show in the theater.

- `seg-0057` · register · low
  > when our heart becomes the bin for things like these
  "Bin" is too slight and household for *receptaculum*.
  Fix: when our heart becomes the storehouse for things like these

## Best lines
My question was my gazing at them; their answer was their beauty.
They love truth when it shows itself, and hate it when it shows them.
