# Confessions · Book XIII · The Sabbath Without Evening
Grade: A
Faithful through the whole allegory of Genesis 1. Firmament as Scripture, sea as the unbelieving, dry land as the faithful, lights as the gifts, living soul as the tamed affections: every mapping holds, and the closing sabbath is beautifully paced.
