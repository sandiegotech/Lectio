# Genesis · Chapter 6 · The Earth Filled With Violence
Grade: A
Strong. The repeated "ruined… ruined… ruin" carries the Hebrew *shachat* chain across 6:11–13, which most English versions break; Nephilim and "the LORD was sorry" match the edition's divergence notes.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > Make yourself an ark of cypress wood.
  Hebrew *atsei-gopher* is a word no one can identify (KJV "gopher wood"); "cypress" is a scholar's guess printed as fact, the kind of gloss the charter keeps out of the sentence.
  Fix: "Make yourself an ark of gopher wood." (the guess can go in a note)
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > every shape the thoughts of the heart took was nothing but evil
  The image (*yetser*, "shaping, inclination") is right, but the relative clause makes the line hard to say; 8:21 handles the same word cleanly ("the shape of the human heart").
  Fix: "every shape of the thoughts of their hearts was nothing but evil, all day long"

## Best lines
- "And God looked at the earth, and there it was, ruined — because all flesh had ruined its way on the earth."
- "And Noah did it. Everything God commanded him, that is what he did."
