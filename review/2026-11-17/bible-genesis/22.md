# Genesis · Chapter 22 · The Binding
Grade: A
The edition at its best. Every "Here I am" is kept identical, "your son, your only one, the one you love — Isaac" holds the Hebrew's slow closing-in, the twice-repeated "the two of them walked on together" frames Isaac's question, and "God will see to the lamb" prepares the place-name.
