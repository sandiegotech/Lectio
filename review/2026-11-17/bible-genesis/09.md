# Genesis · Chapter 9 · The Bow in the Cloud
Grade: A
The covenant speech keeps its insistent repetitions ("all flesh", "never again", "between me and you") as it should, and 9:6 keeps its mirror shape. Only the form of Noah's curse falls short.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · form · low
  > “Cursed be Canaan. The lowest of slaves he will be to his brothers.”
  Noah's oracle (9:25–27) is verse in the Hebrew, three short parallel stanzas each closing "let Canaan be his slave"; run into prose, the refrain loses its hammer.
  Fix: set 9:25–27 as verse lines.

## Best lines
- "Whoever sheds a human being's blood, by a human being his blood will be shed — because God made human beings in his own image."
- "The bow will be in the cloud, and I will look at it and remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature"
