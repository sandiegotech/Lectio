# Genesis · Chapter 28 · The Ladder at Bethel
Grade: B
Jacob's dream is rendered with the right plainness ("came upon a certain place"; "and I did not know it"), and "stairway" matches the edition's note. But one of the most famous phrases in the book has been made smaller.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > and this is the gate of the sky.
  Hebrew *sha'ar hashamayim*, the "gate of heaven" every reader knows (KJV "this is the gate of heaven"). "Sky" turns Jacob's cry into weather and makes the line unrecognizable; *shamayim* here is God's dwelling, not the atmosphere.
  Fix: "This is nothing but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven."
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > a stairway set on the earth with its top reaching the sky
  Same word, same problem, a sentence earlier; with "the gate of heaven" restored, "reaching heaven" keeps the two lines tied (KJV "the top of it reached to heaven").
  Fix: "a stairway set on the earth with its top reaching heaven"

## Best lines
- "Surely the LORD is in this place — and I did not know it."
- "And look — I am with you, and I will keep you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land."
