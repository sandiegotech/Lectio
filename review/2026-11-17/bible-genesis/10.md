# Genesis · Chapter 10 · The Table of Nations
Grade: B
Names are kept in their traditional English forms and the clan formulas are intact. One sentence, though, gives the wrong family tree on a first reading.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · read-aloud · medium
  > And sons were born to Shem as well — the father of all the sons of Eber, the older brother of Japheth.
  As punctuated, "the older brother of Japheth" attaches to Eber, which says Eber is Japheth's brother. The Hebrew describes Shem twice over (KJV "Unto Shem also, the father of all the children of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder").
  Fix: "And sons were born to Shem as well — Shem, the older brother of Japheth, the father of all the sons of Eber."
