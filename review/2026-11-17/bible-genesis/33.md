# Genesis · Chapter 33 · The Face of a Brother
Grade: A
The reunion is told with restraint, and the face language from chapter 32 pays off in "seeing your face is like seeing the face of God". Esau's "I have plenty" against Jacob's "I have everything" keeps the Hebrew contrast (*yesh li rav* / *yesh li kol*).
