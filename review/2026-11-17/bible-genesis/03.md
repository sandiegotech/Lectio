# Genesis · Chapter 3 · The Serpent
Grade: B
Pilot text; the dialogue is the best speech in the edition so far. One dropped noun in the woman's sentence, and the curses lose their verse form.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > I will make your pains in childbirth great
  Hebrew *harbah arbeh itsvonekh weheronekh*, "I will greatly increase your pain and your pregnancies" (KJV "thy sorrow and thy conception"); the pregnancies are dropped and the two nouns folded into one.
  Fix: "I will greatly increase your pain and your pregnancies"
- `seg-0003`, `seg-0004`, `seg-0005` · form · low
  > I will set hatred between you and the woman, between your offspring and hers.
  The three sentences of judgment (3:14–19) are Hebrew poetry in parallel lines; printed as prose paragraphs the parallelism the charter says to "protect above all" is invisible.
  Fix: set 3:14–19 as verse lines, one parallel member per line.
- `seg-0007` · fidelity · low
  > Now he must not be allowed to reach out his hand
  The Hebrew breaks off mid-sentence (*we'attah pen-yishlach*, "and now, lest he reach out his hand… and live forever—") and the narrative acts instead of finishing it; the rendering smooths God's unfinished sentence into a complete rule.
  Fix: "And now — what if he reaches out his hand and takes from the tree of life too, and eats, and lives forever?"

## Best lines
- "The woman you put beside me — she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate."
- "You are dust, and to dust you will return."
