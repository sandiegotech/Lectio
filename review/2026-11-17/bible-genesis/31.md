# Genesis · Chapter 31 · The Flight, and the Heap of Witness
Grade: A
Rachel steals the gods and Jacob "stole the heart of Laban": the edition keeps the one verb for both thefts, which most translations lose. Jacob's twenty-years speech has real heat. One phrase trips.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · read-aloud · low
  > that you have come after me so hot?
  Hebrew *dalaqta acharai*, "you have hotly pursued me" (KJV "that thou hast so hotly pursued after me"); "come after me so hot" is not idiomatic English and stalls the angry rhythm of the line.
  Fix: "that you have come after me in hot pursuit?"

## Best lines
- "This is how I was: by day the heat ate me, and the cold by night, and sleep fled from my eyes."
- "The daughters are my daughters, the children are my children, the flocks are my flocks — everything you see is mine."
