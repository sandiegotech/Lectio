# Genesis · Chapter 11 · Babel, and the Line to Abram
Grade: A
Excellent. The three "Come on —" (11:3, 4, 7) carry the Hebrew *havah* so that God's "Come on — let's go down" answers the builders' words with the irony intact. Every age in the genealogy checks against the base.
