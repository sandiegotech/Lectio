# Genesis · Chapter 21 · Laughter, and the Wilderness
Grade: A
Isaac's birth, Hagar in the wilderness, and the Beersheba oath are each given their own pace; "the thing was very bad in Abraham's eyes" keeps the Hebrew idiom that God then answers word for word. One departure from the charter's *hesed* ruling.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · voice · low
  > As I have kept faith with you, so you will keep faith with me
  Hebrew *kachesed asher-asiti immekha* (KJV "according to the kindness that I have done unto thee"); the charter fixes *hesed* as "kindness", and 19:19 and 20:13 already use it.
  Fix: "As I have shown you kindness, so you will show kindness to me and to the land where you have been living as a stranger."
