# Genesis · Chapter 50 · God Meant It for Good
Grade: A
The book ends well: "Am I in God's place?", "You planned evil against me; God planned it for good", and "spoke to their hearts" (as 34:3). One legal word stands where the edition elsewhere has "offense".

Coverage: 50.1-6, 50.7-14, 50.15-21, 50.22-26 each hold their own verses; nothing missing.

## Issues
- `50.15-21` · register · low
  > forgive the crime of your brothers … forgive the crime of the servants
  *pesha* is rendered "offense" at 31:36 ("What is my offense? What is my sin"), the same pairing with *chatta't* as here; "crime" brings a courtroom register.
  Fix: "crime" → "offense" (both places).

## Best lines
- `50.15-21` "Do not be afraid. Am I in God's place? You planned evil against me; God planned it for good"
