# Genesis · Chapter 27 · The Stolen Blessing
Grade: A
A superb chapter: the blind father's questions, the doubled "Here I am", Esau's bitter cry, and "blessed he will stay" are all exact and speakable. Only the two blessings lose their verse form.

## Issues
- `seg-0005`, `seg-0008` · form · low
  > May God give you the dew of the sky, and the richness of the earth, and plenty of grain and new wine.
  Isaac's two blessings (27:27–29 and 27:39–40) are Hebrew poetry built on parallel pairs; Esau's is a deliberate inversion of Jacob's ("the dew of the sky" / "away from… the dew of the sky"), which shows best as matched lines.
  Fix: set 27:27b–29 and 27:39b–40 as verse lines.

## Best lines
- "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are Esau's hands."
- "Then who was it that hunted game and brought it to me, and I ate it all before you came, and I blessed him? And blessed he will stay."
