# Genesis · Chapter 24 · Rebekah at the Well
Grade: A
The longest chapter so far, and it never sags. The servant's retelling keeps the Hebrew's small variations from the first telling instead of copying it, Rebekah's "I will go" is given its weight, and *hesed* is "kindness" throughout per the charter.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · voice · low
  > the LORD, God of the sky and God of the earth
  Hebrew *elohei hashamayim* (KJV "the God of heaven"); "God of the sky" sounds like one god among the gods of the elements, and the edition elsewhere says "maker of heaven and earth" (14:19) for the same idea. Same in 24:7.
  Fix: "the LORD, God of heaven and God of the earth" (and "The LORD, God of heaven" in 24:7)

## Best lines
- "And they called Rebekah and said to her, “Will you go with this man?” And she said, “I will go.”"
- "And Isaac brought her into the tent of Sarah his mother, and he took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. And Isaac was comforted after his mother's death."
