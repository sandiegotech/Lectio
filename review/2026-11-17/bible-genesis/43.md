# Genesis · Chapter 43 · Benjamin
Grade: A
Judah's pledge, Israel's "if I am bereaved, I am bereaved", and Joseph's held-in weeping are all exact; one gesture is changed.

Coverage: 43.1-10, 43.11-14, 43.15-23, 43.24-31, 43.32-34 each hold their own verses; nothing missing.

## Issues
- `43.24-31` · fidelity · low
  > And they knelt and bowed.
  *vayyiqqedu vayyishtachavu* is "they bowed their heads and bowed down"; *qadad* is bowing the head, not kneeling, and the edition renders the same pair "bowed his head and bowed down" at 24:26, 48.
  Fix: And they bowed their heads and bowed down.

## Best lines
- `43.24-31` "Then he washed his face and came out, and he held himself in and said, “Serve the bread.”"
- `43.11-14` "As for me — if I am bereaved, I am bereaved."
