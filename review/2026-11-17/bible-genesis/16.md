# Genesis · Chapter 16 · Hagar
Grade: A
Sarai's bitterness and Hagar's flight are rendered with the Hebrew's bluntness ("her mistress became small in her eyes"), and the pun in "Maybe I will be built up through her" is caught in the English. One line prints a scholarly emendation as the text.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > “Have I really seen him here, and lived, after he saw me?”
  The Hebrew is obscure ("have I even here seen after the one who sees me?", KJV "Have I also here looked after him that seeth me?"); "and lived" comes from an emendation, not the received text.
  Fix: "Have I really seen here the one who sees me?" (the emended reading can go in the El-roi note)
