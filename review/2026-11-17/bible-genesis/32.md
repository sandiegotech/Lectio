# Genesis · Chapter 32 · The Night at the Jabbok
Grade: A
The wrestling is told in the Hebrew's stark, unattributed "And he said… And he said", which leaves the reader as unsure as Jacob. The "face" thread (cover his face, see his face, lift up my face, face to face) is carried word for word, as the edition's note promises; Peniel/Penuel are both kept as the text has them.
