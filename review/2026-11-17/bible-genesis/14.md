# Genesis · Chapter 14 · The War of the Kings, and Melchizedek
Grade: A
A dense campaign report rendered clearly, place-name glosses kept as the Hebrew gives them ("that is, Zoar"). Abram's refusal to the king of Sodom is well voiced.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > And Abram gave him a tenth of everything.
  The Hebrew says only "and he gave him a tenth of everything" (KJV "And he gave him tithes of all"); who paid whom is a known ambiguity, and naming Abram settles it inside the sentence.
  Fix: "And he gave him a tenth of everything." (if the edition wants to resolve it, say so in a note)

## Best lines
- "I will not take a thread or a sandal strap — I will not take anything that is yours — so that you can never say, ‘I made Abram rich.’ Nothing for me."
