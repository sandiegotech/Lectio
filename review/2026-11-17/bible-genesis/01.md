# Genesis · Chapter 1 · The Seven Days
Grade: B
The charter's set pieces land (1:1 dependent clause, "Let there be light" untouched, the evening/morning refrain verbatim); one dropped word and one awkward pronoun at the most famous verse.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > Let them mark the seasons, the days, and the years
  Drops "signs" (Hebrew *le'otot*; KJV "let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years").
  Fix: "Let them be signs, and mark the seasons, the days, and the years"
- `seg-0007` · read-aloud · medium
  > in the image of God he created it; male and female he created them
  "Created it" makes humanity an object at the line every reader knows; the Hebrew is *oto*, "him" (KJV "created he him"), and the singular-to-plural turn is the point of the verse.
  Fix: "So God created the human in his own image — in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them." (or keep "humanity" in 1:26 and read "he created them" here, accepting the loss of the singular/plural turn)
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > plants that seed themselves
  Hebrew *zorea zera*, "yielding seed" (KJV "herb yielding seed"); "seed themselves" says self-sowing, which is narrower. 1:12 and 1:29 already say "seed-bearing".
  Fix: "seed-bearing plants"

## Best lines
- "When God began to create the heavens and the earth — the earth was a shapeless waste, darkness lay over the face of the deep, and the breath of God hovered over the waters — God said, “Let there be light.” And there was light."
- "And God saw all that he had made — and look, it was very good."
