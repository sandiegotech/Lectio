# Genesis · Chapter 37 · The Dreamer and the Coat
Grade: A
Strong: the key words (sent, pit, recognize, coat) are held constant and the scene moves fast; one emphatic doubling is flattened.

Coverage: 37.1-4, 37.5-11, 37.12-17, 37.18-24, 37.25-30, 37.31-36 each hold their own verses; nothing missing.

## Issues
- `37.5-11` · fidelity · low
  > “Are you going to be king over us? Are you going to rule us?”
  The Hebrew doubles each verb (*hamalokh timlokh … im-mashol timshol*), the emphasis this edition renders with "really" elsewhere (18:13, 27:21) and in this same paragraph at 37:10.
  Fix: “Are you really going to be king over us? Are you really going to rule us?”

## Best lines
- `37.18-24` "Here comes that master of dreams."
- `37.25-30` "The boy is gone! And I — where am I to go?"
