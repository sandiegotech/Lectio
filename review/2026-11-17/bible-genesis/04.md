# Genesis · Chapter 4 · Cain and Abel
Grade: A
Spare and exact: the gap in 4:8 ("And Cain said to his brother Abel —") is kept honestly, and "Am I my brother's keeper?" stands untouched. Two small polish points.

## Issues
- `seg-0007` · read-aloud · low
  > and named him Seth — “because God has set in place another offspring for me
  The quotation has no speaker; the Hebrew has her say it (*ki shat-li elohim*, KJV "For God, said she, hath appointed me").
  Fix: "and named him Seth, saying, “God has set in place another offspring for me instead of Abel, since Cain killed him.”"
- `seg-0006` · form · low
  > Adah and Zillah, hear my voice. Wives of Lamech, listen to what I say.
  Lamech's song is the first poem after Eden, built on paired lines; printed as prose the pairing is lost.
  Fix: set 4:23–24 as verse, one member per line.

## Best lines
- "Your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground."
- "sin is crouching at the door — and its desire is for you, but you can master it."
