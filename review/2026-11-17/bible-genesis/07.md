# Genesis · Chapter 7 · The Flood
Grade: A
The flood's liturgical repetition ("after its kind", "male and female", "the waters overpowered") is kept rather than trimmed, which is right for this text. One pronoun stumbles.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · read-aloud · low
  > And he wiped out every living thing that was on the face of the ground
  "He" has no subject within reach — the last named actor ("the LORD shut him in") is in the previous segment, and Noah is nearer — so a listener hears Noah. The Hebrew *vayyimach* is often read passive (KJV "every living substance was destroyed").
  Fix: "Every living thing that was on the face of the ground was wiped out — human beings and animals and crawling things and birds of the sky."

## Best lines
- "And the LORD shut him in."
