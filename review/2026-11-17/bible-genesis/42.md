# Genesis · Chapter 42 · The Brothers Come Down
Grade: A
Very good: "made himself a stranger" keeps the *hikkir* root, "distress … distress" (42:21) keeps the echo, sack and pack stay apart. One verb is rendered two ways within the chapter.

Coverage: 42.1-5, 42.6-17, 42.18-24, 42.25-28, 42.29-34, 42.35-38 each hold their own verses; nothing missing.

## Issues
- `42.6-17` · fidelity · low
  > while the rest of you stay in chains
  *he'asru* ("be bound, confined") is the verb of 42:19, rendered "stay confined" there; "in chains" sharpens it and breaks the echo.
  Fix: while the rest of you stay confined

## Best lines
- `42.25-28` "And their hearts gave out, and they turned trembling to one another and said, “What is this that God has done to us?”"
- `42.35-38` "Joseph is no more, and Simeon is no more, and now you would take Benjamin. It all falls on me."
