# Genesis · Chapter 25 · Two Nations
Grade: A
The birth of the twins and the stew scene are vivid without decoration; Esau's "that red stuff — that red stuff" carries the Hebrew's doubled *ha'adom* exactly. Two small points.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > He settled alongside all his kin.
  Hebrew *al-penei khol-echav nafal* repeats the oracle of 16:12, which the edition rendered "he will live at odds with all his brothers"; "alongside" drops the hostility and breaks the echo (KJV "he died in the presence of all his brethren").
  Fix: "He settled at odds with all his kin."
- `seg-0004` · form · low
  > Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples will part from your body.
  The oracle (25:23) is four parallel lines in the Hebrew; as prose the pairing is blurred.
  Fix: set 25:23 as verse, one member per line.

## Best lines
- "Let me gulp down some of that red stuff — that red stuff — because I am exhausted."
- "So Esau despised his birthright."
