# Genesis · Chapter 12 · Go
Grade: A
The call lands with the right abruptness ("Go — out of your country…"), and the two different Hebrew verbs in 12:3 (bless / treat with contempt / curse) are kept distinct, which the KJV does not do. The Egypt episode is brisk and exact.
