# Mark · Chapter 4 · What the Sower Went Out to Do
Grade: B
The parables speak well aloud and the storm is superb; the hard saying in verse 12 is softened.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > or they might turn back, and be forgiven.
  *mēpote epistrepsōsin* is purposive, "lest they turn" (KJV "lest at any time they should be converted"); "or they might" reads as a mere possibility and takes the edge off one of Mark's hardest sayings.
  Fix: so that they will not turn back and be forgiven.
- `seg-0003` · read-aloud · low
  > they fall away right away.
  The jingle trips the ear; keep the fixed "right away" but move it.
  Fix: and when pressure or persecution comes because of the word, right away they fall away.

## Best lines
- "He got up, gave an order to the wind, and said to the sea: 'Quiet. Be muzzled.' The wind dropped, and everything went dead calm."
- "He sleeps and gets up, night after night, day after day, and the seed sprouts and grows — he has no idea how."
