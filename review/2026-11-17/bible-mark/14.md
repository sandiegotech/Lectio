# Mark · Chapter 14 · The Supper, the Garden, the Courtyard
Grade: B
The best-written chapter in the edition: Gethsemane and the courtyard are plain and devastating. Three small fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > Some of them were furious with each other about it:
  *aganaktountes pros heautous* is "indignant, saying among themselves" (KJV "had indignation within themselves"); they are angry at the waste, not at each other.
  Fix: Some of them said to one another, indignant:
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · medium
  > As soon as he arrived he went straight up to Jesus
  Greek *elthōn euthys proselthōn autō* (KJV "as soon as he was come, he goeth straightway to him"); the charter fixes *euthys* as "right away", every time.
  Fix: When he arrived he went right away up to Jesus and said, "Rabbi," and kissed him.
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > And right then a rooster crowed a second time.
  Greek *kai euthys ek deuterou alektōr ephōnēsen* (KJV "And the second time the cock crew"; critical text has *euthys*); same rule, at the climax where the fixed word matters most.
  Fix: And right away a rooster crowed a second time.

## Best lines
- "Abba — Father — everything is possible for you. Take this cup away from me. But not what I want. What you want."
- "And they all abandoned him and ran."
