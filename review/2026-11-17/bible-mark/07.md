# Mark · Chapter 7 · What Actually Makes a Person Unclean
Grade: B
Sharp argument and a tender healing; only small slips of wording and consistency.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > Isaiah was right about you hypocrites.
  Greek *kalōs eprophēteusen Ēsaias*, "Isaiah prophesied well" (KJV "Well hath Esaias prophesied"); "was right" drops that he prophesied.
  Fix: Isaiah prophesied rightly about you hypocrites.
- `seg-0005` · voice · low
  > his disciples asked him about the comparison.
  *parabolē* again rendered "comparison" (as in 3:23), while chapter 4 uses "parables"; one word, one rendering.
  Fix: his disciples asked him about the parable.

## Best lines
- "You have a fine way of setting aside God's command in order to keep your own tradition."
- "And the man's ears were opened, the knot of his tongue was untied, and he began speaking clearly."
