# Mark · Chapter 1 · The Beginning of the Good News
Grade: B
Fast and rough as Mark should be, but the charter's "right away" rule slips four times and verse 41 dodges its crux.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · medium
  > as soon as the Sabbath came he went into the synagogue
  Greek *kai euthys tois sabbasin* (KJV "straightway on the sabbath day"); the charter fixes *euthys* as "right away", every time.
  Fix: And right away on the Sabbath he went into the synagogue and taught.
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · medium
  > Just then a man with an unclean spirit was there
  Greek *kai euthys ēn* (KJV "And there was"; critical text has *euthys*); same rule.
  Fix: And right away there was a man with an unclean spirit in their synagogue, and he screamed:
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · medium
  > As soon as they left the synagogue
  Greek *kai euthys ... exelthontes* (KJV "forthwith, when they were come out"); same rule.
  Fix: Right away, as they left the synagogue, they went to the house of Simon and Andrew
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > Jesus sent him off at once with a stern warning
  Greek *embrimēsamenos ... euthys exebalen auton*: *euthys* again rendered "at once", and *exebalen* is Mark's rough "drove out" (the same verb as 1:12), smoothed into "sent off". KJV "straitly charged him, and forthwith sent him away".
  Fix: Jesus warned him sternly and right away drove him off:
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > Moved to the depths, Jesus stretched out his hand
  The charter's named crux (compassion vs. anger). The divergence note explains the choice (render the visceral depth, not the sentiment), and the reasoning is sound, but "moved to the depths" is not living English: it reads as half a phrase, and it recurs in 6:34 and in Jesus' own mouth at 8:2. KJV "moved with compassion".
  Fix: Deeply stirred, Jesus stretched out his hand (still neutral between the two readings; update the divergence's "lectio" field to match).
- `seg-0006` · register · medium
  > The time is up.
  Greek *peplērōtai ho kairos*, "the time is fulfilled" (KJV). "Time's up" now means time has run out, the opposite of a moment arriving.
  Fix: The time has come.
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > Get the road ready for the Lord.
  The famous line (KJV "Prepare ye the way of the Lord") loses recognizability, and *hodos* ("the way") is a word Mark keeps returning to ("on the way", 8:27, 10:52).
  Fix: Prepare the way for the Lord. Make his paths straight. (and "he will prepare your way" in the line before)
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · low
  > Jesus cut him off: "Quiet. Come out of him."
  *epetimēsen* is "rebuked / sternly ordered", a command, not an interruption.
  Fix: Jesus rebuked him: "Be quiet. Come out of him."

## Best lines
- "Simon and his companions hunted him down, and when they found him they said, 'Everyone is looking for you.'"
- "And right away the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness."
