# Mark · Chapter 12 · The Vineyard, the Coin, and the Widow
Grade: B
The controversies are crisp and the Shema is handled exactly; Mark's own coin note is replaced with the editor's.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · medium
  > put in two lepta — the smallest coins there are.
  Greek *lepta duo, ho estin kodrantēs* (KJV "two mites, which make a farthing"). Mark's own aside names the Roman coin; the edition drops it and inserts an explanation of its own. The charter keeps biblical coins.
  Fix: put in two lepta, which make a quadrans.

## Best lines
- "Give Caesar back what belongs to Caesar, and give God what belongs to God."
- "She, out of her need, gave everything she had — her whole living."
