# Mark · Chapter 6 · No Honor at Home; Bread in the Wilderness
Grade: B
Herod's banquet and the sea-walk are told with real drive, but the chapter breaks two charter rulings (units and hours) and drops two *euthys*.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · medium
  > spend two hundred days' wages on bread
  Greek *dēnariōn diakosiōn* (KJV "two hundred pennyworth"). The charter keeps biblical coins; converting to wages explains into the text.
  Fix: spend two hundred denarii on bread
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · medium
  > somewhere around three in the morning he came toward them
  Greek *peri tetartēn phylakēn tēs nyktos* (KJV "about the fourth watch of the night"). The charter keeps biblical hours.
  Fix: And around the fourth watch of the night he came toward them, walking on the sea.
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · medium
  > She hurried straight back in to the king
  Greek *euthys meta spoudēs* (KJV "straightway with haste"); "right away" is fixed for *euthys*.
  Fix: Right away she hurried back in to the king
- `seg-0011` · fidelity · medium
  > As soon as they got out of the boat, people recognized him.
  Greek *euthys epignontes auton* (KJV "straightway they knew him"); same rule.
  Fix: When they got out of the boat, people recognized him right away.
- `seg-0007` · register · medium
  > he was moved to the depths for them
  *splanchnizomai* (KJV "moved with compassion"); "moved to the depths" never says what he felt, and the phrase is not living English. Same rendering in 1:41.
  Fix: his heart went out to them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd.
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > looked up to the sky, gave thanks, broke the loaves
  The verb is *eulogēsen*, "blessed" (KJV); Mark distinguishes it from *eucharisteō* ("gave thanks", 8:6) and the pair recurs at the Last Supper (14:22–23).
  Fix: looked up to heaven, blessed and broke the loaves

## Best lines
- "Ask me for anything you want, and I'll give it to you." And he swore to her, "Whatever you ask me for, I'll give you — up to half my kingdom."
- "He meant to pass by them."
