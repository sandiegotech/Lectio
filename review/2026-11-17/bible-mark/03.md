# Mark · Chapter 3 · The Twelve, and the Divided House
Grade: B
Strong and plain; a few small softenings and one more lost "right away".

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > they went out to take charge of him
  *kratēsai* is "seize, restrain" (KJV "to lay hold on him"), the same verb Mark uses for the arrest in Gethsemane; "take charge of" makes the family sound managerial rather than forcible.
  Fix: they went out to seize him
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > he is guilty of a lasting sin
  *aiōniou hamartēmatos* is "an eternal sin" (KJV, from another reading, "eternal damnation"); "lasting" softens the hardest word in the saying.
  Fix: he is guilty of an eternal sin
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · medium
  > the Pharisees went straight out
  Greek *exelthontes ... euthys* (KJV "straightway"); the charter fixes *euthys* as "right away", every time.
  Fix: the Pharisees went out and right away started plotting with the Herodians how to destroy him.
- `seg-0005` · voice · low
  > spoke to them in comparisons
  First use of *parabolē*; chapter 4 renders the same word "parables" throughout, so the reader loses the thread.
  Fix: spoke to them in parables

## Best lines
- "If Satan has risen against himself and is divided, he can't stand — that's the end of him."
- "Here is my mother, here are my brothers. Whoever does what God wants is my brother and sister and mother."
