# Mark · Chapter 15 · The Cross
Grade: B
The passion is told with restraint and force, but it converts every hour of the crucifixion to clock time, against the charter's own example ("the third hour").

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > It was nine in the morning when they crucified him.
  Greek *ēn de hōra tritē* (KJV "it was the third hour"). The charter names exactly this phrase as one to keep; Mark's hours (third, sixth, ninth) are the frame of the scene.
  Fix: It was the third hour when they crucified him.
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > When noon came, darkness fell over the whole land until three in the afternoon. And at three Jesus cried out
  Greek *hōras hektēs ... heōs hōras enatēs ... tē enatē hōra* (KJV "the sixth hour ... until the ninth hour ... at the ninth hour"); same ruling.
  Fix: When the sixth hour came, darkness fell over the whole land until the ninth hour. And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out in a loud voice,
- `seg-0001` · voice · low
  > and the whole Sanhedrin.
  The same word *synedrion* is "the whole council" at 14:55 and "the council" at 15:43; one body, one name.
  Fix: and the whole council.
