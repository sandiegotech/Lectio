# Mark · verify 2026-11-17 · rejected and policies

## Policies

- **εὐθύς is "right away", every time** (charter ruling, which outranks the pilot's own Mark 1 text). I checked all 41 occurrences in the critical text: 1:10, 12, 18, 20, 21, 23, 28, 29, 30, 42, 43; 2:8, 12; 3:6; 4:5, 15, 16, 17, 29; 5:2, 29, 30, 42; 6:25, 27, 45, 50, 54; 7:25; 8:10; 9:15, 20, 24; 10:52; 11:2, 3; 14:43, 45, 72; 15:1. Thirteen were rendered some other way ("as soon as", "just then", "at once", "straight", "right then"). The twelve the reviewer named are patched, plus **4:29**, which the sweep found and the report missed. Each fix moves "right away" onto the verb that εὐθύς modifies and leaves the rest of the sentence alone. Bracketed readings stay as rendered: 5:42b, where NA has [εὐθύς] before ἐκστάσει, and 7:35, where NA has [εὐθέως] and Westcott-Hort omits it. The edition follows the WH line, and a doubtful word does not bind the rule. 9:15's "Right away, as soon as the whole crowd saw Jesus" keeps "right away" for εὐθύς, and its "as soon as" renders the participle, so it stays.
- **Biblical units and hours stay** (charter: "Keep biblical units (cubits, denarii, 'the third hour')"). Patched: 6:37 denarii, 6:48 the fourth watch, 12:42 lepta / quadrans (Mark's own gloss restored), 15:25 the third hour, 15:33–34 the sixth and ninth hours. I swept the book and found no other converted units: 12:15 and 14:5 already say denarii, and 13:35 already lists the watches as Mark names them.
- **σπλαγχνίζομαι: "his heart went out" except at 1:41.** In 6:34 and 8:2 there is no textual variant, so the plain idiom wins, and in 8:2 it is Jesus speaking, which the charter's read-aloud rule puts first. "Heart" keeps the image of the inward organs. 1:41 keeps "Moved to the depths" (see the rejected items below). 9:22 ("have pity on us") is the father's plea, and it stays as it is.
- **Hard words stay hard.** Patched: 3:21 seize, 3:29 eternal sin, 4:12 "so that they won't turn back", 8:32 rebuking, 8:34 deny himself, 8:38 adulterous. 14:30 and 14:72 keep "disown" for ἀπαρνέομαι. It is strong, recognizable, and nobody flagged it, and 14:68 and 14:70 already say "denied it".
- **παραβολή is "parable"** everywhere it names the form: 3:23 and 7:17 are patched. 4:30 ("What picture can we use") and 13:28 ("the lesson of the fig tree") are idiomatic uses, and they stay.

## Rejected

- 1:41 `seg-0012` "Moved to the depths" → "Deeply stirred": rejected. This is the charter's named crux (compassion or anger). The pilot rendering is neutral between the two readings on purpose, and the book's 1:41 divergence entry quotes it word for word. That entry is not section text, so apply.py cannot change it, and the patch would leave the note and the text out of step. The phrase is understandable as it stands.
- 1:43 `seg-0012` "sent him off" → "drove him off": rejected. Only the εὐθύς is patched. In English "drove him off" reads as chasing him away, and KJV has "sent him away".
- 1:15 `seg-0006` "The time is up" → "The time has come": rejected. This is a pilot choice, and the pilot is the register standard. πεπλήρωται ὁ καιρός means the appointed period is complete, and "time is up" says exactly that: the waiting is over.
- 1:3 `seg-0001` "Get the road ready for the Lord" → "Prepare the way": rejected (low). The pilot chose this de-churched rendering deliberately, and "road" is consistent with "he will prepare your road" in the same verse.
- 1:25 `seg-0008` "Jesus cut him off" → "Jesus rebuked him": rejected (low). This is the pilot's wording. It conveys the sharp silencing, and the edition already varies ἐπιτιμάω by context (3:12, 4:39, 8:30, 10:13).
- 5:7 `seg-0002` the reviewer's added "I beg you": rejected as an addition the Greek does not have. The patch is "In God's name, don't torture me!"
- 11:2 `seg-0001` reviewer wording "Right away, as you enter it": adjusted to "As you enter it, right away you'll find", so the phrase follows the Greek order of the main verb. The issue itself is kept.
- 14:45 `seg-0010` reviewer wording "went right away up to": adjusted to "When he arrived, right away he went up to Jesus" for the read-aloud test. The issue itself is kept.
