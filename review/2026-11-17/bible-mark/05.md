# Mark · Chapter 5 · Legion, and the Girl Who Got Up
Grade: B
A vivid, well-paced chapter; one slangy oath and one more "right away" lost.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · register · medium
  > Swear to God you won't torture me!
  *horkizō se ton theon* is "I put you under oath by God" (KJV "I adjure thee by God"). "Swear to God" is now a casual exclamation and blurs who is binding whom.
  Fix: In God's name, I beg you, don't torture me!
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · medium
  > As soon as he stepped out of the boat, a man with an unclean spirit came out
  Greek *euthys hypēntēsen* (KJV "immediately there met him"); the charter fixes *euthys* as "right away", every time.
  Fix: As he stepped out of the boat, right away a man with an unclean spirit came out of the tombs to meet him.

## Best lines
- "Little girl, I'm telling you, get up." And right away the girl got up and started walking around; she was twelve years old.
- "Go home to your own people and tell them what the Lord has done for you" ... started telling it all over the Decapolis — what Jesus had done for him.
