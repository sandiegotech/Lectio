# The Aeneid · Book VII · Latium, and the Opening of the War
Grade: B
Close and vivid. It follows the Latin almost line for line. A few knots in the syntax and one mistranslated epithet.

## Issues
- `seg-0040` · fidelity · high
  > That is why horned horses are kept far from the temple
  Latin *cornipedes* means "horn-footed", that is, hoofed. Horses with horns is a mistranslation, and a reader will picture something strange.
  Fix: That is why hoofed horses are kept far from the temple

- `seg-0023` · fidelity · medium
  > making a fool of a prophetess afraid of nothing among the wars of kings
  The Latin is *falsa formidine*: old age fools her with *false* fears. "Afraid of nothing" reads as "fearless", which is the opposite.
  Fix: making a fool of you, a prophetess, with false fears among the wars of kings.

- `seg-0014` · read-aloud · medium
  > we Latins are the people of Saturn, just not because we are forced to it or held to any law
  "Just" reads as "only", so the sentence seems to say the Latins are "just not forced".
  Fix: we Latins are the people of Saturn, righteous not because any law binds us, but of our own free will, keeping to the way of our ancient god.

- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · medium
  > These answers his father Faunus gave him, this warning out of the silent night, Latinus did not keep shut in his own mouth
  The Latin word order is kept, so the sentence's subject arrives only at the end. A listener gets lost.
  Fix: Latinus did not keep these answers to himself, this warning his father Faunus gave him out of the silent night;

- `seg-0015` · read-aloud · medium
  > even the man has heard of it who lives shut off at the far edge of the world
  The relative clause has drifted away from "the man" it belongs to.
  Fix: even the man who lives shut off at the far edge of the world, where Ocean curls back, has heard of it, and the man cut off …

- `seg-0028` · read-aloud · medium
  > the weapons chance first stained are wet with fresh blood
  This is garbled. The Latin (*quae fors prima dedit*) means the weapons chance first put in their hands.
  Fix: the weapons chance first put in their hands are wet with fresh blood.

- `seg-0022` · register · low
  > a stranger is being sought to heir the kingdom
  "Heir" used as a verb is not current English.
  Fix: a stranger is being sought to inherit the kingdom.

- `seg-0031` · read-aloud · low
  > When the elders have voted certainly for war
  "Voted certainly" is awkward.
  Fix: When the elders have fixed their vote for war

- `seg-0039` · voice · low
  > the glass water of Fucinus
  Fix: the glassy water of Fucinus

## Best lines
- "Look — we're even eating our tables!" said Iulus, no more than that, joking. (`seg-0009`)
- the blood choked the wet road of his voice and stopped the thin breath of his life. (`seg-0027`)
