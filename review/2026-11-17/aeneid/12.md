# The Aeneid · Book XII · The Single Combat, and the Death of Turnus
Grade: B
A powerful close: the dream simile, Juno's last request and the final scene all carry. A few clauses are muddled, one epithet is wrong, and the poem's echoing last line is worded differently from its first appearance in Book XI.

## Issues
- `seg-0041` · voice · medium
  > and his life fled with a groan, resentful, down to the shades.
  Virgil uses the same line, word for word, for Camilla's death (XI.831) and Turnus's (XII.952). The echo is deliberate. Book XI `seg-0029` renders it "her soul fled with a groan, in anger, down to the shades". The two should match so the echo survives.
  Fix: and his life fled with a groan, in anger, down to the shades.

- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > The poor foreigners frighten you like birds, and ravage your shores; but they'll run for the open sea in a pack, like that eagle.
  The Latin *improbus advena* is singular and hostile: "the shameless stranger", meaning Aeneas. It is not "poor foreigners". The Rutulians are the weak birds.
  Fix: The shameless stranger frightens you as if you were weak birds, and ravages your shores; but he will take to flight and spread his sails far out to sea, like that eagle.

- `seg-0035` · read-aloud · medium
  > and I blessed her daring more for his life
  This is garbled. The Latin: "I approved her daring greater things to save his life".
  Fix: and I approved her daring more to save his life

- `seg-0010` · read-aloud · low
  > No day will break this peace for Italy, no peace however the world goes
  The second "peace" should be "treaty". As written, the phrase is muddled.
  Fix: No day will break this peace or this treaty for Italy, however things fall;

- `seg-0029` · fidelity · low
  > has died by her own hand, and fled the daylight in terror.
  The Latin *lucemque perosa* means "hating the light", not terror.
  Fix: has died by her own hand, hating the daylight.

- `seg-0014` · register · low
  > I alone have the right to fight. Let me at it
  "Let me at it" is slangy in Aeneas's mouth.
  Fix: I alone have the right to fight. Leave it to me

- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > my father Latinus will keep his arms
  The Latin is *socer*, father-in-law.
  Fix: my father-in-law Latinus will keep his arms

- `seg-0033` · read-aloud · low
  > fenced by the crimson feathers of a hunt
  Readers will not know about the hunter's line of scarlet feathers used to scare game.
  Fix: fenced in by the hunters' line of scarlet feathers

- `seg-0021` · register · low
  > out of the car between the reins
  The archaic "car" again (see X).
  Fix: out of the chariot, over the reins

## Best lines
- "Learn courage from me, my boy, and real toil; good luck from others." (`seg-0019`)
- Troy has fallen — let it stay fallen, name and all. (`seg-0035`)
