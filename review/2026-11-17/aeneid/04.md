# Aeneid · Book IV · The Tragedy of Dido
Grade: B
The best book so far: faithful, fierce, and the famous lines land. A few small fixes remain: a duplicated phrase, a modern oath, and one word that now reads wrong.

## Issues
- `seg-0032` · form · medium
  > or the way Orestes is hunted across the stage on stage
  A duplicated phrase, left over from an edit.
  Fix: "or the way Orestes, hunted across the stage, runs from his own mother armed with torches and black snakes,"
- `seg-0039` · register · medium
  > "God in heaven — is he to go?
  This drops the god Virgil names ("pro Iuppiter!") and swaps in a modern oath. Book II ("God, the sight of him") and Book III ("I wish to god") do the same.
  Fix: "Jupiter! Is he to go?"
- `seg-0024` · register · medium
  > I wouldn't feel so utterly used and abandoned.
  "Used" now carries a sexual or therapy-talk sense. "Capta ac deserta" means deceived and deserted.
  Fix: "I would not feel so utterly deceived and deserted."
- `seg-0036` · fidelity · low
  > you've loaded me down with this for the enemy to find.
  "Obicis hosti" means "you threw me to the enemy". The phrase "for the enemy to find" is vague.
  Fix: "you loaded me with these griefs and threw me to my enemy."
- `seg-0038` · voice · low
  > ordered me to cut and run, to cut the twisted cables.
  The idiom echoes the next clause ("cut... cut"), and "cut and run" sounds flippant at this moment.
  Fix: "ordered us to hurry our flight and cut the twisted cables."
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · low
  > Cretans and Scythians and painted folk crowding and shouting
  This drops the proper noun ("picti Agathyrsi"), and the poem names them.
  Fix: "Cretans and Dryopes and the painted Agathyrsi crowding and shouting"
- `seg-0043` · read-aloud · low
  > only to be away — cruel — when you lay down here?
  "Crudelis" addresses Dido ("cruel one"), but here it floats and reads as though it describes Anna.
  Fix: "only to be away, cruel sister, when you lay down here?"

## Best lines
- "She called it marriage, and used that word to cover what she had done."
- "I have lived; I have run the course that Fortune set me, and now my ghost goes down beneath the earth, no small one."
