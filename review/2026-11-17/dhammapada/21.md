# Dhammapada · Chapter XXI. Miscellaneous Verses
Grade: B
The Gotama refrain is well done, and 302 corrects Müller on "unequals." The 295 riddle is garbled, and 302 still has Müller's "monastery."

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > and having destroyed a kingdom along with its tiger
  Verse 295 has no kingdom in it. The Pali (veyagghapañcamaṃ) is "with a tiger as the fifth": the mother, the father, two brahmin kings, and a tiger. The edition has carried over the kingdom from 294 and attached the tiger to it.
  Fix: "Having killed mother and father and two learned kings, and a tiger as the fifth, the true Brahmin goes on his way unharmed."
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · medium
  > Hard is life in the monastery, and painful is life in a house
  The Pali (durāvāsā gharā dukkhā) is about the household life only: houses are hard to live in, and it is painful. "Monastery" is Müller's error, and it makes the verse contradict its own advice.
  Fix: "Hard it is to leave the world; hard it is to delight in that life. Hard and painful is life in a household"

## Best lines
- "The good are seen from far off, like the snowy peaks of the Himalayas; the bad pass unseen, like arrows shot in the night."
- "Sitting alone, sleeping alone, walking alone, never weary, taming himself alone—he will find his joy at the edge of the forest."
