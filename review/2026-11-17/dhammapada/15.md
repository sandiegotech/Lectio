# Dhammapada · Chapter XV. Happiness
Grade: B
The "Let us live happily" refrain sings, and 202 is excellent. One phrase stumbles, and the dash in 208 is spaced.

## Issues
- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > grieves a long road
  This is not idiomatic English. The Pali dīghaddhānaṃ socati means he grieves for a long time.
  Fix: "Whoever keeps company with fools grieves for a long time"
- `seg-0012` · form · low
  > the faithful, the Noble — follow such a one
  The dash is spaced, against the house style.
  Fix: "the faithful, the Noble—follow such a one"

## Best lines
- "There is no fire like craving, no losing throw like hatred, no suffering like the burden of body and mind, no happiness higher than peace."
- "Health is the greatest gift, contentment the greatest wealth, a trusted friend the best of kin, and Nirvana the highest happiness."
