# Dhammapada · Chapter VIII. The Thousands
Grade: B
The refrains carry well, and 103 is still recognizable. "Brahman" names the wrong being, and 108 repeats itself.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · medium
  > not Mara, not even Brahman could turn his victory into defeat.
  The Pali (saha Brahmunā) names the god Brahmā. "Brahman" is Müller's spelling, but today it means the impersonal absolute of Hindu philosophy, which is a different thing.
  Fix: "not Mara, not even Brahma could turn his victory into defeat."
- `seg-0009` · voice · low
  > all of it is not worth a quarter of the reverence shown to the upright; reverence to the upright is better.
  The verse makes its point and then says it again.
  Fix: "all of it is not worth a quarter of the reverence shown to the upright."

## Best lines
- "Though a man should conquer a thousand thousand men in battle, the one who conquers himself is the greatest conqueror of all."
- "Better than a hundred years lived without ever seeing the deathless is a single day in which you see the deathless."
