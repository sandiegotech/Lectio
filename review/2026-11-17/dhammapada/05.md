# Dhammapada · Chapter V. The Fool
Grade: B
Clear and well paced, and "the round of rebirth" improves on Müller. Verse 73 reads as a command when the text is describing the fool.

## Issues
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > Let the fool crave a reputation he has not earned
  The Pali (asantaṃ bhāvanam iccheyya) describes what the fool wants. Müller's archaic "Let the fool wish" means "the fool may wish." Read today, "Let the fool crave" sounds like permission or advice.
  Fix: "The fool wants a reputation he has not earned, precedence among the monks, authority in the monasteries, and honor from people everywhere."
- `seg-0009` · voice · low
  > whose fruit he receives gladly and with a glad heart
  "Gladly ... glad" says the same thing twice. The Pali gives two words (haṭṭho sumano: delighted, happy-minded).
  Fix: "whose fruit he receives with delight and a happy heart."

## Best lines
- "An evil deed does not curdle at once, like fresh milk; it follows the fool smoldering, like a fire hidden under ashes."
- "He does not even belong to himself; how then can sons or wealth be his?"
