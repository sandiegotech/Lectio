# Dhammapada · Chapter I. The Twin Verses
Grade: B
Strong, recognizable chapter; the opening verse doubles its own first line and verse 3 loses "defeated."

## Issues
- `seg-0001`, `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > All that we are is shaped by what we have thought. The mind goes before everything; the mind makes everything; everything is made of the mind.
  Two renderings stacked: Müller's "All that we are is the result of what we have thought" and then a gloss on the Pali (manopubbaṅgamā dhammā manoseṭṭhā manomayā: mind precedes things, mind is their chief, they are mind-made). The verse says it once, and "makes everything" is not what manoseṭṭhā ("mind is chief") means.
  Fix: "Mind goes before all things; mind is their chief; they are made by mind." Keep the same wording in both verses.
- `seg-0003`, `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > He insulted me, he struck me, he beat me, he robbed me
  The Pali has four distinct wrongs: abused, struck, *defeated* (ajini), robbed (Müller: "abused, beat, defeated, robbed"). Right after "struck," "beat" reads as a second blow, so the defeat is lost.
  Fix: "He insulted me, he struck me, he got the better of me, he robbed me"
- `seg-0012` · register · low
  > they follow desires that are real
  This reads oddly in modern English, and it misses the parallel with v11. The Pali sammāsaṅkappagocarā means "ranging in right intention."
  Fix: "they follow right intention." (and in v11: "they wander after wrong intentions.")

## Best lines
- "Hatred is never ended by hatred. Hatred ends only by love—this is the ancient and unchanging law."
- "he is like a hired cowherd counting another man's cattle."
