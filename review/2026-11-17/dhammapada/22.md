# Dhammapada · Chapter XXII. The Downward Course
Grade: A
Faithful and forceful. The 316–319 sequence corrects Müller's "forbid" to "see fault," as the Pali has it.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · read-aloud · low
  > Many a man whose shoulders are wrapped in the yellow robe is undisciplined and out of control; by their own wrongdoing
  It starts with a singular ("many a man ... is") and then shifts to "their."
  Fix: "Many who wear the yellow robe around their shoulders are undisciplined and out of control; by their own wrongdoing such wrongdoers go to hell."

## Best lines
- "Like a frontier fort guarded inside and out, so guard yourself."
- "Just as a blade of kusa grass, wrongly grasped, cuts the very hand that holds it, so the monk's life, wrongly lived, drags a man down to hell."
