# Dhammapada · Chapter IX. Evil
Grade: B
Well rendered, and 127 is right where Müller's text is muddled, but verse 128 is missing from the section.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · high
  > nowhere in all the world is there a place where you can escape your own evil deed.
  The chapter has thirteen verses (116–128) and the edition stops at 127, so v128 is gone. In the Pali, v128 repeats the frame with death: there is no place where death cannot overcome you. Müller's text in this bundle prints that death verse under the number 127. Chapter X opens at 129, so the gap shows.
  Fix: segments are frozen, so append to `seg-0012`: "128. Not in the sky, not in the middle of the sea, not deep in a mountain cave—nowhere in all the world is there a place where death cannot overtake you."

- "the evil falls back on that fool, like fine dust thrown against the wind."
