# Dhammapada · Chapter XIV. The Awakened One
Grade: B
183 and the refuge sequence are right and recognizable. 190 has glosses written into it, and 182 overstates.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · medium
  > in the Dhamma—the Law—and in the Sangha—the Order—
  The glosses are set into the sentence between dashes, which breaks up the refuge formula. Choose one name for each and keep to it (v194 already says "the true Law" and "the Order").
  Fix: "The one who takes refuge in the Buddha, the Teaching, and the Order, and who sees with right wisdom the Four Noble Truths:"
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > and hardest of all, rare beyond rare, is the arising of a Buddha
  The Pali gives four parallel "hard" clauses (kiccho buddhānaṃ uppādo) without ranking them. "Hardest of all, rare beyond rare" adds emphasis the text does not have.
  Fix: "and hard is the arising of a Buddha, an Awakened One."
- `seg-0006` · register · low
  > the one who wounds another is no true seeker.
  "Seeker" now suggests a modern spiritual seeker. The Pali samaṇa means an ascetic or recluse.
  Fix: "the one who harms another is no true ascetic."
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · low
  > crossed beyond all grief and longing
  The Pali pariddava means lamentation, not longing.
  Fix: "crossed beyond all grief and lament"

## Best lines
- "Not to do any evil; to cultivate good; to purify one's own mind—this is the teaching of all the Buddhas, the Awakened Ones."
- "Happy is the arising of the Awakened; happy is the teaching of the true Law; happy is the harmony of the Order"
