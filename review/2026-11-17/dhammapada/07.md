# Dhammapada · Chapter VII. The Arhat
Grade: B
Beautifully exact in places ("threshold-pillar at the city gate," the 97 wordplay handled plainly), but 92–93 have a gloss built into the line.

## Issues
- `seg-0003`, `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > their aim is emptiness, the freedom that depends on nothing—Nirvana.
  The edition takes Müller's parenthetical "(Nirvana)" and makes it part of the sentence. The Pali names emptiness and the signless liberation (suññato animitto ca vimokkho) as the arhat's *range* (gocara), not his "aim." "Depends on nothing" is also a guess at animitta, "without sign."
  Fix: "their range is emptiness, the freedom that has no sign. Their track cannot be followed, like the path of birds across the sky." (and the same in v93, without "—Nirvana—")
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · low
  > Such a one bears all things and resents nothing, like the earth
  The Pali subbato ("of good vows," Müller "who does his duty") is dropped.
  Fix: "Such a one, true to his vows, bears all things and resents nothing, like the earth"

## Best lines
- "Like swans that rise and leave the lake, they leave one resting place after another behind them."
- "he stands firm, like the great threshold-pillar at the city gate"
