# Dhammapada · Chapter XIX. The Just
Grade: A
Clean, and the "You are not ... because" pattern carries the chapter well. One verse repeats itself and leaves a Pali word untranslated.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · voice · low
  > fairly, by what is right, who is guided by what is right and is discerning—that one is called just, established in dhamma.
  "By what is right" comes twice. Lowercase "dhamma" appears only here; everywhere else the edition says "the teaching" or "the law."
  Fix: "The one who leads others not by force but fairly and by law, who is guarded by the law and discerning—that one is called just, one who stands in the law."

## Best lines
- "You are not an elder because your head is gray. To be ripe in years and no more than that is to be old in vain."
- "But the one who is wise, who holds up the scales and chooses the good and turns away from evil—he is a sage"
