# Dhammapada · Chapter VI. The Wise
Grade: A
Faithful, plain and steady. Verses 76, 83 and 89 are more exact than Müller.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > crave a son, or wealth, or power
  The Pali raṭṭha means a kingdom or realm (Müller: "lordship"). "Power" is broader than the text.
  Fix: "crave a son, or wealth, or a kingdom"

## Best lines
- "Farmers channel the water where they want it; arrow-makers straighten the shaft; carpenters shape the wood; and the wise shape themselves."
- "Few among people ever cross to the other shore; the rest run up and down the near bank."
