# Dhammapada · Chapter XVI. What We Hold Dear
Grade: A
The 212–216 sequence follows the Pali order (dear, affection, attachment, lust, craving) better than Müller, and "so where would fear come from?" is right. Verse 218 has a gloss written into it.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · low
  > a longing for the Ineffable, Nirvana, has awakened
  The edition takes Müller's parenthetical "(Nirvana)" into the sentence. The Pali anakkhāta, "the undeclared," stands alone.
  Fix: "The one in whom a longing for the Ineffable has awakened"

## Best lines
- "From what is dear comes grief; from what is dear comes fear. To one who holds nothing dear there is no grief, so where would fear come from?"
- "his good deeds welcome him home—as kinsmen welcome a loved one come back at last."
