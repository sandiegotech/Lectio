# Dhammapada · Chapter XXV. The Monk
Grade: B
Faithful and calm, but Nirvana is set between dashes as a gloss twice, the dashes are spaced throughout, and there are traces of archaism.

## Issues
- `seg-0009`, `seg-0022` · fidelity · low
  > will reach the place of peace — Nirvana — where all that is conditioned is stilled
  The edition takes Müller's parenthetical "(Nirvana)" into the sentence between dashes. The Pali (padaṃ santaṃ saṅkhārūpasamaṃ) has only "the peaceful state."
  Fix: "will reach the place of peace, where all that is conditioned is stilled, and there find happiness."
- `seg-0007` · register · low
  > Though a monk receive but little
  The subjunctive "receive but little" is an archaism.
  Fix: "Though a monk receives little"
- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > stands close upon Nirvana.
  "Close upon" is dated (it also appears in v32).
  Fix: "is close to Nirvana."
- `seg-0003`, `seg-0007`, `seg-0008`, `seg-0009`, `seg-0019`, `seg-0022` · form · low
  > who is content alone — him they call a monk.
  The dashes are spaced, against the closed-up house style.
  Fix: close up the dashes (—).

## Best lines
- "Empty this boat, O monk! Once emptied, it will move swiftly."
- "There is no meditation for one without wisdom, and no wisdom for one who does not meditate."
