# Dhammapada · Chapter II. Heedfulness
Grade: B
Faithful and vigorous, but the key term pamāda is split between "heedlessness" and "carelessness," which blurs the chapter's one contrast.

## Issues
- `seg-0006`–`seg-0012` · voice · medium
  > give themselves over to carelessness
  v21 and v29 set up heedful/heedless, and then from v26 on the same Pali word (pamāda) becomes "carelessness." The whole chapter turns on that pair, and a reader will hear two different ideas.
  Fix: use "heedlessness" throughout (v26, 27, 28, 30, 31, 32), e.g. "Fools, the slow-witted, give themselves over to heedlessness."
- `seg-0007` · fidelity · low
  > do not give yourself to the pleasures of desire
  This is vague. The Pali kāmarati means delight in sensual pleasure (Müller: "love and lust").
  Fix: "do not give yourself to sensual delight."
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · low
  > By heedfulness Indra rose to be lord of the gods.
  The text names him Maghavā (Müller: "Maghavan (Indra)"). Swapping in the gloss drops the proper noun.
  Fix: "By heedfulness Maghavan, Indra, rose to be lord of the gods."
- `seg-0009` · voice · low
  > like a swift horse leaving a slow one in its dust
  "In its dust" is an added stock idiom. The Pali just has a swift horse passing a weak one.
  Fix: "like a swift horse that leaves a weak one behind."
- `seg-0004`, `seg-0008`, `seg-0012` · form · low
  > lives by what is right and heedful — his good name only grows.
  The dashes here are spaced ( — ), while Chapter I and most of the work close them up (—).
  Fix: close up the dashes to match the rest of the edition.

## Best lines
- "Heedfulness is the path to the deathless; heedlessness is the path to death."
- "let the wise build an island that no flood can wash away."
