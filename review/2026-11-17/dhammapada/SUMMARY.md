## Recurring patterns

1. **Müller's glosses written into the verse.** Müller's parenthetical "(Nirvana)", "(the Buddha)", "(the Law)" keep turning up as appositives inside the line: "the unchanging place, Nirvana" (225), "the place of peace — Nirvana —" (368, 381), "the Uncreated, Nirvana" (383), "the Ineffable, Nirvana" (218), "the unmade, the unconditioned, Nirvana" (154), "Dhamma—the Law—and ... Sangha—the Order—" (190), "the one who truly sees—the Buddha" (273), "arrow of craving" (275), and the parenthetical Pali transliterations in 277–279. Sections 7, 11, 14, 16, 17, 18, 20, 23, 25, 26.
2. **Müller's errors carried over where the Pali reads otherwise.** "Brahman" for the god Brahmā (105, 230). "Cravings" for *doubt* (141). "Outward acts" for "no ascetic outside this teaching" (254–255). "Monastery" in 302. "To be a good mother/father" for *honoring* them (332). The tiger and kingdom mixed up in 295. "Let the fool crave" read as a command (73). On top of this, **v128 is missing**: Chapter IX stops at 127 and Chapter X opens at 129. Two places soften instead of following Müller: "come close to Nirvana" for "reached" (134), and "hardest of all, rare beyond rare" (182).
3. **Inconsistent key terms and house style.** Pamāda is "heedlessness" in some places and "carelessness" in others, even inside Chapter II, the chapter built on it. Dhamma is "the teaching," "the true Law," "Dhamma" or "dhamma" from verse to verse. Bhikkhu is usually "monk" but sometimes "seeker." Em dashes are spaced ( — ) in sections 2, 3, 4, 11, 15 and 25 and closed up (—) everywhere else, which suggests separate drafting passes that were never brought into line.

## Verdict

Not ready to ship, but close: no section needs redoing. The register is right for readers from fifteen to ninety: plain, unhurried, free of slang and therapeutic vocabulary. The famous lines are recognizable (1–2, 5, 103, 153–154, 160, 183, 276). In many places the edition is more faithful to the Pali than Müller is (round of rebirth, stream-entry, threshold-pillar, "whom should I call my teacher?", 212–216, 316–319). One targeted pass would make it shippable. That pass needs to restore v128, fix the three high issues (128, 134, 332) and the twenty-one medium ones, which are mostly inherited Müller readings and glosses written into verses. It should also normalize the terms for pamāda, dhamma and bhikkhu, and close up the spaced dashes in the six sections that have them.
