# Dhammapada · Chapter XXIV. Craving
Grade: A
One of the strongest chapters. 353's "whom should I call my teacher?" corrects Müller, and 348 is memorable. Two terms are off.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · register · low
  > So let the seeker drive out craving
  The Pali is bhikkhu, which the edition renders "monk" everywhere else. "Seeker" also carries a modern spiritual sense.
  Fix: "So let the monk drive out craving"
- `seg-0021` · voice · low
  > The gift of the Dhamma surpasses all gifts
  This verse uses the untranslated "Dhamma," while most of the edition says "the teaching."
  Fix: "The gift of the teaching surpasses all gifts; the taste of the teaching surpasses all tastes; the delight in the teaching surpasses all delights"

## Best lines
- "Let go of the past, let go of the future, let go of the present, and cross to the far shore of existence."
- "Knowing this for myself, whom should I call my teacher?"
