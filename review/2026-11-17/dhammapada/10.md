# Dhammapada · Chapter X. The Rod
Grade: B
"The rod" is a real gain over Müller's "punishment," and the list in 137–140 reads cleanly. Verse 134 softens the claim, and 141 follows Müller's error.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · high
  > then you have come close to Nirvana
  The Pali says outright that you have *reached* Nirvana (esa patto'si nibbānaṃ, Müller "thou hast reached Nirvana"). "Come close to" weakens the claim.
  Fix: "then you have reached Nirvana, for there is no more strife in you."
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · medium
  > none of these can cleanse a person who has not gone beyond his cravings.
  The Pali is avitiṇṇakaṅkhaṃ, one who has not crossed beyond *doubt*. "Cravings" repeats Müller's "desires," which is a mistranslation.
  Fix: "none of these can cleanse a person who has not got past his doubts."
- `seg-0016` · fidelity · low
  > be eager and alive
  The Pali (ātāpino saṃvegino) means ardent and stirred to urgency. "Eager and alive" is weak and sounds like an ad.
  Fix: "be ardent, be stirred to urgency."
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > and blow will fall on blow.
  This loses the point that the retaliation lands on *you* (paṭidaṇḍā phuseyyu taṃ).
  Fix: "and blows will come back to you."

## Best lines
- "Everyone trembles at the rod; everyone holds life dear. Knowing that others are just like yourself, do not kill, and do not make others kill."
- "if you can stay silent, like a gong that is cracked and gives no sound"
