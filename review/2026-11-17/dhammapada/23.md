# Dhammapada · Chapter XXIII. The Elephant
Grade: B
A fine chapter, but 332 follows Müller into a wrong meaning, and 327 drops the work's keyword.

## Issues
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · high
  > To be a good mother in this world is a joy, and to be a good father is a joy; to live as one who has gone forth is a joy, and to live as a holy one is a joy.
  The Pali (matteyyatā, petteyyatā, sāmaññatā, brahmaññatā) praises *honoring* one's mother, father, ascetics and brahmins, not being one. Müller's "the state of a mother" leads the edition astray.
  Fix: "To honor your mother is a joy in this world, and to honor your father is a joy; to honor ascetics is a joy, and to honor the holy is a joy."
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · low
  > Take heed; guard your own mind.
  The Pali appamādaratā hotha means "delight in heedfulness." Heedfulness is the edition's keyword everywhere else, and here it is lost.
  Fix: "Delight in heedfulness; guard your own mind."
- `seg-0005` · register · low
  > his temples streaming in must
  Few readers know "must" (musth) as an elephant's rut, so they read it as the verb.
  Fix: "his temples streaming, wild in rut"
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > the untrodden country, Nirvana
  The edition takes Müller's parenthetical "(Nirvana)" into the sentence.
  Fix: "reach the untrodden country."

## Best lines
- "I will bear abuse in silence, as the elephant in battle bears the arrow shot from the bow"
- "Pull yourself out of the wrong path, as an elephant pulls itself out of the mud."
