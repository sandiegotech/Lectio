# Dhammapada · Chapter XIII. The World
Grade: B
Clean and exact, and 178's "first step into the stream" is a real gain, but a refrain lands on an epitaph.

## Issues
- `seg-0002`, `seg-0003` · register · medium
  > The virtuous rest in peace, in this world and the next.
  "Rest in peace" is the gravestone formula, and every modern reader will hear it that way. The Pali (dhammacārī sukhaṃ seti) means the one who lives by the teaching *sleeps happily* or lives at ease.
  Fix: "Those who live by the teaching sleep in peace, in this world and the next." (both verses)

## Best lines
- "Look on the world as a bubble; look on it as a mirage. The King of Death never sees the one who sees the world this way."
- "Better than sole rule over the earth, better than going to heaven, better than lordship over all the worlds, is the fruit of the first step into the stream."
