# Dhammapada · Chapter XII. Self
Grade: B
"You are your own master; who else could be?" is right, but verse 159 now echoes the Golden Rule and says something else.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · medium
  > Do for yourself what you would have others do; train yourself before you train anyone else
  The Pali (attānañce tathā kayirā yathāññamanusāsati) means: do yourself what you tell others to do. Because the phrasing echoes "do unto others," readers will hear the Golden Rule. The next clause then says the same thing again.
  Fix: "Live as you teach others to live. Well mastered yourself, you may master others; the self is the hardest of all to master."
- `seg-0008` · fidelity · low
  > like the reed that dies the year it flowers
  This explains the plant instead of giving the image. The Pali says it bears fruit to its own destruction, like the kaṭṭhaka reed.
  Fix: "like the reed whose fruiting is its own death."

## Best lines
- "You are your own master; who else could be? With yourself well mastered, you have found a master few ever find."
- "By yourself the wrong is done; by yourself you are stained."
