# Dhammapada · Chapter XX. The Path
Grade: B
The three marks (277–279) and "The Buddhas only point the way" are right and memorable, but transliterations sit in parentheses inside the verses, and the chapter has several glosses.

## Issues
- `seg-0005`, `seg-0006`, `seg-0007` · form · medium
  > "All conditioned things are impermanent" (sabbe sankhara anicca)
  Parenthetical Pali (unaccented) sits inside the verse. This is apparatus, not text, and nowhere else in the edition does this.
  Fix: remove the three parentheticals, e.g. "\"All conditioned things are impermanent\"—when you see this with wisdom..."
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > of all who have eyes, the one who truly sees—the Buddha.
  The Pali (dipadānañca cakkhumā) is "of two-footed beings, the one with eyes." "The Buddha" is a gloss added to the end of the line.
  Fix: "and of all beings on two feet, the one who has eyes to see."
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > draw out the arrow of craving.
  "Of craving" is a gloss. The Pali has just the removal of the arrow (salla).
  Fix: "once I had learned to draw out the arrow."
- `seg-0013` · read-aloud · low
  > Tend the road of peace—the Nirvana the Buddha has shown.
  "The Nirvana" with the article stumbles.
  Fix: "Tend the road to peace, to Nirvana, which the Buddha has shown."

## Best lines
- "You yourself must make the effort. The Buddhas only point the way."
- "Cut down the whole forest of desire, not just one tree—for out of that forest comes fear."
