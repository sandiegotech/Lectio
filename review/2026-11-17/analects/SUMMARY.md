## Recurring patterns

1. **Repeated sayings rendered differently.** The Analects repeats itself on purpose, and the edition keeps some repeats identical (I.11 / IV.20, VI.25 / XII.15, VIII.14 / XIV.27) but lets others drift: I.8 vs IX.24, I.3 vs XVII.17, "It is hopeless" (V.27) vs "It is finished" (XV.12), "the Fang bird" (IX.8) vs "Phoenix" (XVIII.5). One pass with a concordance of repeated chapters would fix all of them.
2. **Glosses written into the text.** Small explanations that belong in the margin: Wan "'the cultured'" (V.15, XIV.19), the content of Nan Yung's ode (XI.5), "honesty ... is a knife" (VIII.2), "when the rules run out" (IX.29). Each is correct; each is the translator talking.
3. **Occasional colloquial slips in an otherwise timeless register.** "top up the rich" (VI.3), "get to" (V.13, VII.25, XIX.3), "gone off" (X.8), "sick over" (XII.4), "the salary is in there" (II.18), "without a doubt in his head" (XII.20). Few, but each one sticks out against the plain dignity around it.

Also a form issue: Books VI–X use curly quotes and apostrophes (“ ” ’) while I–V and XI–XX use straight ones, so romanized names (Ch’i / Ch'i) differ byte for byte across the edition, which can break search and glossary matching. Normalize them in one mechanical pass.

## Verdict

This edition is close to ready to ship. Every book is faithful to Legge's text, and where it departs from Legge's interpretation it follows a recognized reading. Proper nouns are kept, and the famous lines are all recognizable and often better than in any public-domain version: "Keep the old warm and know the new", "the load is heavy and the road is long", "a man can make the Way great". No section needs to be redone, and nothing is wrong in a way that would mislead a reader on a point of substance. Before shipping, apply the 15 medium fixes. The ones that matter most are Tsze-kung's "of him" (V.4), Tsze-lu's "Fine" (XVII.3), the tyrant Chau (XVIII.1, XIX.20), and the praise of Min Tsze-ch'ien (XI.4). Then unify the repeated sayings and normalize the quotation marks. The 42 low items are optional polish.
