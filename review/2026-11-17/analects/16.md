# The Analects · Book XVI · The Chi Family
Grade: A
The long Chwan-yu dialogue is clear and forceful, and the numbered sayings stay crisp. Two small things.

## Issues
- `seg-0011` · form · low
  > the ruler calls her FU ZAN, the Lady.
  The all-caps romanizations are Legge's typography carried over; nowhere else does the edition shout names.
  Fix: set them as ordinary italics or capitalized words: "the ruler calls her Fu Zan, the Lady. She calls herself Hsiao T'ung, this little child ..."
- `seg-0004` · register · low
  > the professional pleaser
  "Professional" is a modern trade word laid over a description of manner (Legge: "the insinuatingly soft").
  Fix: "friendship with the smooth flatterer"

## Best lines
- "Ch'iu, a gentleman hates the man who will not say plainly 'I want it,' and has to dress the wanting up in reasons."
- "If you do not learn the Odes, you will have nothing to say."
