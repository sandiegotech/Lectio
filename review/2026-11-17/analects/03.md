# The Analects · Book III · Eight Rows of Dancers
Grade: A
Faithful and crisp; ritual detail kept without footnoting it into the text. Two phrasings to smooth.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · read-aloud · low
  > In festival, better spare than lavish.
  "In festival" is not idiomatic and the clause covers ritual in general (Legge: "In festive ceremonies, it is better to be sparing than extravagant").
  Fix: "In ceremony, better spare than lavish."
- `seg-0008` · register · medium
  > It is Shang who lifts my meaning.
  "Lifts my meaning" is not a sense a reader carries; the source means Shang draws out or opens up the Master's thought (Legge: "can bring out my meaning").
  Fix: "It is Shang who draws out my meaning."

## Best lines
- "The asking is the ritual."
- "Then, given rein, they run pure and clear, each voice distinct, unbroken — and so to the close."
