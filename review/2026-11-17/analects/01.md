# The Analects · Book I · Learning
Grade: A
Faithful, plain, and well voiced; two small polish points only.

## Issues
- `seg-0012` · read-aloud · low
  > To know the worth of ease and be easy, without ritual to give it shape
  "know the worth of ease and be easy" jingles and blurs the sense (Legge: knowing how ease should be prized, manifesting it without regulating it by propriety).
  Fix: "To prize ease and simply be easy, with no ritual to give it shape — that will not do either."
- `seg-0015` · fidelity · low
  > Tell him what came before, and he knows what comes next.
  The imperative reads as advice to the listener; the source reports what happened with Tsze-kung (Legge: "I told him one point, and he knew its proper sequence").
  Fix: "I tell him what came before, and he sees what comes next."

## Best lines
- "Clever words and a winning face rarely keep company with human-heartedness."
- "A gentleman without gravity carries no weight, and his learning does not hold."
