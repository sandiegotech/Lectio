# The Analects · Book V · Weighing Men
Grade: B
Lively and faithful character sketches; one line misleads about who is being discussed, and one gloss sits in the text.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > Tsze-kung asked, "And Ts'ze — what do you say of him?"
  Ts'ze is Tsze-kung's own name; "of him" makes a reader think he is asking about a third man (Legge: "What do you say of me, Ts'ze?").
  Fix: Tsze-kung asked, "And what do you say of me, of Ts'ze?"
- `seg-0015` · fidelity · low
  > given the name Wan — 'the cultured'
  A translator's gloss written into the question; the source asks only why he was called Wan.
  Fix: "Why was Kung-wan given the name Wan?"
- `seg-0011` · read-aloud · low
  > Where would he get unbending?
  Ungrammatical in a way that trips the ear.
  Fix: "How could he be unbending?"
- `seg-0013` · register · low
  > no one gets to hear
  "Gets to" is chatty for a disciple's solemn remark (Legge: "cannot be heard").
  Fix: "these we are never given to hear."

## Best lines
- "They weave brilliant cloth, and they do not know how to cut it."
- "To give the old rest. To keep faith with friends. To cherish the young."
