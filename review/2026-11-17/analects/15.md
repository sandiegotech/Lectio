# The Analects · Book XV · One Word to Walk With
Grade: A
Aphorism after aphorism, each kept short and true; the famous lines are all recognizable but one.

## Issues
- `seg-0008` · read-aloud · medium
  > There are times when they will give the body to death to bring it whole.
  A famous line made obscure: "it" has no clear antecedent and "give the body to death" is stilted (Legge: "They will even sacrifice their lives to preserve their virtue complete").
  Fix: "There are times when they will give up their lives to make human-heartedness whole."
- `seg-0012` · voice · low
  > It is finished!
  The same exclamation is "It is hopeless" at V.27; one rendering should serve both.
  Fix: "It is hopeless! I have yet to meet the man who loves virtue the way he loves beauty."

## Best lines
- "A man can make the Way great. The Way does not make the man great."
- "Plow, and hunger can lie in the middle of it; learn, and pay can lie in the middle of it."
