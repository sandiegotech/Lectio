# The Analects · Book XX · Yao Said
Grade: A
The archaic royal charges are made readable without losing their solemnity, and the five fine things are cleanly drawn. Polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > Hold honestly to the center.
  "Honestly" reads as candor; the source means hold truly, faithfully (Legge: "Sincerely hold fast the due Mean").
  Fix: "Hold faithfully to the center."
- `seg-0003` · read-aloud · low
  > Honor the five fine things, and cast out the four ugly things. Then he is fit to govern.
  The person shifts from "you" (imperative) to "he" within the answer.
  Fix: "Let him honor the five fine things and cast out the four ugly things. Then he is fit to govern."
