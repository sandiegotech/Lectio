# The Analects · Book II · Governing
Grade: A
Clean, compact, and memorable; the famous lines all land. Minor polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > it unfolded everything I had taught him
  "Everything" overstates the source, which says only that his conduct was enough to bring out the teaching (Legge: "able to illustrate my teachings").
  Fix: "and it brought out what I had taught him."
- `seg-0018` · register · low
  > the salary is in there
  "In there" is chatty against the aphoristic close of the saying.
  Fix: "— the salary lies in that."
- `seg-0020` · register · low
  > Raise up the good and teach the unable, and they will spur one another on.
  "The unable" is an awkward noun, and "one another" is added (Legge: they will "eagerly seek to be virtuous").
  Fix: "Raise up the good and teach those who fall short, and they will be spurred on."

## Best lines
- "Keep the old warm and know the new, and you are fit to teach."
- "See what a man does. Look into why he does it. Find where he comes to rest."
