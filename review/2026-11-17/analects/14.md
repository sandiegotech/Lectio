# The Analects · Book XIV · Hsien Asked
Grade: A
A long, varied book handled with steady control; the Kwan Chung debate and the recluses' jibes are vivid. Polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0045` · read-aloud · low
  > He cultivates himself into reverent care.
  "Into" is unidiomatic; the source pairs self-cultivation with reverence (Legge: "The cultivation of himself in reverential carefulness").
  Fix: "He cultivates himself with reverent care."
- `seg-0019` · fidelity · low
  > He deserved to be called Wan — 'the cultured.'
  The gloss on the name sits inside the Master's speech (same pattern as V.15).
  Fix: "He deserved to be called Wan."
- `seg-0030` · read-aloud · low
  > The Way of the gentleman runs three ways
  "Way ... three ways" is a clumsy echo (Legge: "The way of the superior man is threefold").
  Fix: "The Way of the gentleman has three parts, and I can manage none of them."

## Best lines

- "I do not rail at Heaven. I do not blame men. I learn from what is lowest and reach toward what is highest. And the one who knows me — is that not Heaven?"
