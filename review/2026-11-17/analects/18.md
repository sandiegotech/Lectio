# The Analects · Book XVIII · The Viscount of Wei
Grade: A
The recluse encounters read like small stories, unhurried and exact. Minor points only.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > "Phoenix! Phoenix! How your virtue has withered!
  The same bird is "the Fang bird" at IX.8; the edition should name it one way.
  Fix: use "phoenix" in both places ("The phoenix does not come; the river sends forth no chart.").
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > Danger, now, for men who meddle in government!
  "Meddle" adds contempt the source does not carry (Legge: "Peril awaits those who now engage in affairs of government").
  Fix: "Danger, now, for those who take part in government!"
- `seg-0003` · read-aloud · low
  > and Confucius never got his word
  Unidiomatic; trips at the close of the scene.
  Fix: "and Confucius could not speak with him."

## Best lines
- "A man cannot flock with the birds and beasts. If I am not to live among these people — among men — whom am I to live with?"
- "Nothing is ruled in for me, and nothing is ruled out."
