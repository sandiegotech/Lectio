# The Analects · Book VII · The Transmitter
Grade: A
Excellent: the self-portrait of Confucius comes through warm and exact. Polish only.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · form · low
  > “I transmit; I do not invent.
  Curly quotes and apostrophes (“ ” ’, P’ang) where most of the edition uses straight ones; see Book VI.
  Fix: normalize to the edition's majority form.
- `seg-0017` · fidelity · low
  > Of these he never stopped speaking.
  "Never stopped" overstates; the source says these were his frequent themes (Legge: "On all these he frequently discoursed").
  Fix: "These he spoke of again and again."
- `seg-0025` · register · low
  > A sage I will never get to see
  "Get to" is chatty for a line of quiet resignation (Legge: "it is not mine to see").
  Fix: "A sage I shall never see; a gentleman would be enough. A good man I shall never see; a man of constancy would be enough."
