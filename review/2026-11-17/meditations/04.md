# Meditations · Book IV
Grade: B
The great entries (the inner retreat, the river of time, the olive, the headland) land well; fix an invented refrain in IV.3, a misread line in IV.30, and a few slips of register.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · medium
  > and be at peace at last. … and be at peace at last. … and be at peace at last.
  The Greek has παύου ποτέ once, after the first answer; Long supplies the later ones only in square brackets as an editor's suggestion. Lectio prints the refrain four times as if Marcus wrote it, turning an editorial guess into the text.
  Fix: keep "and be at peace at last" after the first answer only; end the other three answers at "providence, or there are only atoms…political community.", "…about pain and pleasure.", and remove nothing else.

- `seg-0032` · fidelity · medium
  > and I get no living from my learning, yet still I hold to my reason.
  Marcus contrasts himself with the half-naked philosopher: he gets no nourishment (τροφάς) from his studies and still holds on. "Get no living" reads as income, which makes no sense from an emperor.
  Fix: And I — I get no nourishment from my studies, and still I hold to reason.

- `seg-0035` · register · medium
  > And to settle the matter, what even is an eternal memory? Nothing at all.
  "What even is" is current slang and will date fast; ὅλον κενόν is "utterly empty."
  Fix: And after all, what is everlasting remembrance? Utterly empty.

- `seg-0003` · voice · low
  > You too long for such things very much.
  Stiff and flat (calque of Long). The Greek says it is his habit.
  Fix: And you, too, are in the habit of longing for such places.

- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > Consider how many, after their mutual hostility, suspicion, hatred, and fighting, have been laid out dead
  διαδορατισθέντες is "running each other through with spears"; "fighting" loses the picture.
  Fix: how many, after hating, suspecting and spearing one another, have been laid out dead and burned to ashes

- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > my watery part from another element, and my hot, fiery part
  Drops the airy part (τὸ πνευματικὸν ἀπὸ πηγῆς τινος), the third of four elements. Long drops it too; the Greek has it.
  Fix: my watery part from another element, my airy part from some source, and my hot, fiery part

- `seg-0036` · fidelity · low
  > Willingly give yourself up to Clotho, one of the Fates,
  "One of the Fates" is a gloss written into the sentence (Long brackets it). Leave the gloss to the enrichment layer.
  Fix: Willingly give yourself up to Clotho, and let her spin your thread into whatever she pleases.

- `seg-0034` · read-aloud · low
  > farming, flattering, stubbornly arrogant, suspicious, scheming,
  The list slides from verbs into adjectives mid-run and trips aloud.
  Fix: farming, flattering, being stubbornly proud, suspicious, scheming,

## Best lines
- "Time is like a river of all that happens, a violent current; for no sooner has a thing come into view than it is swept away, and another takes its place, and that too will be swept away."
- "as an olive falls when it is ripe, blessing the nature that produced it and giving thanks to the tree on which it grew."
