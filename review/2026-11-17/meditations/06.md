# Meditations · Book VI
Grade: B
Clear and strong in the short sayings (the swarm and the bee, "not to become like the wrongdoer," the warning against becoming a Caesar); a few longer passages carry Long's syntax over and lose their logic.

## Issues
- `seg-0016` · fidelity · medium
  > Will you not stop valuing so many other things? Then you will be neither free, nor self-sufficient
  As written, "Then" follows stopping, so the reader hears that giving up these things makes you unfree, the reverse of the sense (οὐκοῦν: "if not, then…").
  Fix: Will you not stop valuing so many other things? If you do not, you will never be free, or sufficient for your own happiness, or free from passion.

- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > or simply rational in the sense of owning a number of slaves.
  Nonsense as English. The Greek ranks what people admire: a rational soul, though only a skilled one, "or simply possessing a crowd of slaves" — i.e., they admire owning many rational beings.
  Fix: though not a universal one: a soul skilled in some craft or clever in some other way, or simply the souls of the many slaves they own.

- `seg-0015` · read-aloud · medium
  > For just as it is to draw in the air once and give it back, which we do every moment, so it is with the whole power of breathing that you received at your birth, only yesterday or the day before, and give back to the element from which you first drew it.
  Long's construction carried over; it trips, and "and give back" lacks a subject.
  Fix: For as we draw in a breath and give it back every moment, so it is with the whole power of breathing that you received at birth, only yesterday or the day before: you will give it back to the element you first drew it from.

- `seg-0041` · fidelity · medium
  > and indeed we do much wrong because we draw a distinction over these things.
  διὰ τὴν πρὸς ταῦτα διαφοράν is our caring which way such things go; "draw a distinction" is opaque.
  Fix: and indeed we do much wrong because we care so much how these things turn out.

- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > Look within. Do not let the particular quality of anything, or its worth, escape you.
  Ἔσω βλέπε here means look inside things, below their surface; "Look within" will be read as introspection, which the second sentence contradicts.
  Fix: Look beneath the surface. Do not let the particular quality of anything, or its worth, escape you.

- `seg-0043` · fidelity · low
  > or Asclepius the work of the earth that bears fruit?
  The Greek names a goddess, "the Fruit-Bearer" (Demeter), set against another god; "the earth" flattens the parallel.
  Fix: or Asclepius the work of the Fruit-Bearer?

- `seg-0029` · read-aloud · low
  > It is a shame for the soul to give way first in this life, while your body does not give way.
  Person shifts from "the soul" to "your body" and the repeated "give way" is flat.
  Fix: It is a disgrace for your soul to give out first in this life while your body still holds up.

- `seg-0016` · register · low
  > Transpiration, as in plants, is nothing to be valued;
  "Transpiration" is a botany-class term most readers will not carry.
  Fix: Breathing out moisture, as plants do, is nothing to be valued;

## Best lines
- "Take care that you are not turned into a Caesar, that you are not stained with that dye; for such things happen."
- "It would be like falling in love with one of the sparrows flying past, when it has already passed out of sight."
