# Meditations · Book VIII
Grade: B
Faithful and lively (the cucumber, the spring that cannot be fouled, "teach them, or put up with them"), but the book switches to a different pronoun policy from the rest of the work, and one Greek pun is stated as if it were English.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · voice · medium
  > By holding to principles from which their feelings and actions flow. … nothing is good for a person unless it makes them just
  Book VIII consistently uses "a person … they/them" (also `seg-0022`, `seg-0036`, `seg-0049`, `seg-0055`, `seg-0058`), where Books I–VII say "a man … he" throughout and VIII itself slips back to "himself" in `seg-0056`. The edition reads as two hands. Choose one policy for the whole work and apply it everywhere; a mixed text is worse than either choice.
  Fix: (if the house style is "a man … he") By holding to principles from which his impulses and actions flow. … nothing is good for a man unless it makes him just, self-controlled, courageous, and free

- `seg-0060` · fidelity · medium
  > that is why its beams are called extensions, because they are extended.
  The wordplay is Greek (ἀκτῖνες, "rays," from ἐκτείνεσθαι, "to be extended"); in English no one calls sunbeams "extensions," so the sentence is false as written.
  Fix: that is why in Greek its rays are called aktines, from the word for "being extended."

- `seg-0003` · register · low
  > and their guiding principles were aligned with their pursuits.
  "Aligned with" is office vocabulary; the Greek says their ruling minds were their own (or matched their subject).
  Fix: and their ruling minds were their own.

- `seg-0050` · fidelity · low
  > For this reason a mind free from passions is a fortress
  ἀκρόπολις is a citadel, the inner stronghold one retreats into; "the inner citadel" is the phrase readers know, and the sentence that follows ("to flee to for refuge") depends on that image.
  Fix: For this reason a mind free from passions is a citadel

## Best lines
- "Do you wish to be praised by someone who curses himself three times an hour?"
- "People exist for the sake of one another. So either teach them, or put up with them."
