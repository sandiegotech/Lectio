# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter VII · Knowing the Supreme
Grade: B
Clean and resonant; the "I am the taste in water" litany and the pearls on a thread are exact. One proper name lost at the chapter's climax.

## Issues
- `seg-0007` · fidelity · medium
  > knowing that all this is the divine, that all this is God.
  7.19 *vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti*: "that Vasudeva is all". The name is the point of the line (Krishna naming himself as the whole), and the doubled abstraction is filler.
  Fix: knowing that Vasudeva is all that is.

## Best lines
- "There is nothing higher than I am; everything that exists is strung upon me like rows of pearls upon a single thread."
- "I am the taste in water, Arjuna; I am the light in the moon and the sun"
