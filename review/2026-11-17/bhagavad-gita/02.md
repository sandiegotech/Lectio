# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter II · The Deathless Self and the Way of Action
Grade: B
The great set pieces (worn-out clothes, weapons cannot cut it, anger to ruin) land well; a handful of glosses and lost proper terms need pulling back to the Sanskrit.

## Issues
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · medium
  > who delight in the mere letter of the scriptures
  The text names the Vedas (*vedavādaratāḥ*, 2.42; *traiguṇyaviṣayā vedāḥ*, 2.45; the well simile 2.46 is about the Vedas). "Scriptures" erases a proper noun the argument depends on. Same in "The scriptures deal with the three qualities" and "the scriptures are worth no more than a well".
  Fix: who delight in the mere letter of the Vedas … The Vedas deal with the three qualities of nature … To one who truly knows, the Vedas are worth no more than a well in a country flooded with water.
- `seg-0014` · fidelity · medium
  > for skill in action is exactly this discipline
  2.50 *yogaḥ karmasu kauśalam*, "Yoga is skill in action", is one of the most quoted lines in the work; inverting it and swapping Yoga for "discipline" makes it unrecognizable.
  Fix: so give yourself to Yoga, for Yoga is skill in action.
- `seg-0019` · fidelity · medium
  > is to pass into the peace of God
  2.72 *brahmanirvāṇam ṛcchati*: "reaches the nirvana of Brahman". "Peace of God" is a Christian phrase and drops both Sanskrit terms the chapter has just used ("the state of Brahman").
  Fix: is to pass into the stillness of Brahman, the nirvana of Brahman.
- `seg-0007` · fidelity · medium
  > — they have learned to tell the abiding substance from the passing shadow.
  A gloss smuggled into 2.16; the Sanskrit says only that the seers of truth have seen the end (the truth) of both. "Substance" and "shadow" are commentary.
  Fix: delete the dash clause; end at "have seen the truth of both."
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > it shuts you out of the noble path
  2.2 *anāryajuṣṭam asvargyam akīrtikaram*: unworthy of a noble man, not leading to heaven, bringing disgrace. "Shuts you out of heaven" is the sense; "noble path" moves the word *ārya* to the wrong slot. Arnold: "it shuts the gate of heaven".
  Fix: It is unworthy of a noble man; it closes the door to heaven and brings disgrace.
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > stand up, and be yourself again
  *uttiṣṭha parantapa* is "stand up, scorcher of foes"; "be yourself again" is added.
  Fix: Shake off this cowardly mood and stand up, scourge of your enemies.
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > Krishna, the lord of hearts
  *Hṛṣīkeśa* is "lord of the senses"; "hearts" is a different epithet.
  Fix: Krishna, the lord of the senses
- `seg-0009` · fidelity · low
  > unchanging, present everywhere, steady and sure
  2.24 ends *acalo 'yaṃ sanātanaḥ*, "immovable, eternal"; "sure" drops "eternal".
  Fix: unchanging, present everywhere, steady, immovable, eternal.
- `seg-0012` · fidelity · low
  > the wisdom of analysis, of Sankhya
  "analysis" is a gloss on the name; keep the name.
  Fix: What I have told you so far is the wisdom of Sankhya.

## Best lines
- "To one who truly knows, the scriptures are worth no more than a well in a country flooded with water." (with "the Vedas" restored)
- "From anger comes confusion; from confusion the memory fails; when memory fails, the understanding is lost; and when the understanding is lost, the person is ruined."
