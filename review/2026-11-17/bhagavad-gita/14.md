# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XIV · The Three Qualities of Nature
Grade: B
Clear exposition of the gunas, but tamas is mislabeled as "ignorance" and then said to be born of "darkness", which inverts 14.8 and makes 14.16–17 circular.

## Issues
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · high
  > goodness, passion, and ignorance … But tamas, born of darkness, deludes all who live in a body
  14.8 *tamas tv ajñānajaṃ viddhi*: tamas is born of ignorance. The edition names tamas "ignorance" and makes it born of "darkness", reversing source and product. Chapter VII already calls it "the dark".
  Fix: goodness, passion, and darkness … But tamas, born of ignorance, deludes all who live in a body
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · medium
  > the fruit of ignorance is more ignorance still.
  14.16 *ajñānaṃ tamasaḥ phalam*: "ignorance is the fruit of tamas". The "more ignorance still" tautology follows from the mislabel above.
  Fix: the fruit of darkness is ignorance.
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > being clear and untroubled, gives light and brings health
  14.6 *prakāśakam anāmayam*: "illuminating and free of ill"; "brings health" doubles "untroubled" and reads as medical.
  Fix: sattva, being clear, gives light and is free of all affliction

## Best lines
- "He who does not hate the light of goodness, nor the stir of activity, nor even the cloud of delusion when they come, and does not long for them when they are gone"
- "is set free from birth and death, from old age and sorrow, and drinks the nectar of the deathless."
