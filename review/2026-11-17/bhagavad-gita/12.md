# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XII · The Way of Devotion
Grade: A
The ladder of fallbacks and the "dear to me" refrain are clear and well weighted. A few adjectives added where the Sanskrit is bare.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > For knowledge is better than blind practice
  12.12 *śreyo hi jñānam abhyāsāt*: "knowledge is better than practice". "Blind" belittles the very practice Krishna recommended two verses earlier.
  Fix: For knowledge is better than practice
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > who is pure within and capable without, untroubled and unconcerned, who has let go of every anxious undertaking
  12.16 *śucir dakṣa … sarvārambhaparityāgī*: "pure, capable … who has given up all undertakings". "Within/without" and "anxious" are decoration.
  Fix: who is pure and capable, untroubled and unconcerned, who has let go of every undertaking

## Best lines
- "The one before whom the world does not flinch, and who does not flinch before the world"
- "I become before long the one who lifts them up out of the ocean of death and rebirth"
