# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XVII · The Threefold Faith
Grade: B
The three-by-three scheme is laid out cleanly, and "a person is made of his faith" is exact. Named beings are blurred, and one label puts the extreme ascetics in the wrong category.

## Issues
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > Know them for men whose resolve is set on what is dark.
  17.6 *tān viddhy āsuraniścayān*: "know them to be of demonic resolve". The chapter has just used "darkness" for tamas, so this files the ascetics under the wrong quality and drops the link to XVI.
  Fix: Know them for men of demonic resolve.
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · medium
  > those ruled by passion worship the spirits and demons of power
  17.4 *yakṣarakṣāṃsi*: the yakshas and rakshasas, named classes of beings. "Spirits and demons of power" is vague and invents "of power".
  Fix: those ruled by passion worship the yakshas and rakshasas
- `seg-0007` · fidelity · low
  > by which, in the beginning, the priests, the scriptures, and the sacrifices were all ordained.
  17.23 *brāhmaṇās tena vedāś ca yajñāś ca*: the Brahmins, the Vedas, and the sacrifices. Same habit as II of turning "the Vedas" into "the scriptures".
  Fix: by which, in the beginning, the Brahmins, the Vedas, and the sacrifices were all ordained.
- `seg-0005` · read-aloud · low
  > words that are true, kind, and to the good
  "To the good" is not idiomatic and the list trips. 17.15 *satyaṃ priyahitaṃ*: true, pleasing, beneficial.
  Fix: words that are true, kind, and helpful

## Best lines
- "A person is made of his faith: as his faith is, so is he."
- "But whatever is offered, given, or done without faith is called asat, \"unreal\" — it is nothing, Arjuna, neither here nor hereafter."
