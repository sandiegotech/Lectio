# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XVI · The Divine and the Demonic
Grade: A
The demonic monologue ("This much I have won today") is vivid and faithful, and the three gates of hell land cleanly. Only small polish.

## Issues
- `seg-0001` · voice · low
  > the marks of a person born to the divine endowment, the daivi nature.
  The Sanskrit tag is dropped in untranslated and in a different form from its partner ("the asura nature", seg-0002); a reader gets no help from either.
  Fix: these, Arjuna, are the marks of a person born to the divine endowment. (and in seg-0002: born to the demonic endowment.)
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > and a hard, honest discipline of the self; uprightness
  16.1 *tapa ārjavam*: austerity, uprightness. "Honest" anticipates *ārjava* and then it is said again.
  Fix: and austerity; uprightness

## Best lines
- "There are three gates to this hell, three gates by which the self is destroyed: desire, anger, and greed."
- "Bound by a hundred cords of expectation, slaves to lust and anger"
