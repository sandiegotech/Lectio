# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XVIII · Freedom Through Surrender and Letting Go
Grade: A
A strong close: the threefold tables are lucid, 18.66 is plain and recognizable, and Sanjaya's coda keeps its wonder. Only small additions.

## Issues
- `seg-0006` · fidelity · low
  > as the teaching of analysis lays them out
  18.13 *sāṅkhye kṛtānte*: "in the Sankhya doctrine". The school is named in II and III; here it is turned into a gloss.
  Fix: as the Sankhya teaching lays them out
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > that is the renunciation of light, the only true one.
  18.9 *sa tyāgaḥ sāttviko mataḥ*: "that letting go is held to be sattvic"; "the only true one" is added emphasis.
  Fix: that is the renunciation of light.
- `seg-0013` · fidelity · low
  > fixed in the discipline of devotion.
  18.33 *yogenāvyabhicāriṇyā*: "by unwavering Yoga"; "of devotion" is added.
  Fix: fixed by an unwavering discipline.
- `seg-0025` · fidelity · low
  > this highest secret, this discipline of devotion
  18.75 *guhyam etat paraṃ yogam*: "this supreme secret Yoga"; "of devotion" narrows it to bhakti.
  Fix: this highest secret, this Yoga
- `seg-0015` · register · low
  > and faith in things unseen
  18.42 *āstikyam*: religious faith, belief in the sacred teaching; "things unseen" echoes Hebrews 11:1 and colors the line Christian.
  Fix: and religious faith

## Best lines
- "Abandon all duties and come to me alone for refuge. I will set you free from every sin; do not grieve."
- "Weigh it fully in your heart, and then do as you choose."
