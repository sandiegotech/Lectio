# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XI · The Vision of the Universal Form
Grade: A
The theophany is the high point of the edition: the thousand suns, the warriors ground between the teeth, the moths into flame, and Arjuna's apology all carry their full weight.

## Issues
- `seg-0009` · register · low
  > I am Time, grown mighty, the destroyer of worlds, and I am come forth to annihilate these people.
  "I am come forth" is leftover archaism in the most famous line of the chapter; the rest of the edition uses current grammar.
  Fix: I am Time, grown mighty, the destroyer of worlds, and I have come forth to annihilate these people.

## Best lines
- "If the light of a thousand suns were to blaze out all at once in the sky, that would be something like the splendor of that mighty being."
- "As a father bears with a son, a friend with a friend, a lover with his beloved, so, O God, bear with me."
