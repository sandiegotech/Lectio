# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter IV · The Way of Knowledge and Sacrifice
Grade: B
The avatar passage and the sacrifice litany read well and keep "dharma" and "Brahman" intact. One loosened line on the wise man of action, plus small glosses.

## Issues
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · medium
  > he is whole, and everything he does is done in the right spirit.
  4.18 *sa yuktaḥ kṛtsnakarmakṛt*: "he is a yogi (disciplined), and he performs all action". "Whole" and "in the right spirit" are invented; the point is that he acts fully while seeing inaction.
  Fix: he is disciplined, even while he does every kind of work.
- `seg-0001` · fidelity · low
  > This undying discipline, this deep union, I once taught to Vivasvat, the lord of light
  *imaṃ … yogam avyayam* is one phrase; "this deep union" is a doubled rendering, and "the lord of light" is a gloss.
  Fix: This undying Yoga I once taught to Vivasvat, the sun;
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · low
  > Age after age I come into the world, in visible form,
  *sambhavāmi yuge yuge*: "I come into being age after age"; "in visible form" is added.
  Fix: Age after age I come into being
- `seg-0004` · fidelity · low
  > for the workings of action run deep and dark.
  *gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ*, "the way of action is deep"; "and dark" is added for rhythm.
  Fix: for the way of action is hard to fathom.

## Best lines
- "Even if you were the worst sinner of all sinners, the boat of knowledge would carry you safely across every wrong you have done."
- "As a kindled fire turns its fuel to ashes, Arjuna, so the fire of knowledge turns all action to ashes."
