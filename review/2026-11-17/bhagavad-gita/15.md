# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XV · The Supreme Person
Grade: A
The inverted tree, the axe of non-attachment and the wind carrying scents are all exact and well cadenced; the Purushottama passage is clear.

## Best lines
- "This ashvattha, with its roots grown so deep, must be cut down by the strong axe of non-attachment."
- "Neither the sun lights it, nor the moon, nor fire; once they have gone there, they do not return. That is my supreme abode."
