# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter X · The Divine Glories
Grade: A
The long catalogue keeps every name and moves briskly; only two small slips in the glosses on divine classes.

## Issues
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > Among the storm-spirits I am Shankara
  10.23 *rudrāṇāṃ śaṅkaraḥ*: "among the Rudras". The edition has just used "storm-gods" for the Maruts, so two different groups now read as the same one. Chapter XI names the Rudras outright.
  Fix: Among the Rudras I am Shankara
- `seg-0005` · fidelity · low
  > Of the chants of the Veda I am the great hymn
  10.35 *bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnām*: the Brihat Saman, a named chant; "the great hymn" loses the name.
  Fix: Of the Sama chants I am the Brihat Saman

## Best lines
- "I am the gambling of the cheat, and I am the splendor of the splendid"
- "I stand holding up this whole universe with a single fragment of myself."
