# Bhagavad Gita · Chapter XIII · The Field and the Knower of the Field
Grade: B
The list of what knowledge is and the "hands and feet everywhere" passage read beautifully. One wrong element in the closing simile and a muddled rendering in the list of the field.

## Issues
- `seg-0010` · fidelity · medium
  > As the all-pervading air, for its very subtlety, is never tainted by what it moves through
  13.32 *yathā sarvagataṃ saukṣmyād ākāśaṃ nopalipyate*: *ākāśa* is space (ether), not air (*vāyu*). The simile depends on space containing everything without being touched; air does not work. The edition itself renders *ākāśa* as "ether" (VII) and "space" (IX).
  Fix: As all-pervading space, for its very subtlety, is never tainted by anything it holds
- `seg-0003` · fidelity · medium
  > the body as a whole, the power that holds awareness together, and the will to keep on being
  13.6 *saṅghātaś cetanā dhṛtiḥ*: the aggregate (body), consciousness, steadfastness. The two paraphrases are hard to parse and say something the text does not ("holds awareness together", "keep on being").
  Fix: the body as a whole, awareness, and steadfastness
- `seg-0002` · fidelity · low
  > and the one who knows it — who watches over it — is called the knower of the field.
  13.1 *etad yo vetti*: "he who knows it". "Who watches over it" is a gloss.
  Fix: and the one who knows it is called the knower of the field.
- `seg-0005` · voice · low
  > without the three strands of nature, and yet it experiences them.
  The *guṇas* are "qualities" in VII, XIV and the chapter titles, "strands" here and through the rest of XIII. One term, two names, a reader may take for two things.
  Fix: free of the three qualities of nature, and yet it experiences them. (and likewise "strands" → "qualities" in seg-0007, seg-0008, seg-0010)

## Best lines
- "The one who truly sees is the one who sees the supreme Lord dwelling alike in all beings, undying within the dying."
- "As the one sun lights up this whole world, so the knower of the field lights up the whole field."
