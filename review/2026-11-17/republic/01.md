# The Republic · Book I · What Is Justice?
Grade: B
A close, lively rendering that follows the Greek more faithfully than Jowett; one ambiguous clause and a recurring "God" for Zeus are the only real fixes.

## Issues
- `seg-0032` · fidelity · medium
  > Wealth does a great deal to keep a man from cheating or lying, even against his will
  "Even against his will" attaches to the wrong thing; the Greek (μηδὲ ἄκοντα) means not cheating anyone *even unintentionally*, and Jowett has "either intentionally or unintentionally". As written it reads as if wealth restrains the man against his will.
  Fix: Wealth does a great deal to keep a man from cheating or lying to anyone, even unintentionally,

- `seg-0054`, `seg-0119`, `seg-0288`, `seg-0289`, `seg-0406` · register · low
  > in god's name … God, no … God forbid … By God, I know it … Nothing, by God
  The Greek oaths here are by Zeus (πρὸς Διός, μὰ τὸν Δία), and elsewhere the edition keeps them ("by Zeus", `seg-0229`; "By Heracles", `seg-0186`). A monotheistic "God" in a pagan mouth is an anachronism, and lowercase "in god's name" looks like a typo.
  Fix: `seg-0054` "Then for heaven's sake, I said"; `seg-0119` "No, by Zeus, he said"; `seg-0289` "Think it? By Zeus, I know it."; `seg-0406` "Nothing at all, I said"; `seg-0288` "Heaven forbid, I said".

- `seg-0184` · fidelity · low
  > As it was, I had glanced at him just as the argument began to drive him wild
  Drops "first" (πρότερος), which the joke depends on: the folk belief that a wolf who sees you first strikes you dumb. The previous sentence sets it up; this one must pay it off.
  Fix: As it was, I had looked at him first, just as the argument began to drive him wild,

- `seg-0281` · fidelity · low
  > you can't even tell her sheep from her shepherd
  The sheep and the shepherd are not the nurse's; "for her" means "thanks to her neglect". The doubled "her" muddies the insult.
  Fix: thanks to her you can't even tell sheep from shepherd.

- `seg-0262` · read-aloud · low
  > An art, so long as it is strictly and wholly what it is, remains unblemished and pure — being correct.
  The dangling "being correct" trips the ear after a long build.
  Fix: An art that is truly and wholly an art is correct, and so stays unblemished and pure.

- `seg-0329` · read-aloud · low
  > How sweet you are, he said. Likely, too — when I am telling you that injustice pays and justice doesn't.
  "Likely, too" is a fragment that loses its sarcasm aloud.
  Fix: Oh, very likely, my sweet man — when I'm telling you that injustice pays and justice doesn't.

- `seg-0124` · form · low
  > the good are their enemies and the bad their friends? True.
  Polemarchus's answer "True." sits inside Socrates's question (inherited from the source split), so the speaker change is lost.
  Fix: end the segment at the question mark; give "True." its own line when segmentation is next revised (not by reordering existing ids).
