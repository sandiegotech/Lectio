# Republic · Book VII · The Cave
Grade: B
Faithful and exact, with the cave and the curriculum intact; but it is written in a stiffer, uncontracted voice than the books around it, and a few lines trip.

## Issues
- section-wide · voice · medium
  > Do not suppose that anything I have said applies to men any more than to women (`seg-0365`)
  Book VII almost never contracts (about 4 contractions per 1,000 words, against 14 to 17 in Books VI and VIII), so Socrates and Glaucon speak in "I do not", "it is not", "we will" here and in "don't", "isn't", "we'll" on either side. The change is audible on reading straight through, and in quick exchanges it reads as formal (`seg-0010` "They would have to.", `seg-0148` "I cannot tell", `seg-0344` "There is not, he said.").
  Fix: one light pass bringing contractions in dialogue to the Book VI norm, leaving the long expository sentences (the cave, the line recap) as they are.
- `seg-0039` · read-aloud · low
  > Then come agree with me on this too, I said
  "Come agree" trips.
  Fix: "Then agree with me on this too, I said, and don't be surprised".
- `seg-0077` · fidelity · low
  > This, it seems, is not a matter of flipping a shell, as in the children's game
  "as in the children's game" explains the allusion inside the sentence (Jowett's long note, shortened into the text).
  Fix: "This, it seems, is not the flip of a shell; it is the turning around of a soul". The game belongs in a note.
- `seg-0275` · fidelity · low
  > as irrational as the incommensurable lines the geometers talk about.
  "the geometers talk about" is an added explanation; Plato only says "irrational, like lines".
  Fix: "while they were as irrational as incommensurable lines."
- `seg-0272` · read-aloud · low
  > How could I not deny it? he said.
  The double negative makes the listener stop to work out that he is agreeing.
  Fix: "How could I say he had? he said."
- `seg-0353` · register · low
  > they crash — fast and hard — into believing nothing they believed before.
  You crash into a thing, not into believing something; the image doesn't hold up.
  Fix: "they fall, fast and hard, into believing nothing they believed before."

## Best lines
- `seg-0063` "And so the city — ours and yours — will be governed awake, not in a dream"
- `seg-0273` "He dreams and dozes through his present life, and before he ever wakes up here, he arrives in Hades and falls asleep for good."
