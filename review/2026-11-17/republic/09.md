# Republic · Book IX · The Tyrant’s Soul
Grade: B
Faithful and often fine (the dream-beast, the cattle at the trough, the city laid up in heaven); a few modern words, one smuggled note, and oaths handled differently from the other books.

## Issues
- `seg-0055` · register · medium
  > for the sake of a girlfriend of a week
  "Girlfriend" is modern and softens the Greek (a newly acquired courtesan/mistress, hetaira) into a dating word.
  Fix: "for the sake of a mistress he took up last week".
- `seg-0055` · fidelity · medium
  > But in God’s name, Adeimantus
  The oath is "by Zeus" (pros Dios); everywhere else this edition keeps Zeus, and the monotheistic "God" is out of place in Socrates' mouth.
  Fix: "But by Zeus, Adeimantus".
- `seg-0344` · fidelity · medium
  > (729 is close to the number of days and nights in a year.)
  An editor's note (Jowett's) carried into Socrates' speech.
  Fix: delete the parenthesis; put the note in enrichment for `seg-0344`.
- section-wide · voice · low
  > Honestly, he said, I don’t see how such a man could think anything else. (`seg-0272`)
  Book IX never says "by Zeus" (Books VI to X otherwise keep it, about 3 times a book); where the Greek swears (here Ma Dia) it puts "Honestly" or nothing (`seg-0056`, `seg-0194`), which is a modern conversational tic.
  Fix: put the oaths back where the Greek has them: "By Zeus, he said, I don’t see how such a man could think anything else."
- section-wide · form · low
  > I don’t think we have adequately settled what they are (`seg-0005`)
  Book IX alone uses typographic apostrophes (’) throughout; Books VI, VII, and X use straight ones ('), and Book VIII mixes the two. This won't show in reading but will in search, compare, and print.
  Fix: normalize the apostrophe and quote characters across the edition in the pipeline, without touching the wording.
- `seg-0009` · register · low
  > goes looking for a way to satisfy its own instincts
  "Instincts" is a modern psychological term; Plato says its own ways/nature (ēthē).
  Fix: "goes looking for a way to satisfy its own nature".
- `seg-0187` · register · low
  > unless some of it converts to cash
  Banking idiom, slightly glib for the money-lover's own claim.
  Fix: "unless they bring in money".
