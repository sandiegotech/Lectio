# The Republic · Book V · The Three Waves
Grade: A
The best-sustained book so far. The three waves, the philosopher-king sentence and the knowledge/opinion argument are exact, unsoftened where Plato is hard, and read aloud cleanly. The few flags are cross-book term drift and polish.

## Issues
- `seg-0036`, `seg-0044`, `seg-0096`, `seg-0108`, `seg-0132` · voice · low
  > The men were given music and gymnastics.
  This book says "gymnastics", as Book II did, but Books III and IV call the same subject "physical training". It is a back-reference ("the men were given…"), so the reader should meet the same word Book III used.
  Fix: use the single term chosen for the edition (see the Book III and IV reports).

- `seg-0172` · register · low
  > Ye gods, my dear friend, I said
  Leftover archaism for Βαβαί, the only "ye" in the book.
  Fix: Good heavens, my dear friend, I said

- `seg-0302` · register · low
  > with hands-on experience and a full view of what their work will be?
  "Hands-on experience" is job-listing language.
  Fix: by letting them practice and watch what their work will be?

- `seg-0447`, `seg-0498` · register · low
  > the oddest people to file among philosophers … or shall we file it under some other kind?
  "File" (also in Book I `seg-0338` and Book II `seg-0011`) is office vocabulary, and it recurs often enough to become a tic.
  Fix: `seg-0447` "the oddest people to count among philosophers"; `seg-0498` "or shall we put it in some other class?"
